We use your email for receipts, statements and important notices about your account.

## Change your email

To change it:

1. Go to **Profile** > **Personal details** > **Email**.
2. Enter your new email address and tap **Save**.
3. Open the confirmation email we send and tap the link.

The link is valid for {{account.email_link_expiry.ALL}}. Until you confirm, we keep using your old address.

## Didn't get the confirmation email?

Check your spam or junk folder, and that the address is spelled correctly. You can resend the link from the same screen. Some company email systems block automated emails, so a personal address usually works better.

Your email isn't used to log in, so changing it doesn't affect your password or security codes.
