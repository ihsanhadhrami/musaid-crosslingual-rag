A one-time password (OTP) is a 6-digit code we send by SMS to your registered phone number. We ask for it when you log in on a new device, add a recipient, send money abroad, or change your security settings.

## How long is a code valid?

Each code works once and expires after {{security.otp_expiry.ALL}}. If it expires, request a new one. Only the latest code works, so older codes stop working as soon as you ask for a new one.

## I didn't get the code

Wait {{security.otp_resend_wait.ALL}} and tap **Resend code**. If it still doesn't arrive, see [Not receiving OTP codes](kb-0153).

## Will Sahla Pay ever ask me for my code?

No. Nobody from Sahla Pay will ever call, message or email you asking for an OTP, your PIN or your password. If someone asks, it's a scam, even if the caller knows your name or recent transactions. Hang up and report it from **Help** > **Report fraud**.

## Can I get codes by email instead?

Not at the moment. Codes are only sent by SMS to the phone number on your account. If you've changed your number, update it first; see [Changing your registered phone number](kb-0013).
