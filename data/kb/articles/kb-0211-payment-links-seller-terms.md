These terms apply when you create a payment link to receive money for something you sell. They add to the Terms of Use. If you don't agree to them, don't create payment links.

## 1. What payment links are for

Payment links let you receive occasional payments for goods or services you sell personally, such as second-hand items, home-made food or private lessons. Anyone with a debit card can pay a link, whether or not they use Sahla Pay. Payment links are not a merchant service. You must not use them as the main way of collecting payments for a registered business, a shop, or anyone other than yourself.

## 2. Creating a link

Each link has a fixed amount and description that you set. Links expire after {{payment_link.expiry.ALL}} if they are not paid. The maximum amount per link is {{payment_link.max_amount.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{payment_link.max_amount.AE}} in the UAE and {{payment_link.max_amount.EG}} in Egypt. You must describe what you are selling accurately and not use links for anything prohibited by our Acceptable Use Policy.

## 3. Fees

When a link is paid, we deduct a seller fee from the payment before crediting your balance: {{fees.payment_link.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{fees.payment_link.AE}} in the UAE and {{fees.payment_link.EG}} in Egypt. The buyer pays only the amount on the link. Seller fees are not refunded if the payment is later refunded or reversed.

## 4. Your obligations as a seller

You must deliver what you described, on time, and handle questions and complaints from buyers yourself. You are responsible for any taxes or permits that apply to what you sell. If a buyer asks for a refund, you can refund them from the payment in **Activity**.

## 5. Buyer disputes and reversals

Buyers pay by card, so they can dispute the payment with their bank under card network rules, for example if they say the item never arrived. If a buyer disputes a payment, we will deduct the disputed amount from your balance while the dispute is open, and ask you for evidence of delivery. If the dispute is decided in the buyer's favour, the amount is not returned to you. If your balance is not enough to cover a disputed amount, you must repay the difference, and we may restrict your account until you do.

## 6. Limits on use

The money you receive through payment links counts towards your balance limit and may trigger a source of funds review. If you receive a large number of link payments, or payments that look like business activity, we may ask about your activity, limit or stop your use of payment links, or ask you to stop using your personal account for those sales.

## 7. Fraud and prohibited sales

If we believe a link is being used to sell something that doesn't exist, or to collect money by deception, we will block the link, hold the funds received through it, and may close your account. Funds held for this reason are released only to the buyers, or as required by the authorities.

## 8. Ending your use of payment links

You can stop using payment links at any time. We may stop you from using them, with notice, or immediately in the situations described in section 7. Links that have already been paid remain subject to these terms, including buyer disputes, for as long as a dispute can be raised.
