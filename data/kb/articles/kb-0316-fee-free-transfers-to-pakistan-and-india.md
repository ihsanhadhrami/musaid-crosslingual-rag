**This offer ended on 16 August 2025.**

## The offer

From 10 to 16 August 2025, international transfers from Saudi Arabia and the UAE to bank accounts and mobile wallets in Pakistan and India had no transfer fee.

## Terms

- Up to {{promo.remit_week_max_free.ALL}} fee-free transfers per customer during the week. Later transfers had the normal fee.
- Our exchange rate markup still applied: {{fees.fx_markup.SA}} from Saudi Arabia and {{fees.fx_markup.AE}} from the UAE.
- The recipient's bank could still charge its own fee.
- Transfers scheduled before the offer started, and recalled transfers, weren't fee-free.
- If a transfer was returned by the receiving bank, the fee-free transfer still counted towards the maximum.

Normal fees for these destinations are in [Sending money to India](kb-0217) and [Sending money to Pakistan](kb-0218).
