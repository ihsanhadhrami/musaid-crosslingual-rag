There's a time limit for raising a problem with a transaction. Deadlines are counted in calendar days from the date of the transaction, not the date you noticed it.

| | Saudi Arabia | UAE | Egypt |
|---|---|---|---|
| Time to open a dispute | {{dispute.filing_window.SA}} | {{dispute.filing_window.AE}} | {{dispute.filing_window.EG}} |
| Our investigation | up to {{dispute.investigation.SA}} | up to {{dispute.investigation.AE}} | up to {{dispute.investigation.EG}} |

These deadlines apply to transfers and card payments alike.

Card disputes that go to the card network (a chargeback) can take longer than our own investigation, up to {{chargeback.max_resolution.ALL}} in total, because the merchant's bank has time to respond. We'll tell you if your case becomes a chargeback. See [Chargebacks explained](kb-0079).

Check your **Activity** regularly. Once the deadline for your country has passed, we can't open a dispute, even if the transaction was a mistake.
