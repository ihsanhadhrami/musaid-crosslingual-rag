There's a maximum amount you can keep in your Sahla Pay balance. It depends on whether you're verified and where your account is registered.

| | Saudi Arabia | UAE | Egypt |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unverified account | {{limits.balance_max_unverified.SA}} | {{limits.balance_max_unverified.AE}} | {{limits.balance_max_unverified.EG}} |
| Verified account | {{limits.balance_max_verified.SA}} | {{limits.balance_max_verified.AE}} | {{limits.balance_max_verified.EG}} |

The limit includes everything in your account, including Pockets.

## What happens if I reach it?

Top-ups and incoming transfers that would take you over the limit are declined in full and returned to the sender. Card payments and outgoing transfers still work.

## Can I raise it?

Verifying your account raises it to the verified level. Above that, the balance limit can't be increased, even with a temporary limit increase; that only affects how much you can send per day. See [Can I increase my limits?](kb-0069).

Refunds are the one exception: a refund is credited even if it takes your balance over the limit. You won't be able to add more money until you're back under it.
