Streaming services, apps, gyms, phone plans: subscriptions are easy to start and easy to forget.

## Find them

Go to **Cards** > **Recurring payments**. We list every merchant that has charged your card on a repeating basis, with the date and amount of the last payment.

Some subscriptions are paid through the App Store or Google Play rather than directly. These appear under Apple or Google, not the service's own name. Check your subscriptions in your phone's settings as well.

## Cancel the ones you don't use

Cancel with the merchant, in their app or website. That's the only way to end the agreement.

## Block a merchant

If a merchant keeps charging after you cancelled, tap the merchant in **Recurring payments** and choose **Block**. This stops them charging your card, but doesn't cancel your agreement with them. If you owe them money, they can still ask you to pay another way.

## Free trials

Many trials turn into paid subscriptions automatically. When you start a trial, set a reminder for a few days before it ends.

## Charges you don't recognize

If you see a subscription you never signed up for, see [Subscription charges I don't recognize](kb-0081).

## When your card is replaced

Many merchants get your new card details automatically through the card network. Others don't, and the subscription stops when the old card is cancelled. Check **Recurring payments** after a replacement.
