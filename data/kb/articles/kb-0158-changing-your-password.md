## Change your password

To change it while logged in:

1. Go to **Profile** > **Security** > **Change password**.
2. Enter your current password.
3. Enter a new password, at least {{security.password_min_length.ALL}} characters long, twice.
4. Confirm with the code we send to your phone.

## Forgot your password?

On the login screen, tap **Forgot password**. Enter the code we send to your phone, take a quick selfie, and choose a new password.

## Password rules

Your password must include letters and numbers, and can't be the same as your last three passwords. Avoid your name, phone number or date of birth.

## What happens after I change it?

You stay logged in on the device you used. All other devices are logged out and will need the new password.

Changing your password also removes all your trusted devices except the one you're using. The next time you log in on another device, you'll need a code and a selfie again.
