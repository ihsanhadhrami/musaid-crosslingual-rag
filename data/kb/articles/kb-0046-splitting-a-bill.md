Paid for the whole group? Split the cost and send everyone a request in one go.

## Split a payment

To split a bill:

1. In **Activity**, tap the card payment you want to split.
2. Tap **Split this payment**.
3. Choose who to split with, up to {{transfer.split_max_people.ALL}} people including you.
4. Split it equally, or edit each person's share.
5. Tap **Send requests**.

Each person gets a normal payment request and can pay their share with one tap. You can see who has paid on the original payment.

## Can I split something I paid for with cash?

Yes. Tap **Request** > **Split an amount** and enter the total yourself.

Splitting only works with people who use Sahla Pay in the same country as you. Anyone else you add is left out of the split, and the app asks you to share a payment link with them instead.
