Ten minutes once a month helps you spot problems early, from a forgotten subscription to a payment you didn't make.

## 1. Look through Activity

Use the filters in **Activity** to show card payments only. Look for anything you don't recognize. Merchant names can be different from shop names, so search for the name online before you assume it's fraud.

If you still don't recognize a payment, freeze your card and report it. See [Transactions I didn't make](kb-0090).

## 2. Check your recurring payments

Go to **Cards** > **Recurring payments**. Is there anything you no longer use? Cancel it with the merchant, and block it if they keep charging. See [Recurring card payments](kb-0127).

## 3. Review your Pockets

Are your savings on track? If a goal has changed, update the target or move the money.

## 4. Check pending refunds

If you're waiting for a refund, check how long it's been since the merchant issued it. If it's late, see [When will I get my money back?](kb-0072).

## 5. Check your security

- In **Profile** > **Security** > **Trusted devices**, remove any phone you no longer use.
- Check that your ID hasn't expired, or won't expire soon. See [Updating an expired ID or Iqama](kb-0011).

## 6. Plan next month

Look at the big payments coming up, like rent, school fees or a trip, and make sure the money will be there.
