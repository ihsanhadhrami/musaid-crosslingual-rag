A transfer marked **Pending** has left your available balance but hasn't been sent yet.

## Why is my transfer still pending?

Most pending transfers complete within seconds. A transfer can stay pending for longer if:

- the recipient's bank isn't responding, which is common during maintenance windows
- it was sent outside business hours to a bank that doesn't process instantly
- it has been picked for a security check (it will then show **Under review**)

## Can I cancel it?

Yes, within {{transfer.cancel_window.ALL}} of sending, as long as it's still pending. Open it in **Activity** and tap **Cancel transfer**.

## Does a pending transfer count towards my limit?

Yes. If it fails or you cancel it, the amount is added back to your limit straight away.

## It's been pending for hours

Contact us through **Help** > **Chat with us** with the reference number from the transfer. For the full list of statuses, see [Transfer statuses explained](kb-0052).
