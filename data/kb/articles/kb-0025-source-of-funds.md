Like all regulated payment companies, we have to understand where large amounts of money come from. If your account receives a lot more than usual, we'll ask you a few questions.

## When do you ask?

Usually when the money you receive in one calendar month goes above {{kyc.source_of_funds_threshold.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{kyc.source_of_funds_threshold.AE}} in the UAE or {{kyc.source_of_funds_threshold.EG}} in Egypt. We may also ask below these amounts if the activity looks unusual for your account.

## What will you ask for?

A short form in the app: what you do for work, where the money came from, and who sends it. Sometimes we ask for a document, such as:

- a salary certificate or recent payslip
- a sale contract, for example for a car
- a bank statement showing the money before it came to Sahla Pay

## What happens to my account in the meantime?

Your account keeps working while you answer. Once you've submitted everything, our team reviews it within a few business days.

If you don't reply by the date in our message, outgoing transfers are paused until you do. Money that arrives during a review isn't returned to the sender automatically. It stays in your balance, but you can't send it on until the review is closed.
