October is security awareness month. Most of the fraud we see doesn't involve hacking. It involves someone being persuaded to share a code or send money. These habits stop most of it.

## 1. Never share a code

No one from Sahla Pay, your bank or the government will ask for a one-time password. Not by phone, not by chat, not by SMS.

## 2. Hang up and call back

If someone calls saying they're from Sahla Pay, hang up and call the number in **Help** > **Call us**.

## 3. Slow down

Scammers create urgency: your account will be blocked, a relative is in trouble, an offer ends today. Real problems can wait the few minutes it takes to check.

## 4. Turn on notifications

You'll see every payment as it happens.

## 5. Use the extra protection

**Code at every login** and **Passcode for transfers** add a second check before money can move. See [Two-step verification](kb-0169).

Already shared something? Freeze your card and call the fraud line straight away. See [Reporting fraud](kb-0156).
