This article describes the transfer allowances that applied to accounts registered in Saudi Arabia during 2025. They covered transfers to Sahla Pay users, Saudi bank accounts, and international recipients.

| Limit | Verified account | Unverified account |
|---|---|---|
| Per day | {{limits.daily_transfer_verified.SA@2025-01-01}} | {{limits.daily_transfer_unverified.SA}} |
| Per month | {{limits.monthly_transfer_verified.SA}} | No separate limit |

The daily allowance resets at midnight Riyadh time, while the monthly allowance resets on the first day of each month. Bill payments and card purchases were outside these limits.

Account holders could request the verified allowance by completing identity verification through **Profile** > **Verify identity**. Personal accounts could not receive limits above the verified level.
