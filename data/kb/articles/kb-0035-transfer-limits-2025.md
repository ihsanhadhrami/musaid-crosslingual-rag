These are the transfer limits for accounts registered in Saudi Arabia. They cover transfers to other Sahla Pay users, to local banks, and international transfers.

| Limit | Verified account | Unverified account |
|---|---|---|
| Per day | {{limits.daily_transfer_verified.SA@2025-01-01}} | {{limits.daily_transfer_unverified.SA}} |
| Per month | {{limits.monthly_transfer_verified.SA}} | No separate limit |

The daily limit resets at midnight Riyadh time. The monthly limit resets on the 1st.

Bill payments and card purchases are not included in these limits.

To raise your limits, complete verification in **Profile** > **Verify identity**. Limits above the verified level aren't available for personal accounts.
