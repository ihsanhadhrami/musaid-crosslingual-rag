You can pay rent from your Sahla Pay balance by bank transfer to your landlord, and set it up to repeat every month. Here's what to know in each country.

## Paying your landlord

To pay rent by bank transfer:

1. Ask your landlord or agent for the IBAN and the name on their bank account.
2. Tap **Send** > **To a bank account** and enter the details.
3. In the reference, write the property and the month, for example "Flat 12, March rent".
4. Tap **Send later** and choose **Monthly**, if you want it to repeat. See [Scheduled and recurring transfers](kb-0051).

Keep the receipts from **Activity**. They're your proof of payment if there's ever a disagreement.

## Saudi Arabia

Many rental contracts are registered on the national rental platform, which issues payment bills for each instalment. If your contract has bills, pay them through SADAD; the payment is linked to your contract automatically. Bank transfers to the landlord are free in Saudi Arabia.

## UAE

Many landlords in the UAE ask for post-dated cheques. Sahla Pay doesn't issue cheques, so you'll need to agree another method with your landlord, such as monthly bank transfers. Each local bank transfer costs {{fees.local_transfer.AE}} plus VAT and arrives in {{transfer.local_bank_delivery.AE}}. Your landlord may ask for your tenancy contract (Ejari) before agreeing.

## Egypt

Bank transfers to your landlord cost {{fees.local_transfer.EG}} of the amount, at least {{fees.local_transfer_min.EG}} and at most {{fees.local_transfer_max.EG}}, plus VAT. If your landlord has an InstaPay address, you can send to it directly; see [Sending to InstaPay addresses](kb-0059).

## Paying a whole year's rent at once

A full year's rent can be more than your daily transfer limit. In Saudi Arabia and the UAE, verified customers can ask for a temporary increase, up to {{limits.temp_increase_daily.SA}} in Saudi Arabia and {{limits.temp_increase_daily.AE}} in the UAE, by uploading the tenancy contract. See [Requesting a temporary limit increase](kb-0150). Temporary increases are {{limits.temp_increase_available.EG}} in Egypt, so split a large payment over several days.

A temporary increase raises your daily limit only. Your monthly limit stays at {{limits.monthly_transfer_verified.SA}} in Saudi Arabia and {{limits.monthly_transfer_verified.AE}} in the UAE, so an annual rent payment above your monthly limit has to be split across two calendar months.
