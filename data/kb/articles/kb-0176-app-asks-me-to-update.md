## Why do I have to update?

Updates include security fixes and changes needed by our partners. When an older version is no longer safe to use, we make the update required.

## Can I skip it?

Optional updates can be skipped. Required updates can't: the app won't let you continue until you update.

## The update won't install

- Check you have enough free storage.
- Connect to Wi-Fi.
- Make sure your phone meets the requirements: {{app.min_ios.ALL}} or {{app.min_android.ALL}} or later.

## Will I lose anything?

No. Updating keeps you logged in and doesn't change your balance, cards or history.

If your phone can't install the required update because its operating system is too old, the app stops working on that phone. Your card and account keep working, but you'll need a supported phone to use the app again.
