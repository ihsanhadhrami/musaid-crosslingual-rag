Quick answers about your Sahla Pay card.

## Is it a credit card?

No. It's a prepaid card that spends from your Sahla Pay balance. You can't spend more than you have.

## How much is a physical card?

{{fees.physical_card_issuance.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{fees.physical_card_issuance.AE}} in the UAE and {{fees.physical_card_issuance.EG}} in Egypt, one-time.

## How long does delivery take?

Up to {{card.delivery.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{card.delivery.AE}} in the UAE and {{card.delivery.EG}} in Egypt.

## I lost my card. What do I do?

Freeze it in **Cards** straight away. If you can't find it, report it lost; a replacement costs {{fees.card_replacement.SA}}, {{fees.card_replacement.AE}} or {{fees.card_replacement.EG}} depending on your country.

## How many wrong PIN attempts are allowed?

{{card.pin_attempts.ALL}}. After that the PIN is blocked, and you can unblock it in the app.

## Can I use my card abroad?

Yes, wherever the card network logo is shown. Our exchange rate markup applies.

## Can I use Apple Pay?

In Saudi Arabia and the UAE, yes. In Egypt, Apple Pay is {{wallet.apple_pay.EG}}, but Google Pay works.
