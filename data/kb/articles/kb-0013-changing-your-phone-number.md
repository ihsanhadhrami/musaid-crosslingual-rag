Your phone number is your login and where we send security codes, so changing it takes a few extra checks.

## If you still have your old number

To change it:

1. Go to **Profile** > **Personal details** > **Phone number**.
2. Enter the code we send to your current number.
3. Enter your new number, and the code we send to it.
4. Take a quick selfie to confirm it's you.

## If you've lost access to your old number

Tap **Forgot password** > **I no longer have this number** on the login screen. You'll need to scan your ID and take a selfie. The change is reviewed by our team before it takes effect.

## After the change

For your protection, sending money to new recipients and sending money abroad are blocked for {{account.phone_change_hold.ALL}} after the number changes. Transfers to recipients you've paid before and card payments work normally.

The new number must be from the same country your account is registered in. A number from another country can't be added, even temporarily. See [Moving to another country](kb-0016).
