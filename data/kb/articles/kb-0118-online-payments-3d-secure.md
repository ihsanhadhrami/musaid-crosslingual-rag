When you pay online, many shops ask you to approve the payment in the Sahla Pay app. This is 3-D Secure, and it stops anyone using your card details without your phone.

## How does it work?

1. You enter your card details on the shop's website.
2. We send a notification to your phone.
3. Open it, check the shop name and amount, and tap **Approve**.

You have {{card.3ds_approval_window.ALL}} to approve. After that, the payment is cancelled and you'll need to start again.

## I didn't get the notification

Open the Sahla Pay app directly. Pending approvals appear at the top of the home screen. If notifications are off, you'll get an SMS code instead.

## I got an approval request for a payment I'm not making

Tap **Decline**, freeze your card, and report it. Someone has your card details.

## Some payments don't ask me to approve

That's normal. Some merchants, such as subscriptions you've already approved once, don't use 3-D Secure for repeat payments.

Payments approved with 3-D Secure are treated as authorized by you. If you approve a payment that turns out to be a scam, the protection for unauthorized transactions doesn't apply.
