To start paying and sending money, add money to your Sahla Pay balance. You can top up with a debit card or by bank transfer.

## Top up with a debit card

To top up by card:

1. On the home screen, tap **Add money** > **Debit card**.
2. Enter the amount.
3. Add your card details, or choose a saved card.
4. Confirm the payment in your bank's app or with the code your bank sends you.

The money arrives in your balance straight away. A small fee applies to card top-ups; see [Top-up fees](kb-0055).

## Top up by bank transfer

Bank transfers are free. Tap **Add money** > **Bank transfer** to see your Sahla Pay IBAN, then send money to it from your banking app. See [Topping up by bank transfer](kb-0054).

Credit cards can't be used for top-ups, and neither can prepaid cards issued by other companies. The payment will be declined by us even if your bank approves it.
