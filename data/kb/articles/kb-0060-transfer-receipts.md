Every transfer and payment has a receipt in the app, with a reference number you can share.

## Where do I find a receipt?

Tap the transaction in **Activity**, then **Share receipt**. You can save it as a PDF or send it by WhatsApp, email or any other app.

## What's on the receipt?

The date and time, amount, fee, recipient name, the last digits of their account, the status and a reference number starting with **SP**. For bank transfers we also show the bank's own reference once it's available.

## How far back can I get receipts?

For transactions in the last {{account.statement_history.ALL}}. For older ones, ask us for a statement through **Help** > **Chat with us**.

## Is a receipt proof of payment?

It shows that we sent the money. If the recipient says they didn't get it, see [Recipient didn't receive the money](kb-0063).
