Whether you need a proof of address depends on the country your account is registered in.

## Egypt

A proof of address is required for every account in Egypt, as part of verification. It must be dated within the last {{kyc.address_proof_max_age.EG}} and show your name and address.

## UAE

Not usually. We only ask for one if the address on your Emirates ID record doesn't match the one you gave us, or during a source of funds review. The document must be dated within the last {{kyc.address_proof_max_age.AE}}.

## What documents are accepted?

- utility bills (electricity, water, gas, internet or landline)
- a tenancy contract registered with the authorities (Ejari in Dubai)
- a bank or credit card statement

Mobile phone bills, screenshots of online banking and handwritten letters are not accepted.

## How do I upload it?

Go to **Profile** > **Verify identity** > **Proof of address** and either take a photo or upload a PDF. All four corners of the page must be visible.
