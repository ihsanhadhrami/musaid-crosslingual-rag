Happy National Day. Public holidays in the UAE are expected on 1 and 2 December 2025.

- Transfers between Sahla Pay users, card payments and QR payments work as usual.
- Local bank transfers and international transfers sent during the holidays are processed on the next business day.
- Bill payments are accepted, but the biller receives them after the holidays.

Salary payments into your Sahla Pay IBAN may also arrive after the holidays. See [Receiving your salary in Sahla Pay](kb-0252).
