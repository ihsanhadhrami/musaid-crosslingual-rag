## Check your connection

Most slowness comes from a weak connection. Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data, or move closer to your router.

## Update and restart

Install the latest version of the app, then restart your phone.

## Clear space

If your phone storage is almost full, everything gets slower, including Sahla Pay.

## Android: clear the cache

Go to Settings > Apps > Sahla Pay > Storage > **Clear cache**. Don't tap **Clear data** unless you're happy to log in again.

## Is it everyone?

Sometimes the problem is on our side. We post service updates in the app's **Inbox** and on our social media accounts.

Still slow? See [App won't open or keeps crashing](kb-0171) for more steps.
