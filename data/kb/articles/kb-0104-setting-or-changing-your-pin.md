You set your card PIN in the app. It's never sent by post or SMS.

## Set or change your PIN

To change your PIN:

1. Go to **Cards** and tap your card.
2. Tap **Card settings** > **Change PIN**.
3. Confirm it's you with your fingerprint, face or passcode.
4. Enter a new four-digit PIN twice.

The new PIN works straight away, everywhere.

## Choosing a PIN

Avoid your birth year, repeated digits like 1111, or simple sequences like 1234. The app won't accept the most common ones.

## I've forgotten my PIN

You can see it in the app: **Card settings** > **Show PIN**. Or simply set a new one.
