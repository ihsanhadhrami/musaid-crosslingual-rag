Find the message you see, or what's happening, below.

## "Incorrect password"

Check that caps lock is off and that you're using the right phone number. If you've forgotten it, tap **Forgot password**. Don't keep guessing: after {{security.max_login_attempts.ALL}} wrong attempts, login is locked for {{security.lockout_duration.ALL}}. See [Account locked after failed login attempts](kb-0154).

## "We couldn't send a code"

See [Not receiving OTP codes](kb-0153).

## "New device detected"

You're logging in on a phone we don't recognize. Enter the code and take a selfie when asked. See [Trusted devices](kb-0160).

## "Your account is restricted"

Follow the steps on screen, or see [Removing a restriction from your account](kb-0168).

## The selfie keeps failing

See [Selfie check failed](kb-0017).

## Biometric login doesn't work

Log in with your password, then turn biometric login on again in **Profile** > **Security**. It turns off automatically when you add a new fingerprint or face to your phone.

## Nothing happens when I tap Log in

Check your internet connection, and update the app.
