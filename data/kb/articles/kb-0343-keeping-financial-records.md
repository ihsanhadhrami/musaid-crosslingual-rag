Landlords, embassies, employers and family sometimes need proof of your income, your balance or a payment. Keeping good records makes these requests easy.

## Statements

Download statements from **Profile** > **Statements**, as PDF for sharing or CSV for spreadsheets. Statements in the app cover the last {{account.statement_history.ALL}}; for older records, contact support. Many people download a statement for each month and keep it in a folder. See [Downloading a statement](kb-0181).

## Receipts for transfers

Every transfer has a receipt with a reference number in **Activity**. Tap the transfer and choose **Share receipt**. If a landlord or a seller says they didn't get your payment, the reference number lets their bank trace it. See [Transfer receipts and reference numbers](kb-0060).

## Account details for your employer

If your employer or a family member needs your IBAN and account name, go to **Add money** > **Bank transfer** and tap **Share** to send them as a PDF.

## Visa and embassy applications

Some embassies ask for bank statements for the last few months. Check exactly what they need: some accept only statements from a bank, and may not accept statements from a payment account like Sahla Pay. If you're not sure, ask the embassy before you apply.

## Tax invoices

If you need to show the VAT you paid on fees, download tax invoices from **Profile** > **Statements** > **Tax invoice**.

## Before you close your account

Download everything you might need first. After closure, you can't log in to the app, and older records have to be requested from support. See [Closing your account](kb-0193).
