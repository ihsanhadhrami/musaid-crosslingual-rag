The selfie check compares your face with the photo on your ID and checks that it's a live person, not a photo of a photo.

## "We couldn't match your face to your ID"

- Take off glasses, hats and face coverings. A headscarf is fine if your face is fully visible, from forehead to chin.
- Face a window or a light, not away from it.
- Hold the phone at eye level, about an arm's length away.
- If your ID photo is very old, the match can fail even if you do everything right. Try once more, and if it fails again, contact us.

## "Please follow the instructions"

The app asks you to turn your head or blink to show you're really there. Move slowly and keep your face inside the oval until the circle completes.

## The camera won't open

See [Camera not working during verification](kb-0183).

## Out of attempts

You can try the selfie {{kyc.selfie_attempts.ALL}} times a day. After that, the button is greyed out until the next day.

If you're still blocked the next day, or your account shows **Manual check needed**, our team will compare the photos by hand. That takes the normal manual review time for your country, and you don't need to submit anything again.
