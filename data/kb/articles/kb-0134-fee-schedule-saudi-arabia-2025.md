These fees applied to personal accounts registered in Saudi Arabia during 2025. VAT at {{tax.vat.SA}} applied wherever marked.

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| Transfer to another Sahla Pay user | free |
| Transfer to a local bank account | {{fees.local_transfer.SA}} |
| International transfer | {{fees.intl_transfer_pct.SA@2025-01-01}}, with a minimum of {{fees.intl_transfer_min.SA}} + VAT |
| Debit card top-up | {{fees.card_topup.SA}} + VAT |
| Bill payment | {{fees.bill_payment.SA}} |
| Physical card | {{fees.physical_card_issuance.SA}} |
| Card replacement | {{fees.card_replacement.SA@2025-01-01}} |
| ATM withdrawal | {{fees.atm_withdrawal.SA}} + VAT; the first {{fees.atm_free_withdrawals.SA}} withdrawals each month were free |
| Foreign-currency markup | {{fees.fx_markup.SA}} |
| Dormant account | {{fees.dormancy_monthly.SA}} |
