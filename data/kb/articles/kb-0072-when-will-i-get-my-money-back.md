The return time depends on why the money is being refunded. The following periods cover the most common cases.

## A payment failed but my balance was charged

The amount is reversed automatically within {{failed_payment.reversal.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{failed_payment.reversal.AE}} in the UAE, and {{failed_payment.reversal.EG}} in Egypt. Contact us only if the applicable period has passed.

## A merchant says they issued a refund

After the merchant sends the refund, it reaches your Sahla Pay balance within {{refund.merchant_card.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{refund.merchant_card.AE}} in the UAE, or {{refund.merchant_card.EG}} in Egypt. The merchant's own processing time comes first, and we cannot track or accelerate that part.

## My wrong-transfer dispute was approved

Once the dispute is approved, the funds are returned within {{refund.wrong_transfer.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{refund.wrong_transfer.AE}} in the UAE, or {{refund.wrong_transfer.EG}} in Egypt. This period begins on the approval date, not the date you submitted the dispute.

## Are these business days?

Yes. All timeframes are measured in business days, excluding public holidays.
