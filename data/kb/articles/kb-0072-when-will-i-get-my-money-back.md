Waiting for money to come back is frustrating. Here's what to expect for the most common situations.

## A shop or website said they've refunded me

Once the merchant has sent the refund, it reaches your Sahla Pay balance within {{refund.merchant_card.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{refund.merchant_card.AE}} in the UAE and {{refund.merchant_card.EG}} in Egypt. Before that, the merchant has to process it on their side, and we can't see or speed that part up.

## I paid but the payment failed

Sometimes a payment fails but the money still leaves your balance. It comes back automatically: within {{failed_payment.reversal.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{failed_payment.reversal.AE}} in the UAE and {{failed_payment.reversal.EG}} in Egypt. You don't need to contact us unless that time has passed.

## I sent money to the wrong person and opened a dispute

If we approve your dispute, the money is returned within {{refund.wrong_transfer.SA}} (Saudi Arabia), {{refund.wrong_transfer.AE}} (UAE) or {{refund.wrong_transfer.EG}} (Egypt). That time starts when the dispute is approved, not when you open it.

## Business days?

Yes, all of these are business days, and public holidays don't count.
