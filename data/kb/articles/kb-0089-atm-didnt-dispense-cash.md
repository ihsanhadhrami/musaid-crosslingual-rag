The ATM took the money from your balance but gave you no cash, or less than you asked for.

## First, wait a little

Most failed ATM withdrawals are reversed automatically, often within the same day. Check **Activity** again later.

## Report it

If the money hasn't come back by the next day, open the withdrawal in **Activity** > **Report a problem** > **ATM didn't give me cash**. Tell us which ATM, the time, and how much cash you got, if any.

## How long does it take?

We check the ATM's records with the bank that owns it. Cases are resolved within:

- Saudi Arabia: {{atm.dispense_resolution.SA}}
- UAE: {{atm.dispense_resolution.AE}}
- Egypt: {{atm.dispense_resolution.EG}}

If the bank's records show the ATM had extra cash at the end of the day, the money is returned to your balance.

ATM withdrawal fees are refunded together with the amount. If the problem was at an ATM abroad, though, the case goes through the card network and can take up to {{chargeback.max_resolution.ALL}}.
