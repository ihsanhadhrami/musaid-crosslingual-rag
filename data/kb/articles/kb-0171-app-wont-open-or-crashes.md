Try these steps in order. Most problems are fixed by the first two.

## 1. Update the app

Open the App Store or Google Play and install the latest version of Sahla Pay.

## 2. Restart your phone

Turn it off completely and on again. This clears temporary problems.

## 3. Check your phone's software

The app needs {{app.min_ios.ALL}} or later on iPhone, or {{app.min_android.ALL}} or later on Android. See [Supported phones and operating systems](kb-0172).

## 4. Free up space

If your phone storage is almost full, the app may close unexpectedly. Delete some photos, videos or unused apps.

## 5. Reinstall

Delete the app and install it again. Your money and history are safe; they're stored with us, not on your phone. You'll need your password and a code to log in again.

## Still crashing?

Contact us through **Help** on the login screen, and tell us your phone model and what you were doing when it closed. If the app won't open at all, call the number on the back of your card. Your card keeps working in the meantime.
