Many customers send part of their salary to family abroad every month. Here's how to make it quick, cheaper and reliable.

## Save your recipients

The first time you send to a family member, save them as a recipient. Later transfers are just a few taps, and you won't need a one-time password again for the same recipient. See [Saved recipients](kb-0057).

## Can I schedule a monthly international transfer?

Not at the moment. Scheduled and recurring transfers only work for transfers to Sahla Pay users and local bank accounts. For international transfers, set a monthly reminder in your phone instead, and send it manually.

## Keeping costs down

- **Send one larger transfer instead of several small ones.** Each transfer has a minimum fee of {{fees.intl_transfer_min.SA}} in Saudi Arabia or {{fees.intl_transfer_min.AE}} in the UAE, so small transfers cost more per riyal or dirham.
- **Top up by bank transfer, not by card.** Card top-ups cost {{fees.card_topup.SA}} in Saudi Arabia and {{fees.card_topup.AE}} in the UAE, plus VAT. Bank transfers are free.
- **Compare the amount the recipient gets.** The app shows the exchange rate and the final amount before you confirm.

## Timing

Most transfers arrive within hours, but banks in many countries are closed at weekends and on holidays, and these differ from Saudi Arabia's and the UAE's. Check the guide for your destination country, such as [Sending money to Pakistan](kb-0218) or [Sending money to Bangladesh](kb-0219).

## Limits

International transfers count towards your daily limit of {{limits.daily_transfer_verified.SA}} in Saudi Arabia or {{limits.daily_transfer_verified.AE}} in the UAE. Some destinations also have a limit per transfer set by our payout partners.

Salary day is the busiest day for international transfers, and first-time or unusually large transfers are more likely to be held for a security review. If your family needs the money on a specific date, send it a day or two early.
