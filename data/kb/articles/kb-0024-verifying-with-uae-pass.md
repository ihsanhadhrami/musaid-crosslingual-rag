If your account is registered in the UAE, you can verify with UAE Pass. We receive your verified details directly, so you don't have to scan your Emirates ID.

## Before you start

You need a UAE Pass account at the verified level, not the basic level. You can check your level in the UAE Pass app under your profile.

## Verify with UAE Pass

To verify:

1. Go to **Profile** > **Verify identity** > **Continue with UAE Pass**.
2. Approve the request in the UAE Pass app.
3. Allow Sahla Pay to read your name, date of birth, nationality and Emirates ID details.
4. Take a selfie when asked.

## What if my UAE Pass is at the basic level?

The request will be declined. Either upgrade your UAE Pass account first, or verify by scanning your Emirates ID instead.

## Does Sahla Pay keep access to my UAE Pass?

No. We read your details once, when you approve the request, and when you update an expired Emirates ID. We can't sign anything or log in on your behalf.
