We're upgrading our international payment systems on **Friday 7 November 2025, from 6:00 AM to 10:00 AM Riyadh time** (7:00 AM to 11:00 AM UAE time).

International transfers can't be sent during this time. Transfers you confirmed before the upgrade continue as normal. Everything else in the app, including local transfers and card payments, works as usual.

The exchange rate you see after the upgrade may be different from the one before it. If you've been waiting for a good rate, check it again before confirming.
