Ramadan Kareem from everyone at Sahla Pay. This update is about planning ahead.

## New

- **Automatic transfers into Pockets.** When you create a pocket, you can now set a weekly or monthly transfer from your main balance, so money for Eid, school fees or rent builds up without you thinking about it. See [Budgeting with Pockets](kb-0259).

## Improvements

- Scheduled transfers now show the next payment date on the confirmation screen.
- Verifying with UAE Pass takes fewer steps. See [Verifying with UAE Pass](kb-0024).

## Fixes

- Freezing your card from the home screen sometimes took a few seconds to show as frozen. It now updates straight away.
