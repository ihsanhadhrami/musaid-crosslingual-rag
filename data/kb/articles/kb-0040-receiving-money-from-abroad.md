You can receive international transfers into your Sahla Pay balance in all three countries, including Egypt.

## What details do I give the sender?

Go to **Add money** > **Bank transfer** > **From abroad**. You'll see your name, your Sahla Pay IBAN and our bank's SWIFT code. Send these to the person paying you.

## Is there a fee?

Receiving is {{fees.intl_receive.ALL}} on our side. The sender's bank, and sometimes an intermediary bank, may take a fee, so you can receive a little less than was sent.

## How long does it take?

Up to {{transfer.intl_receive_time.ALL}} after the sender's bank releases the money. Money sent in another currency is converted to your local currency when it arrives, at our rate on that day.

## The money hasn't arrived

Ask the sender for the transfer receipt, sometimes called an MT103, and share it with us through **Help** > **Chat with us**.

Incoming transfers count towards your balance limit. If the amount would take you over it, the transfer is returned to the sender, and their bank may charge them for the return. Unverified accounts can't receive international transfers at all.
