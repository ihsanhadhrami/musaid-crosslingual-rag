## The app won't open

Update it, restart your phone, and check you have {{app.min_ios.ALL}} or {{app.min_android.ALL}} or later.

## I can't log in

Use **Forgot password** rather than guessing. After {{security.max_login_attempts.ALL}} wrong attempts, login is locked for {{security.lockout_duration.ALL}}.

## Codes aren't arriving

Wait {{security.otp_resend_wait.ALL}} and tap **Resend code**. Check your signal and that your number is up to date.

## Can I use Sahla Pay on a computer?

No, it's app-only.

## Is the app available in Arabic?

Yes. Change it in **Profile** > **Settings** > **Language**.

## When can I reach support?

Chat and phone support are open {{support.hours.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{support.hours.AE}} in the UAE, and {{support.hours.EG}} in Egypt.

## How far back is my history?

{{account.statement_history.ALL}} in the app. Ask us for anything older.
