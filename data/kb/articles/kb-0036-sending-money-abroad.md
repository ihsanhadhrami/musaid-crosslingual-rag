You can send money from your Sahla Pay balance to a bank account in another country. The recipient gets the money in their local currency.

## Before you start

- Your account must be verified.
- You'll need the recipient's full name as it appears on their bank account, their IBAN or account number, and for some countries the bank's SWIFT/BIC code.
- Check the fee before you confirm. See [International transfer fees](kb-0037).

## Send an international transfer

To send money abroad:

1. Tap **Send** > **International**.
2. Choose the destination country. The currency is filled in for you.
3. Enter the amount, either in your currency or in the currency the recipient will get.
4. Add the recipient's bank details, or pick a saved recipient.
5. Check the exchange rate, the fee and the amount the recipient will receive.
6. Tap **Confirm** and enter the one-time password we send you.

## What happens next

You'll see the transfer under **Activity** as **Processing**. Most transfers arrive the same day; see [How long do international transfers take?](kb-0039) for the maximum times. You'll get a notification when the receiving bank accepts it.

The exchange rate is locked when you confirm, so the amount the recipient gets won't change even if the rate moves before the money arrives. If the receiving bank rejects the transfer, for example because the IBAN is wrong, the full amount including the fee comes back to your balance in your own currency, at the rate on the day it's returned.

Sending money abroad is {{transfer.intl_available.EG}} for accounts registered in Egypt. Customers in Egypt can still receive money from abroad into their Sahla Pay balance; see [Receiving money from abroad](kb-0040).
