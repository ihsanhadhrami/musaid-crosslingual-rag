You can use your Sahla Pay balance to send funds to a bank account in another country. The recipient receives the transfer in their local currency.

## Before sending

Make sure that:

- Your Sahla Pay account is verified.
- You have the recipient's full bank-account name, IBAN or account number, and, where required, the bank's SWIFT/BIC code.
- You review the applicable charge before confirming. See [International transfer fees](kb-0037).

## Steps to send the transfer

1. Select **Send** > **International**.
2. Choose the recipient's country; the applicable currency is selected automatically.
3. Enter the amount in either your currency or the currency the recipient will receive.
4. Enter the bank details or select an existing recipient.
5. Review the exchange rate, transfer fee, and expected delivery amount.
6. Select **Confirm** and enter the one-time password sent to you.

## After confirmation

The transfer appears in **Activity** with a **Processing** status. Many transfers arrive on the same day, but maximum delivery periods are listed in [How long do international transfers take?](kb-0039). We notify you when the recipient's bank accepts the payment.

The exchange rate is fixed at confirmation, so changes in the market rate will not affect the recipient's amount while the transfer is in progress. If the receiving bank rejects the payment, such as when the IBAN is incorrect, the full amount and fee are returned to your balance in your account currency using the rate available on the return date.

International transfers are {{transfer.intl_available.EG}} for accounts registered in Egypt. Customers in Egypt can still receive international payments into their Sahla Pay balance; see [Receiving money from abroad](kb-0040).
