We keep your data while your account is open, and for a period after it's closed, because the law requires payment companies to keep records.

## After you close your account

| Country | We keep your records for |
|---|---|
| Saudi Arabia | {{data.retention_after_closure.SA}} after closure |
| UAE | {{data.retention_after_closure.AE}} after closure |
| Egypt | {{data.retention_after_closure.EG}} after closure |

This includes your identity documents, transactions and our messages with you. After that, the data is deleted or anonymized.

## Can I ask you to delete my data?

You can ask, and we'll delete what we're allowed to, such as marketing preferences and device data. Records we have to keep by law can't be deleted before the end of the period above.

## Selfies and ID photos

Used for verification and fraud prevention, and kept for the same period as your other records.

If your account was part of a fraud investigation or legal case, we may have to keep the related records for longer, until the case is closed.
