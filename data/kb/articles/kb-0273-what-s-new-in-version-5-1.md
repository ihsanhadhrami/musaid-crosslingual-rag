## Improvements

- Logging in with your fingerprint or face is faster.
- If face login fails three times, the app now asks for your passcode instead of locking you out.
- **Notifications** settings are now grouped: payments, security, offers and news. You can switch off offers and news. Security alerts are also sent by SMS and email, whatever your settings.

## Supported phones

As a reminder, Sahla Pay supports {{app.min_ios.ALL}} or later and {{app.min_android.ALL}} or later. See [Supported phones and operating systems](kb-0172).

## Fixes

- The app sometimes asked you to update when you already had the latest version.
