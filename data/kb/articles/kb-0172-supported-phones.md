## Which phones can I use?

- iPhone with {{app.min_ios.ALL}} or later
- Android phones with {{app.min_android.ALL}} or later, with Google Play services

## Can I use a tablet?

Yes, iPads and Android tablets that meet the same requirements work. The app is designed for phones, so some screens look stretched.

## Huawei phones without Google Play

Phones without Google Play services can't install the app from an official store, and we don't support installing it from other sources.

## Is there a web version?

No. Sahla Pay is app-only. You can't log in from a computer.

## My phone is no longer supported

When we stop supporting an older version, we tell you in the app first. After the end date, the app won't open on that phone, but your account, balance and card keep working. Log in on a supported phone to manage your money.

Rooted or jailbroken phones are blocked, even if they meet the requirements above, because we can't keep your account secure on them.
