The app needs your camera to scan your ID and take your selfie.

## Allow camera access

- **iPhone:** Settings > Sahla Pay > Camera, switch on.
- **Android:** Settings > Apps > Sahla Pay > Permissions > Camera > Allow.

Then close and reopen the app.

## Black screen

Another app may be using the camera. Close video calls and camera apps, and try again.

## The camera opens but won't focus

Clean the lens, and hold the ID a bit further away. Good light helps a lot.

## Can I upload a photo instead?

No. For security, IDs and selfies have to be captured live in the app. Photos from your gallery aren't accepted.

## Front camera not working

The selfie uses your front camera, and every verification method needs it, including Nafath and UAE Pass. If it's broken, contact us through **Help** > **Chat with us**. We can verify you on a video call instead, on another device of your own. Don't log in on someone else's phone to verify; it would become a trusted device on your account (see [Trusted devices](kb-0160)).
