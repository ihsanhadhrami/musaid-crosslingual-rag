You can pay online with your Sahla Pay virtual card, or with **Pay with Sahla Pay** where a website or app offers it.

## Pay with your card

Use your card number, expiry date and CVV from **Cards** > **Show details**. Many payments also ask you to approve them in the Sahla Pay app; see [Online payments and 3-D Secure](kb-0118).

## Pay with Sahla Pay

If you see the **Pay with Sahla Pay** button at checkout:

1. Tap it and enter your phone number.
2. Approve the payment in the Sahla Pay app.
3. Return to the shop. The order is confirmed straight away.

You don't share your card details with the shop this way.

## Something went wrong with my order

Contact the shop first. If they don't help, you can open a dispute. See [Goods or services not received](kb-0099).
