Notifications tell you about payments, transfers and security events, and they're how you approve online card payments.

## Check your phone settings

- **iPhone:** Settings > Notifications > Sahla Pay > Allow Notifications.
- **Android:** Settings > Apps > Sahla Pay > Notifications, and make sure battery saving isn't stopping the app.

## Check the app settings

In **Profile** > **Notifications**, make sure the types you want are switched on.

## Focus and do-not-disturb modes

These can hide notifications. Add Sahla Pay to your allowed apps.

## After a new phone or reinstall

Open the app once and log in. Notifications only start after the first login on a device.

Security alerts are also sent by SMS and email, so you'll still get those even if app notifications are off. Payment approvals for online shopping are different: if notifications are off, they arrive as an SMS code instead, which can be slower.
