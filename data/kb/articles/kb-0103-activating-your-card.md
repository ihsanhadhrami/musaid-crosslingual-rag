Your physical card arrives inactive, for your protection. Activate it in the app before you use it.

## Activate your card

To activate:

1. Go to **Cards** and tap **Activate card**.
2. Scan the QR code on the card's letter, or enter the last four digits of the card number.
3. Set your PIN. See [Setting or changing your PIN](kb-0104).

Your card works for online and contactless payments straight away.

## Why does the shop's machine ask me to insert my card?

The first payment with a new card has to be a chip-and-PIN payment, not contactless. Insert the card and enter your PIN once, and contactless works after that.

## What happens to my old card?

If this card replaces an old one, the old card stops working as soon as you activate the new one. Cut it through the chip before throwing it away.

Cards that aren't activated within {{card.activation_deadline.ALL}} of being sent are cancelled automatically, and you'd need to order a new one.
