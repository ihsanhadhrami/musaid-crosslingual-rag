Your money is protected by your phone's screen lock, your Sahla Pay password and biometric login. Still, act quickly.

## What should I do first?

1. Call your mobile operator and block your SIM.
2. Call our fraud line from another phone, open {{support.fraud_line_hours.ALL}}. We'll block the stolen device and freeze your card.
3. If you can, log in on another device and remove the stolen phone from **Trusted devices**.

## Can the thief use my app?

Not without unlocking your phone and then getting past the app's own lock. The app locks itself after {{security.app_auto_lock.ALL}} without use, and payments need your fingerprint, face or passcode.

## How do I get back into my account?

On your new phone, install the app and log in with your password. You'll need a code sent to your number, so get a replacement SIM from your operator first. Then take a selfie to confirm it's you. See [Using Sahla Pay on a new phone](kb-0178).

## Do I need a new card?

If your card was in a phone case or wallet that was also stolen, yes; see [Reporting a lost or stolen card](kb-0107). Otherwise, keep your card frozen until your phone situation is sorted, then unfreeze it.
