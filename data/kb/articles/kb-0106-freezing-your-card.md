Can't find your card? Freeze it. Freezing stops new payments straight away, and you can undo it any time.

## Freeze your card

Go to **Cards** and tap **Freeze**. The card turns grey in the app. No new card payments, ATM withdrawals or online payments will go through.

## Unfreeze it

Found your card? Tap **Unfreeze**. It works again within seconds.

## What still happens while my card is frozen?

- Your balance is safe, and you can still send money and pay with QR codes.
- Refunds still arrive.
- Payments that were already approved before you froze the card can still complete.

## Freeze or report lost?

Freeze if you think the card is just misplaced. If you know it's lost or stolen, report it instead, and we'll cancel it and send a new one. See [Reporting a lost or stolen card](kb-0107).

Recurring payments and subscriptions are also declined while the card is frozen. If you keep it frozen for a long time, update the card details with those merchants, or you may miss a payment.
