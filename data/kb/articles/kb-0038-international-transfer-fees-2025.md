This schedule lists the fees that applied to international transfers from a Sahla Pay balance during 2025.

| | Saudi Arabia | UAE |
|---|---|---|
| Transfer fee | {{fees.intl_transfer_pct.SA@2025-01-01}} | {{fees.intl_transfer_pct.AE@2025-01-01}} |
| Minimum fee | {{fees.intl_transfer_min.SA}} | {{fees.intl_transfer_min.AE}} |

The transfer fee was calculated as a percentage of the amount sent. If that calculation was lower than the applicable minimum, the minimum fee was charged instead.

The exchange rate included a margin above the reference rate. Before confirming a transfer, you could view the final rate and the amount expected to reach the recipient.

VAT applied to the transfer fee.
