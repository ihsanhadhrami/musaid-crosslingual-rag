Fees for sending money abroad from your Sahla Pay balance.

| | Saudi Arabia | UAE |
|---|---|---|
| Transfer fee | {{fees.intl_transfer_pct.SA@2025-01-01}} | {{fees.intl_transfer_pct.AE@2025-01-01}} |
| Minimum fee | {{fees.intl_transfer_min.SA}} | {{fees.intl_transfer_min.AE}} |

The transfer fee is a percentage of the amount you send. If that percentage comes to less than the minimum fee, you pay the minimum fee instead.

Exchange rates include a margin over the reference rate. You'll see the final rate and the amount your recipient gets before you confirm.

VAT applies to the fee.
