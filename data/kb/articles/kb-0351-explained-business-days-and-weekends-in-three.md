Sahla Pay works in three countries with different weekends and public holidays. That's why the same transfer can arrive at different speeds depending on where you are.

Public holidays don't count as business days in any country, and they're different in each one.

## What works every day

Transfers between Sahla Pay users, QR payments and card payments work at any time, including weekends and holidays.

## What depends on business days

- **Local bank transfers** usually arrive {{transfer.local_bank_delivery.SA}} in Saudi Arabia and {{transfer.local_bank_delivery.EG}} in Egypt. In the UAE, they take up to {{transfer.local_bank_delivery.AE}}.
- **International transfers** wait for a business day in your country, and they can also be delayed by weekends and holidays in the recipient's country.
- **Bill payments** are accepted at any time, but billers receive them on business days.
- **Reviews by our team**, like manual verification and disputes, are counted in business days.

## Two countries, two weekends

When you send money abroad, both weekends matter. A transfer sent from the UAE on a Friday leaves on a business day for you, but if Friday is part of the weekend in the recipient's country, their bank may only credit it on its next working day.

## Timelines in our articles

When an article says "business days", it means business days in the country where your account is registered. When it says "calendar days", weekends and holidays count. See [Transfers on weekends and public holidays](kb-0061).
