If your account is registered in Saudi Arabia, you can pay SADAD bills, such as government fees, traffic fines and utility bills, from your Sahla Pay balance.

## Pay a SADAD bill

To pay:

1. Tap **Payments** > **SADAD**.
2. Choose the biller, or search by biller number.
3. Enter the bill or subscriber number.
4. Check the amount and tap **Pay**.

## When is it posted?

Within {{bill.posting_time.SA}}. Some government services update their own records more slowly, so check with them before booking an appointment that depends on the payment.

## Fees

SADAD payments are {{fees.bill_payment.SA}}.

## I paid the wrong bill

SADAD payments can't be cancelled by us once they're posted. Contact the biller to ask for a refund, or see [Bill paid to the wrong account](kb-0096).
