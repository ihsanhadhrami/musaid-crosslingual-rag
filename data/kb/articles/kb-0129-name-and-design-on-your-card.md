## Whose name is printed on the card?

Yours, in English capital letters, taken from your verified ID. You can't choose a nickname or another person's name.

## My name is too long

Card names can be up to 21 characters. If yours is longer, we shorten it automatically, usually by using initials for middle names. You can see the printed version before you order.

## Can I have my name in Arabic?

Not on the card itself. Card networks only support Latin letters. Your Arabic name is used everywhere in the app if your app language is Arabic.

## Can I choose a card design?

There's one design for physical cards. Virtual cards come in several colours, which you can change in **Card settings** > **Card colour**.

If the name on your ID is spelled differently in English from what you'd expect, the card follows your ID. Correct your ID details first if they're wrong; see [Changing the name on your account](kb-0015).
