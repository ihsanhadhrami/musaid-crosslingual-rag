We don't send PINs by post, SMS or email. You choose your PIN yourself in the app when you activate your card.

## I activated my card but didn't set a PIN

Setting a PIN is the last step of activation. If you closed the app before finishing it, a temporary PIN was created for you. Go to **Cards** > **Card settings** > **Show PIN** to see it, and change it whenever you like.

## Someone called and gave me my PIN

That's a scam. We never call with your PIN and we never ask for it. Hang up, change your PIN in the app and report the call through **Help** > **Report fraud**.

## The letter with my card mentions a PIN

The letter only explains how to activate your card. If it contains a PIN or a sealed PIN slip, it didn't come from us; don't use the card, and contact us.

For how to change your PIN, see [Setting or changing your PIN](kb-0104).
