If your card is lost or stolen, report it so we can cancel it and send you a new one.

## Report your card

To report it:

1. Go to **Cards** and tap your card.
2. Tap **Card settings** > **Report lost or stolen**.
3. Choose **Lost** or **Stolen**.
4. Confirm your delivery address for the replacement.

The old card is cancelled permanently, straight away. It can't be reactivated, even if you find it.

## Fees

A replacement for a lost or stolen card costs {{fees.card_replacement.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{fees.card_replacement.AE}} in the UAE and {{fees.card_replacement.EG}} in Egypt. See [Card replacement fees](kb-0109).

## Were there payments I didn't make?

Check **Activity**. If you see anything you don't recognize, report it from the payment. See [Transactions I didn't make](kb-0090).

## I don't have my phone

If your phone was stolen too, call the number for your country from another phone and we'll cancel the card for you. See [My phone was stolen](kb-0165).
