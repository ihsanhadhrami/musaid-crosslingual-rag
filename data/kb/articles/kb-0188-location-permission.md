## Why does the app ask for my location?

To protect your account. Knowing roughly where you are helps us tell your real payments from fraud, for example when someone uses your card in another country while your phone is at home.

## Do I have to allow it?

No. The app works without it. Without your location, though, we may ask for extra checks more often, and some card payments abroad may be declined until you confirm them in the app.

## Do you track me?

No. We only check your location when you open the app or make a payment, not in the background. We don't share it with merchants or advertisers.

## How do I change it?

- **iPhone:** Settings > Sahla Pay > Location, and choose **While Using the App** or **Never**.
- **Android:** Settings > Apps > Sahla Pay > Permissions > Location.

The nearby-ATM map also uses your location; without it, you'll need to search by area.
