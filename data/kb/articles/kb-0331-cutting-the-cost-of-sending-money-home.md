If you send money to family every month, small changes add up over a year. Here's how to keep more of your money for the people receiving it.

## Send less often, in larger amounts

International transfers have a minimum fee. If you send several small amounts, the minimum fee applies to each one, so you can pay more in total than for one larger transfer. If your family can plan around it, send once a month rather than every week.

Check the fee before you confirm. The app shows the fee and the amount your recipient will get on the confirmation screen.

## Look at the whole cost

The fee is only part of the cost. The exchange rate includes our markup, and the recipient's bank may charge its own fee. Compare the amount that arrives, not just the fee. See [Currency conversion and exchange rates](kb-0064).

## Get the details right the first time

Returned transfers cost time, and a recall costs money. Before you send:

- Copy the IBAN or account number from a message from your recipient, rather than typing it.
- Use your recipient's full name exactly as their bank has it, including middle names.
- For mobile wallets, check the phone number with your recipient.

The guides for each destination list the common mistakes. For example, see [Sending money to the Philippines](kb-0220) or [Sending money to Pakistan](kb-0218).

## Avoid sending in a hurry

Transfers sent on Thursday evening, Friday or before a public holiday often wait for the next business day. If your family needs the money by a certain date, send it a few days early.

## Keep an eye on offers

We sometimes run offers on transfers to certain countries. They have end dates and conditions, so read them before you rely on them. Expired offers are kept in the news section for reference, but they no longer apply.
