The merchant says they've refunded you, but you don't see the money yet.

## "Completed" on the merchant's side isn't the same as arrived

When a merchant marks a refund as completed, it means they've sent it to their bank. It then travels through the card network to us. That takes up to {{refund.merchant_card.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{refund.merchant_card.AE}} in the UAE and {{refund.merchant_card.EG}} in Egypt.

## Look in the right place

Refunds appear in **Activity** as a separate line, with a green amount and the merchant's name. They don't change or remove the original payment. Use the search in **Activity** with the merchant name.

## It's been longer than the time above

Ask the merchant for the refund reference, sometimes called an ARN. Then contact us through **Help** > **Chat with us** and share it, and we'll trace the refund with the card network.
