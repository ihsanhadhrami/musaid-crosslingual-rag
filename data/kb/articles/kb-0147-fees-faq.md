## Is there a monthly fee?

No. Keeping an account is free. The only regular fee is the dormancy fee, if your account is inactive for a long time in the UAE or Egypt.

## How much does it cost to send money abroad?

{{fees.intl_transfer_pct.SA}} of the amount (minimum {{fees.intl_transfer_min.SA}}) from Saudi Arabia, and {{fees.intl_transfer_pct.AE}} (minimum {{fees.intl_transfer_min.AE}}) from the UAE, plus VAT. International transfers aren't available in Egypt.

## How much is a card?

A physical card is {{fees.physical_card_issuance.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{fees.physical_card_issuance.AE}} in the UAE and {{fees.physical_card_issuance.EG}} in Egypt.

## Is there a fee to top up?

Bank transfers are free. Debit card top-ups cost {{fees.card_topup.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{fees.card_topup.AE}} in the UAE and {{fees.card_topup.EG}} in Egypt, plus VAT.

## Do you charge for using my card abroad?

There's no separate fee for card payments, but our exchange rate includes a markup. ATM withdrawals abroad have a fee.
