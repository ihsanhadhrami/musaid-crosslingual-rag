What we charge for cash withdrawals with your Sahla Pay card.

| | Saudi Arabia | UAE | Egypt |
|---|---|---|---|
| ATM in your country | {{fees.atm_withdrawal.SA}} + VAT | {{fees.atm_withdrawal.AE}} + VAT | {{fees.atm_withdrawal.EG}} + VAT |
| Free withdrawals each month | first {{fees.atm_free_withdrawals.SA}} | none | none |
| ATM abroad | {{fees.atm_abroad.SA}} + VAT | {{fees.atm_abroad.AE}} + VAT | {{fees.atm_abroad.EG}} + VAT |
| Balance enquiry | free | free | free |

Withdrawals abroad are also converted at our exchange rate, which includes our markup.

The ATM's owner may add their own fee. The ATM shows it on screen before you confirm the withdrawal, and you can cancel if you don't want to pay it. We don't receive or control that fee.

Declined withdrawals are free.
