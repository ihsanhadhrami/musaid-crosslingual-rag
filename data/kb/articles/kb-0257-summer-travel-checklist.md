Going away for the summer? Run through this list a week before you leave.

## A week before

1. **Check your card's expiry date** in **Cards** > **Show details**. If it expires while you're away, the renewal card is sent about {{card.renewal_sent_before.ALL}} before expiry, to your home address. Activate it before you go.
2. **Check your ID's expiry date.** If it expires while you're away, payments are paused after the grace period.
3. **Turn on international payments** in **Card settings**.
4. **Add your card to a mobile wallet**, so you have a backup if your card is lost. Apple Pay is {{wallet.apple_pay.EG}} for accounts registered in Egypt; Google Pay works in all three countries.
5. **Read the guide for your destination**, for example [Using your card in Türkiye](kb-0233) or [Using your card in the United Kingdom](kb-0234).

## Money

- Top up enough for your trip, and leave room for hotel and car rental holds, which can last up to {{card.hold_release_extended.ALL}}.
- Know your daily ATM limit: {{limits.atm_daily.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{limits.atm_daily.AE}} in the UAE or {{limits.atm_daily.EG}} in Egypt.
- Always pay in the local currency, not your own.

## Security

- Keep your phone number active while roaming, because we may send you codes.
- Save the fraud line number from **Help** > **Call us**. It's open {{support.fraud_line_hours.ALL}}.
- Avoid logging in through public Wi-Fi without a passcode lock on your phone, and turn off any VPN when using the app.

## While you're away

Lost card? Freeze it in the app straight away. Replacement cards can only be sent to an address in your country, so keep using your virtual card or mobile wallet until you're home.
