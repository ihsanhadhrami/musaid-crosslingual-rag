If your balance looks wrong, first pull down on the home screen to refresh it.

## Money I expected hasn't appeared

Check how it was sent:

- From another Sahla Pay user: instant. Ask them to check the transfer status.
- From a bank: depends on your country; see [Topping up by bank transfer](kb-0054).
- From abroad: up to {{transfer.intl_receive_time.ALL}}; see [Receiving money from abroad](kb-0040).
- A refund: see [Refund timelines](kb-0071).

## My balance is lower than I expected

Look for **Pending** items in **Activity**. Card holds and pending transfers reduce your available balance before they're completed. See [Merchant holds and pre-authorizations](kb-0123).

Money in Pockets isn't included in your main balance on the home screen. Tap **Pockets** to see it.

## The balance is different on two devices

Log out and back in on the device showing the old balance. If it's still different, contact us.
