Subscriptions, memberships and other regular payments can be charged to your Sahla Pay card automatically.

## Where can I see them?

Go to **Cards** > **Recurring payments**. We list every merchant that has charged your card on a repeating basis, with the last payment date and amount.

## How do I stop one?

Cancel with the merchant. That's the only way to end the agreement. To stop the merchant from charging your card anyway, tap the merchant and choose **Block**. See [Subscription charges I don't recognize](kb-0081).

## What happens if my balance is too low?

The payment is declined. Many merchants try again a few days later, and may pause your subscription in the meantime.

## My card was replaced

Many merchants receive your new card details automatically through the card network and keep charging without interruption. Others don't. Check **Recurring payments** after a replacement.

Because some merchants update automatically, a subscription can keep charging your new card even after you replaced the card to get away from it. Blocking the merchant is the reliable way to stop it.
