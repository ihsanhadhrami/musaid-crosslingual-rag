Two-step verification means you need two things to get into your account or approve important actions: something you know (your password or passcode) and something you have (your phone).

## When is it used?

It's always on for:

- logging in on a new device
- adding a new recipient
- sending money abroad
- changing your phone number, email or password

You can't turn it off for these.

## Extra protection: approve every login

To be asked for a code every time you log in, even on your trusted phone, go to **Profile** > **Security** > **Code at every login** and switch it on.

## Passcode for payments

You can also require your app passcode, instead of fingerprint or face, for all transfers: **Profile** > **Security** > **Passcode for transfers**.

If you lose access to your phone number, two-step verification means you can't get codes until you've updated the number. Keep your phone number up to date, and see [Changing your registered phone number](kb-0013).
