Our support team now includes agents in Riyadh, Dubai and Cairo, so you can chat with someone who understands how you write, whether that's Gulf Arabic, Egyptian Arabic, Modern Standard Arabic or Arabizi.

## How to reach us

**Help** > **Chat with us** in the app. Write the way you normally write. You don't need to use formal Arabic.

## Hours

Chat and phone support are open {{support.hours.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{support.hours.AE}} in the UAE, and {{support.hours.EG}} in Egypt. See [Contacting customer support](kb-0185).
