When money is converted between currencies, we use a reference market rate plus a markup.

| Country | Markup on the reference rate |
|---|---|
| Saudi Arabia | {{fees.fx_markup.SA}} |
| UAE | {{fees.fx_markup.AE}} |
| Egypt | {{fees.fx_markup.EG}} |

## When does it apply?

- Card payments and ATM withdrawals in a foreign currency
- International transfers (Saudi Arabia and the UAE)
- Money received from abroad in another currency
- Refunds of foreign-currency payments

## Is VAT added to the markup?

No. VAT is only added to separate fees, not to the exchange rate.

## Where can I see today's rate?

In **Cards** > **Exchange rates**, and on the confirmation screen of every international transfer.
