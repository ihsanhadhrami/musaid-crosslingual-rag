Just moved for work? Here's how to get set up with Sahla Pay, from your first days to sending money home.

## What you need

- A local mobile number in your own name.
- Your residency ID once it's issued: {{kyc.id_document.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, or {{kyc.id_document.AE}} in the UAE.
- Your employer's details, if you plan to receive your salary into Sahla Pay.

## Before your residency ID is issued

You can open an account and use it at the unverified level, but you can't verify with your passport or entry visa. Until you verify, you can hold up to {{limits.balance_max_unverified.SA}} in Saudi Arabia or {{limits.balance_max_unverified.AE}} in the UAE, send up to {{limits.daily_transfer_unverified.SA}} or {{limits.daily_transfer_unverified.AE}} a day to other Sahla Pay users, and you can't send money abroad or get a card.

## Once you have your residency ID

Verify in **Profile** > **Verify identity**. In Saudi Arabia, the fastest way is Nafath; in the UAE, UAE Pass. See [Verifying with Nafath](kb-0023) and [Verifying with UAE Pass](kb-0024). After you're verified, you can get a card and start sending money home.

## Sending money home

International transfers are available as soon as you're verified. Check the guide for your home country, for example [Sending money to India](kb-0217) or [Sending money to the Philippines](kb-0220), for the details your family's bank needs and how long it takes.

## Your first month

- Link your bank account if you have one, so you can move money easily. See [Linking a bank account](kb-0026).
- Turn on notifications, so you see every payment.
- Learn the scams that target new arrivals, such as fake job fees and calls pretending to be from immigration. See [Recognizing phishing and scam messages](kb-0155).

If you change your mobile number when your employer gives you a new SIM, sending money to new recipients and abroad is paused for {{account.phone_change_hold.ALL}} after the change. Change the number in the app before you plan to send money home, not on the same day.
