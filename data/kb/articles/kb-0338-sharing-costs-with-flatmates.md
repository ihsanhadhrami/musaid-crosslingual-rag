Living with flatmates is cheaper, but sharing rent and bills can cause arguments if nobody is sure who owes what. A few simple habits keep things fair.

## Agree the rules first

Before you move in, agree:

- How rent is split. Equally, or by room size?
- Which bills are shared: electricity, water, internet, cleaning.
- Who pays the landlord and the bills, and when everyone pays them back.
- What happens if someone moves out early.

Write it down in a group chat so nobody forgets.

## One person pays, everyone pays them back

Usually one person pays the landlord or the bill, and the others send their share. With Sahla Pay:

1. Pay the bill from **Payments** > **Bills**, or pay the landlord by transfer.
2. Open the payment in **Activity** and tap **Split this payment**.
3. Choose your flatmates and adjust each share if needed.
4. Each flatmate gets a request they can pay with one tap.

See [Splitting a bill](kb-0046).

## Make it automatic

Each flatmate can set up a scheduled monthly transfer to the person who pays the rent, a day or two after salary day. Nobody has to remember, and nobody has to ask. See [Scheduled and recurring transfers](kb-0051).

## Flatmates who don't use Sahla Pay

Splitting only works with people who use Sahla Pay in the same country as you. For anyone else, share a payment link, or ask them to transfer to your IBAN.

## Deposits

If you pay the whole deposit to the landlord, keep the receipt and the tenancy agreement, and agree in writing how much each flatmate owes you. When someone leaves, settle their share of the deposit before they go.

## Keep records

Your **Activity** shows every request and who has paid. If there's ever a disagreement, it's all there.
