You can remove a linked bank account at any time, for example if you've closed it or want to link a different one.

## Remove a bank account

To remove it:

1. Go to **Profile** > **Bank accounts**.
2. Tap the account you want to remove.
3. Tap **Remove** and confirm with the code we send you.

Removing a bank account here doesn't affect the bank account itself, only its link to Sahla Pay.

## What about scheduled transfers?

Any scheduled or recurring transfers to that bank account are cancelled when you remove it. You'll see them disappear from **Payments** > **Scheduled**.

If a transfer to the account is already **Processing** when you remove it, it still goes through. It can't be stopped by removing the account.

You can't remove a bank account that you've chosen to receive your balance after closing your Sahla Pay account, while that closure is in progress.
