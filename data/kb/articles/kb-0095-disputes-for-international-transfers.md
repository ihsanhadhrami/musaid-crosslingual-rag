International transfers go through other banks, sometimes several, so getting money back after it's sent is harder than for local transfers.

## Wrong details

If the IBAN or name was wrong, the receiving bank usually returns the transfer on its own. The amount comes back to your balance in your currency, at the exchange rate on the day it returns.

## Sent to the wrong person, or want it back

We can ask the receiving bank to return the money. This is called a recall. Open the transfer in **Activity** and tap **Report a problem** > **Recall this transfer**.

A recall costs {{fees.intl_recall.SA}} in Saudi Arabia and {{fees.intl_recall.AE}} in the UAE. The receiving bank decides whether to return the money, and it usually needs the recipient's agreement. Recalls can take several weeks.

## Scams

If you were tricked into sending the transfer, report it as fraud straight away. The sooner we contact the receiving bank, the better the chance of stopping the money.

The recall fee is charged even if the receiving bank refuses to return the money, and it isn't refunded. If the bank does return it, intermediary banks may deduct their own fees, so you may get back less than you sent.
