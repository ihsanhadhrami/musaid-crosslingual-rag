Your Sahla Pay card works in Europe (Schengen area) wherever the card network logo is shown, for payments in shops, online and at ATMs. Most Schengen countries use the euro; a few, such as Switzerland, Norway and Czechia, have their own currency.

## Before you travel

- Check that **International payments** is switched on in **Cards** > **Card settings**.
- Top up enough to cover your trip, plus some extra for hotel and car rental holds.
- Keep your phone number active while roaming, because we may send you a code to approve a payment.
- Note the fraud line number from **Help** > **Call us**. It's open {{support.fraud_line_hours.ALL}}.

## Paying in local currency

When a card machine or ATM offers to charge you in your own currency instead of the local currency, always choose the local currency. If you accept the conversion, the shop's or ATM's exchange rate is used, which is usually worse than ours.

## If your account is in Saudi Arabia

- Card payments in euros or another local currency: no fee, but our exchange rate includes a markup of {{fees.fx_markup.SA}}.
- ATM withdrawals: {{fees.atm_abroad.SA}} per withdrawal plus VAT, as well as the markup.
- You can withdraw up to {{limits.atm_daily.SA}} a day and spend up to {{limits.card_daily_spend.SA}} a day by card, counted in riyals after conversion.
- Contactless without a PIN is limited to the equivalent of {{limits.contactless_no_pin.SA}} per payment.

You can also pay with Apple Pay or Google Pay.

## Local tips

Card payments are widely accepted. Some museums, small cafés and markets in southern and eastern Europe still prefer cash.

Hotels and car rental companies usually place a hold on your card at check-in or pick-up. Leave enough in your balance to cover it.

Some countries in the Schengen area, such as Switzerland, Czechia, Hungary and Poland, don't use the euro. Always pay in the local currency.

## If your card is lost or stolen

Freeze it straight away in **Cards** > **Freeze**. If you can't find it, report it as lost in the app. We can only deliver replacement cards to an address in the country your account is registered in, so use your virtual card or a mobile wallet for the rest of your trip.
