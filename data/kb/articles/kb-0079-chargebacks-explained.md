A chargeback is how we get your money back from a merchant's bank when a card dispute can't be solved directly. It runs under the rules of the card network, not just our own.

## How it works

1. We review your dispute and decide whether it qualifies for a chargeback.
2. We send the chargeback to the merchant's bank, with your evidence.
3. The merchant can accept it, or respond with their own evidence.
4. If they respond, we review both sides, and may send the case back to them once more.

The whole process can take up to {{chargeback.max_resolution.ALL}}, mainly because the merchant has time to respond at each step.

## What can't be charged back?

- Transfers to other Sahla Pay users or bank accounts. These are handled as transfer disputes instead.
- QR payments, which don't use the card network.
- Payments you approved and later regretted, if the merchant delivered what was described.

## What if the merchant wins?

You'll get the merchant's evidence and the reasons for our decision. Any temporary credit we gave you is taken back.

Chargebacks are also not available when the merchant is a gambling or crypto service, even if the card payment was approved. Disputes about these payments are reviewed only as possible fraud.
