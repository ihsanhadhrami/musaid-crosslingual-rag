You can send money from your Sahla Pay balance to a bank account in India, if your account is registered in Saudi Arabia or the UAE and is verified. The recipient receives Indian rupees (INR).

## What your recipient needs to give you

- the recipient's full name exactly as their bank has it
- their bank account number
- the bank's IFSC code, an 11-character code found on their cheque book or banking app

Ask the recipient to send you these details in writing, for example by copying them from their banking app, rather than reading them out. Most returned transfers are caused by a wrong digit or a name that doesn't match.

## How to send

To send money to India:

1. Tap **Send** > **International** and choose India.
2. Enter the amount, in your currency or in Indian rupees (INR).
3. Enter the recipient's details, or choose a saved recipient.
4. Check the exchange rate, the fee and the amount the recipient will get.
5. Tap **Confirm** and enter the one-time password we send you.

The exchange rate is fixed when you confirm.

## Things to know about this destination

Most Indian banks credit incoming transfers through the instant payment network, including on weekends and Indian public holidays. A few smaller cooperative banks only process them on their working days.

For larger amounts, the recipient's bank may ask them to confirm the purpose of the payment or to provide their PAN. This happens on their side and doesn't delay the money leaving Sahla Pay.

Transfers to Indian bank accounts are always paid out in rupees. You can't send dollars or riyals to an Indian account through Sahla Pay.

## If something goes wrong

If the receiving bank rejects the transfer, the full amount, including our fee, comes back to your balance in your own currency, at the exchange rate on the day it's returned. If you sent it to the wrong person, you can ask us to recall it, but a recall fee applies and the receiving bank decides whether to return the money. See [Disputes for international transfers](kb-0095).
