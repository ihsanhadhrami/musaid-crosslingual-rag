This notice explains what information we collect from your device when you use the Sahla Pay app and our website, and how you can control it. It supplements our Privacy Notice.

## 1. The app

The Sahla Pay app doesn't use browser cookies, but it collects similar information from your device.

### 1.1 Information that's always collected

To keep your account secure, the app always collects:

- a device identifier that we create when you install the app;
- your device model, operating system and version, and app version;
- whether the device appears to be rooted, jailbroken or emulated;
- the IP address of your connection, and whether it appears to use a VPN or proxy;
- technical logs of errors and crashes.

This information is needed to recognize trusted devices and to detect fraud. It can't be switched off.

### 1.2 Information you can control

With your permission, the app also uses:

- **camera**, for scanning your ID, taking your selfie and scanning QR codes;
- **location**, for fraud checks and the nearby-ATM map;
- **contacts**, to help you find friends who use Sahla Pay (contact numbers are compared in a protected form and not stored unless you send them money);
- **notifications**, for payment alerts and approvals.

You can change these permissions at any time in your phone's settings. Some features won't work without them.

### 1.3 Analytics

We use analytics tools to understand how the app is used, such as which screens are visited and where people get stuck. Analytics data is linked to a random identifier, not your name. You can opt out in **Profile** > **Privacy** > **App analytics**.

## 2. Our website

### 2.1 Cookies we use

| Type | Purpose | Can you turn them off? |
|---|---|---|
| Essential | Make the site work and keep it secure | No |
| Preferences | Remember your language | Yes |
| Analytics | Measure how the site is used | Yes |
| Marketing | Show you relevant Sahla Pay offers on other sites | Yes |

### 2.2 Managing cookies

When you first visit our website, you can accept or reject non-essential cookies. You can change your choice at any time using the cookie settings link at the bottom of each page, or by clearing cookies in your browser.

## 3. Sharing

Device information is shared only with service providers that help us run the app, analyse its use and prevent fraud, under contracts that protect your data. We don't sell device data or use it to build advertising profiles for other companies.

## 4. Retention

Device information linked to your account is kept as part of your account records. Analytics data not linked to your account is kept for a shorter period and then deleted.
