You can have one physical card and up to {{card.max_virtual.ALL}} virtual cards on your account.

## Can I have two physical cards?

No. Each account has one physical card at a time. If you order a replacement, the old one stops working.

## Can I get a card for my husband, wife or child?

Not on your account. Cards can only be used by the account holder. Your family member can open their own Sahla Pay account and get their own card. You can then send them money instantly and for free.

## Why have more than one virtual card?

Some people use one for subscriptions and one for one-off online shopping, so they can freeze or close one without affecting the other.

## Is there a fee for extra virtual cards?

In Saudi Arabia and the UAE, no. In Egypt, each virtual card has a one-time fee of {{fees.virtual_card.EG}}.
