By opening a Sahla Pay account, you agree to receive information, notices and documents from us electronically, and to sign agreements electronically. This consent forms part of the Terms of Use.

## 1. What we send electronically

We send electronically:

- the Terms of Use, Card Terms and Conditions, Fees and Charges Schedule, Privacy Notice and other policies, and any changes to them;
- statements, receipts and tax invoices;
- notices about your account, such as restrictions, dormancy and closure;
- security alerts and one-time passwords;
- responses to your disputes and complaints.

## 2. How we send it

We use one or more of these channels:

- the **Inbox** in the app;
- push notifications;
- SMS to your registered phone number;
- email to your verified email address.

A notice is treated as received when it is available in your app Inbox, or when we send it to your phone number or email address, whichever comes first. One-time passwords are sent by SMS only.

## 3. Electronic signatures

When you confirm an action in the app, for example by accepting terms, approving a payment with your passcode or biometrics, or entering a one-time password, that confirmation has the same effect as your signature on paper.

## 4. Keeping your details up to date

You must keep your phone number and email address up to date, so that you receive our communications. If we can't reach you because your details are out of date, notices sent to the details we hold are still treated as received.

## 5. Paper copies

You can download and save any document from the app. If you need a paper copy of a legal document, ask us through **Help** > **Chat with us**, and we'll send it free of charge.

## 6. Withdrawing consent

Because our service is provided only through the app, we can't provide an account without electronic communications. If you withdraw this consent, we'll treat it as notice that you want to close your account. You can still opt out of marketing messages without withdrawing this consent.
