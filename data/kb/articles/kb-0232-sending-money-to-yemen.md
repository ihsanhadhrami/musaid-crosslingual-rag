Sending money to Yemen is {{corridor.available_YE.SA}} from Saudi Arabia and {{corridor.available_YE.AE}} from the UAE at the moment, because of the restrictions that apply to transfers into the country and the lack of reliable payout partners.

## Can I send money to someone from Yemen who lives elsewhere?

Yes. The restriction is about where the money is sent, not about the recipient's nationality. You can send to a bank account in any country where international transfers are available.

## Can I pay with my card in Yemen?

Card payments and ATM withdrawals in Yemen are also declined while the restriction applies.

## Will this change?

We review restricted destinations regularly. When transfers to Yemen become available again, the country will appear in the list under **Send** > **International**.

## I received money from Yemen

Incoming transfers from Yemen may be held for review or returned to the sender, depending on the sending bank and the route the money took. See [Restricted countries and transactions](kb-0200).
