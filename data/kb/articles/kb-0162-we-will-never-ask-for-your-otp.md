Nobody from Sahla Pay will ever ask you for:

- a one-time password (OTP) or verification code
- your PIN
- your password
- your card's CVV
- remote access to your phone, for example through a screen-sharing app

This applies to calls, WhatsApp, SMS, email, social media and in person. It applies even if the person says they're from our fraud team, even if they know your name, card number or recent payments, and even if the call seems to come from our real number. Caller numbers can be faked.

## Why is the OTP so important?

The code is what approves a payment, a new device or a new recipient. Anyone who has it can act as you. When you read a code to someone, you're approving whatever they're doing.

## What do I do if someone asks?

Hang up. Then open the Sahla Pay app yourself, or call the number on the back of your card. If you've already shared a code, freeze your card and call our fraud line straight away.
