A car is one of the biggest payments most people make. Here's how to pay for one with Sahla Pay, and how to avoid the scams that target car buyers.

## Buying from a dealer

Dealers usually accept a bank transfer or a card payment for the deposit, and a bank transfer for the rest. Ask for an invoice with the dealer's company name and IBAN, and check that the account name on the transfer screen matches the company.

## Buying from a private seller

Never pay a private seller before you've seen the car, checked its documents and confirmed that the seller is the registered owner. Pay only when the ownership transfer is done or is being done in front of you. A transfer to a person can't be reversed if the car turns out not to exist; see [Investment scams and fake sellers](kb-0164).

## Paying more than your daily limit

Your daily transfer limit is {{limits.daily_transfer_verified.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{limits.daily_transfer_verified.AE}} in the UAE and {{limits.daily_transfer_verified.EG}} in Egypt.

In Saudi Arabia and the UAE, you can ask for a temporary increase, up to {{limits.temp_increase_daily.SA}} in Saudi Arabia and {{limits.temp_increase_daily.AE}} in the UAE, for {{limits.temp_increase_duration.ALL}}. Upload the car's sale agreement or the dealer's invoice. We review requests within {{limits.temp_increase_review.ALL}}. See [Requesting a temporary limit increase](kb-0150).

In Egypt, temporary increases are {{limits.temp_increase_available.EG}}. Split the payment over several days, or pay the rest from a bank account.

## Your balance limit

To pay a large amount, you first need it in your balance, and your balance can't go above {{limits.balance_max_verified.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{limits.balance_max_verified.AE}} in the UAE or {{limits.balance_max_verified.EG}} in Egypt. For a car that costs more than that, pay part from your bank directly.

## Records

Keep the receipt of every payment, the sale agreement and the ownership documents together. If anything goes wrong, they're what we and the police will ask for.

Large incoming transfers, for example when you sell your old car, may lead to a source of funds question. Keep the sale agreement for your old car; it's the document we'll ask for.
