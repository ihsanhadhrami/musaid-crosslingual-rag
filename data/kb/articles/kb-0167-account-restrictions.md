We may restrict an account when we see activity that could mean fraud, someone else using the account, or a breach of our terms.

## Why do restrictions happen?

- Payments that look like a scam, for example many transfers to new recipients in a short time
- Logins from unusual places or devices, together with other unusual activity
- Receiving money that other customers report as stolen
- Not answering a source of funds or re-verification request
- Repeated login lockouts

## What does a restriction look like?

A banner in the app explains what's restricted. Usually outgoing transfers and card payments are paused, while you can still log in, see your balance and receive money.

## What do I do?

Follow the steps in the banner. See [Removing a restriction from your account](kb-0168).

## Can you tell me exactly why?

Usually, yes. In some cases, the law doesn't allow us to explain a restriction, for example when it relates to an investigation by the authorities. When that happens, we'll tell you we can't share the reason, and we may not be able to say how long it will last.
