We send you an alert by SMS and email when someone tries to log in to your account from a new device.

## What does the alert say?

The device type, the approximate location, and the time. It also has a link to the app, never to a login page.

## It was me

You don't need to do anything. If you were trying to log in on a new phone, finish the steps on that phone.

## It wasn't me

Someone may know your password. Open the app on your usual phone and:

1. Tap **This wasn't me** in the alert in your **Inbox**. This blocks the new device.
2. Change your password.
3. Check **Trusted devices** for anything you don't recognize.

They can't log in without the SMS code and your selfie, so if you haven't shared a code, your account is still safe. See [Someone else accessed my account](kb-0157) if you think they got in.

## The location looks wrong

Locations are approximate and based on the internet connection. A mobile network can show a different city, especially when using mobile data.
