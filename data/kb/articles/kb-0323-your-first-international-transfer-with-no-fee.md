**This offer ended on 31 May 2026.**

## The offer

From 1 March to 31 May 2026, customers in Saudi Arabia and the UAE paid no transfer fee on their first international transfer, up to {{promo.first_intl_free_max.SA}} from Saudi Arabia or {{promo.first_intl_free_max.AE}} from the UAE.

## Terms

- Only your first international transfer ever counted. If you'd sent one before 1 March 2026, the offer didn't apply.
- If the transfer was larger than the maximum, the normal fee of {{fees.intl_transfer_pct.SA}} in Saudi Arabia or {{fees.intl_transfer_pct.AE}} in the UAE applied to the whole amount, not just to the part above the maximum.
- The exchange rate markup still applied.
- If the receiving bank returned the transfer, your next transfer didn't become fee-free.

See [International transfer fees](kb-0037).
