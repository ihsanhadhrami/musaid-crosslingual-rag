Happy Saudi National Day. Tuesday 23 September 2025 is a public holiday in Saudi Arabia.

- Transfers between Sahla Pay users, card payments, QR payments and local bank transfers work as usual.
- International transfers sent on the holiday leave on the next business day.
- SADAD bill payments are accepted, but the biller receives them after the holiday, so pay bills that are due on 23 September a day early.

Support and the fraud line are open as usual.
