Recipients you've sent money to are saved automatically, so you don't have to enter their details again.

## Manage your recipients

Go to **Send** > **Recipients**. From there you can:

- rename a recipient, for example "Mum" instead of the full name
- mark someone as a favourite so they appear first
- delete a recipient you no longer send to

## Adding a new recipient

The first time you send to a new bank account or a new international recipient, we send you a one-time password to confirm it. Later transfers to the same recipient don't need it.

## Editing bank details

You can't change the IBAN of a saved recipient. Delete them and add them again with the new details.

Deleting a recipient also deletes any scheduled transfers to them. After you add a recipient back, transfers to them are treated as new for a short time, so they may be held for a security review.
