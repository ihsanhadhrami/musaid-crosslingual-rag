Log in and approve payments with your fingerprint or face instead of typing your password.

## Turn it on

To turn on biometric login:

1. Go to **Profile** > **Security** > **Biometric login**.
2. Switch it on and enter your password once.
3. Confirm with your fingerprint or face.

## Which phones are supported?

Any phone with Face ID, Touch ID, or Android fingerprint or face unlock that's registered as "strong" biometrics in your phone's settings.

## It stopped working

If you add a new fingerprint or face to your phone, biometric login in Sahla Pay switches off automatically, for your safety. Log in with your password and turn it on again.

## Can someone else's fingerprint open my app?

Any fingerprint or face saved on your phone can unlock the app. Only keep your own biometrics on the phone you use for Sahla Pay.

Biometric login can't be used for everything. Changing your phone number, password or email always needs your password and a code, even if biometric login is on.
