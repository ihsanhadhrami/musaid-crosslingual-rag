Monday 6 October 2025 is a public holiday in Egypt.

- Transfers between Sahla Pay users, card payments and InstaPay transfers work as usual.
- Bill payments are accepted, but the biller receives them on the next business day.
- Customer support is open during its usual hours, {{support.hours.EG}}. The fraud line is open {{support.fraud_line_hours.ALL}}.
