We're sorry for your loss. This explains how a family member or legal representative can tell us about a death and receive the account balance.

## Tell us

Contact us through our website's contact form or by phone. You don't need a Sahla Pay account yourself. We'll freeze the account straight away, so no payments can be made from it.

## Documents we'll need

- The death certificate
- Your own ID
- A document showing you're entitled to the balance, such as an inheritance deed (a court-issued heirs certificate) or a court order

## How the balance is paid

Once we've checked the documents, we pay the balance to the heirs, to a bank account in their names, as set out in the inheritance document. If there are several heirs, we pay each one their share, or a single representative appointed by all of them.

## What about payments after the date of death?

Card payments and transfers made after the date of death are reviewed. Scheduled transfers and subscriptions are cancelled.

We can't share account details, transactions or statements with anyone until we've received the inheritance documents, even with close family members.
