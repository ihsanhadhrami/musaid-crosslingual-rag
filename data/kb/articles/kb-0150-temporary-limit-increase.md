Need to make a large one-off payment, like a deposit for a flat or a car? Verified accounts in Saudi Arabia and the UAE can ask for a higher daily transfer limit for a short time.

## How much and for how long?

| Country | Temporary daily limit | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Saudi Arabia | up to {{limits.temp_increase_daily.SA}} | {{limits.temp_increase_duration.ALL}} |
| UAE | up to {{limits.temp_increase_daily.AE}} | {{limits.temp_increase_duration.ALL}} |
| Egypt | {{limits.temp_increase_available.EG}} | |

## Before you start

- Your account must be verified, and your ID must be valid.
- Have a document showing what the payment is for, such as a tenancy contract, car sale agreement or invoice.

## Request an increase

To request it:

1. Go to **Profile** > **Limits** > **Request a temporary increase**.
2. Enter the amount and what it's for.
3. Upload your document.
4. Tap **Submit**.

We review requests within {{limits.temp_increase_review.ALL}}. If approved, the new limit starts straight away.

## Things to know

You can ask for a temporary increase up to {{limits.temp_increase_max_requests.ALL}} times per calendar year. Your monthly limit doesn't change during the increase, and the balance limit stays the same too. See [Can I increase my limits?](kb-0069).
