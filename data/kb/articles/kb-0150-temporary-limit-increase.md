Verified customers in Saudi Arabia and the UAE may request a short-term daily transfer increase for a significant one-time payment, such as a rental deposit or vehicle purchase.

## Available increase

| Country | Temporary daily limit | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Saudi Arabia | up to {{limits.temp_increase_daily.SA}} | {{limits.temp_increase_duration.ALL}} |
| UAE | up to {{limits.temp_increase_daily.AE}} | {{limits.temp_increase_duration.ALL}} |
| Egypt | {{limits.temp_increase_available.EG}} | |

## Eligibility and documents

Before applying:

- Confirm that your account is verified and your identity document is still valid.
- Prepare evidence of the payment purpose, such as a tenancy agreement, vehicle sale contract, or invoice.

## Submit a request

1. Open **Profile** > **Limits** > **Request a temporary increase**.
2. Enter the amount you need and explain the reason for the payment.
3. Upload the supporting document.
4. Select **Submit**.

Requests are reviewed within {{limits.temp_increase_review.ALL}}. If approved, the temporary daily limit takes effect immediately.

## Important restrictions

You may request this type of increase no more than {{limits.temp_increase_max_requests.ALL}} times in a calendar year. It changes only your daily transfer allowance: your monthly limit and maximum account balance remain unchanged. A large payment can therefore still use most of your monthly allowance or be blocked if it would exceed your balance limit. See [Can I increase my limits?](kb-0069).
