If the merchant refunded you, or you recognize the payment after all, you can cancel your dispute.

## Cancel a dispute

To cancel:

1. Go to **Help** > **My cases** and open the case.
2. Tap **Cancel dispute**.
3. Tell us why, for example "Merchant refunded me".

The case is closed straight away.

## What happens to a temporary credit?

If we gave you a temporary credit for the dispute, it's taken back from your balance when you cancel. If the merchant has refunded you as well, you'd otherwise get the money twice.

## Can I reopen it?

No. Once cancelled, a dispute can't be reopened, and you can't open a new one for the same payment.

Disputes that have already been sent to the merchant's bank as a chargeback can't be cancelled from the app. Message us from the case instead, and we'll withdraw it if the card network rules allow.
