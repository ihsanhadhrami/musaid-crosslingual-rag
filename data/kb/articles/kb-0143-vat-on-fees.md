Our fees are subject to value added tax (VAT) at the rate of the country your account is registered in.

| Country | VAT rate |
|---|---|
| Saudi Arabia | {{tax.vat.SA}} |
| UAE | {{tax.vat.AE}} |
| Egypt | {{tax.vat.EG}} |

## Which fees include VAT, and which have it added?

Card fees (physical card and replacement) already include VAT; the amount you see is what you pay. Most other fees, such as transfer, top-up and ATM fees, have VAT added on top. The fee schedule for your country shows which is which.

## What isn't taxed?

The money you send, receive or spend is not taxed by us. The exchange rate markup has no VAT either.

## Can I get a tax invoice?

Yes. Go to **Profile** > **Statements** > **Tax invoice** and choose the month.

Free services have no VAT. When a fee is waived, for example a free ATM withdrawal in Saudi Arabia or a free card replacement, there's no VAT to pay either.
