Opening an account takes a few minutes in the app. You'll need a phone number from the country you live in, and your ID if you want to verify straight away.

## Before you start

- Check you're eligible. See [Who can open a Sahla Pay account](kb-0002).
- Use a phone number registered in your name. It becomes your login and receives your security codes.
- Have your ID with you. You can skip verification for now, but your limits will be lower.

## Create your account

To sign up:

1. Download the Sahla Pay app from the App Store or Google Play.
2. Tap **Create account** and enter your mobile number.
3. Enter the code we send by SMS.
4. Add your full name exactly as it appears on your ID, your date of birth and your email.
5. Create a password and, if your phone supports it, turn on fingerprint or face login.
6. Tap **Verify now** to scan your ID and take a selfie, or **Later** to skip.

## What happens next

Your account is ready to receive money and top up as soon as you finish step 5. Until you verify, it works with the limits for unverified accounts; see [Unverified vs verified accounts](kb-0010). When you're ready to add money, see [Getting started: your first top-up](kb-0029).
