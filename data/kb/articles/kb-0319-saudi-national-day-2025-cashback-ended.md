**This offer ended on 25 September 2025.**

## The offer

To celebrate Saudi National Day, from 23 to 25 September 2025, card payments in Saudi Arabia earned {{promo.national_day2025_pct.SA}} cashback, up to {{promo.national_day2025_cap.SA}} per customer.

## Terms

- Only accounts registered in Saudi Arabia, and only card payments in Saudi riyals.
- Online payments to merchants outside Saudi Arabia didn't count, even if they were charged in riyals.
- Payments at petrol stations counted once the final amount was charged, not when the hold was placed.
