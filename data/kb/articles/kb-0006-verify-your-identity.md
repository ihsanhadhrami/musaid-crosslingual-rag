Verifying your identity lifts your limits and unlocks cards and international transfers. You only need to do it once, unless your ID expires.

## Before you start

- Have your original ID with you, not a copy. The documents for your country are listed in [Saudi Arabia](kb-0003), [UAE](kb-0004) and [Egypt](kb-0005).
- Find a well-lit spot and remove glasses and hats for the selfie.

## Verify

To verify your account:

1. Go to **Profile** > **Verify identity**.
2. Choose your document type.
3. Place your ID on a dark, flat surface and scan the front, then the back when asked.
4. Take a selfie, turning your head slowly when the app tells you to.
5. In Egypt, upload your proof of address.
6. Check your details and tap **Submit**.

## What happens next

Most checks are approved within minutes. If yours needs a manual review, you'll see **In review** on your profile; see [How long does verification take?](kb-0007). If something isn't clear, we'll tell you exactly what to redo. If the selfie fails, see [Selfie check failed](kb-0017).
