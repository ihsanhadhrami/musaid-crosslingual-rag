Good evidence makes a dispute faster and more likely to succeed. You can add files when you open the dispute, or later from the case in **Help** > **My cases**.

## What to include

| Problem | Useful evidence |
|---|---|
| Item not received | Order confirmation, expected delivery date, your messages with the merchant |
| Item not as described | Photos of what arrived, the listing or description you ordered from |
| Cancelled but charged | The cancellation confirmation, the merchant's cancellation policy |
| Charged twice | Both payments in **Activity**; a receipt showing one purchase |
| Refund promised, never came | The merchant's message promising the refund |

## Tips

- Screenshots are fine, but make sure the date and the merchant's name are visible.
- Add a short explanation in your own words. Arabic or English are both fine.
- Upload PDFs or photos, up to five files per upload.

## What not to send

Never send your full card number, CVV, PIN or password, even to us.
