Instead of sharing your account details, send a payment request. Your friend gets a notification and can pay with one tap.

## Request money

To send a request:

1. Tap **Request**.
2. Choose one or more contacts who use Sahla Pay.
3. Enter the amount and a short note, for example "Dinner Thursday".
4. Tap **Send request**.

## What happens next

You'll see the request under **Activity** > **Requests**. When your friend pays, the money arrives instantly and the request is marked **Paid**. They can also decline it, and you'll be told.

Requests expire after {{transfer.request_expiry.ALL}} if they're not paid. You can remind someone once a day, or cancel the request yourself.

## Can I request money from someone without Sahla Pay?

Not with a request. Share a payment link instead; anyone with a debit card can pay it. See [Payment links for small sellers](kb-0066).
