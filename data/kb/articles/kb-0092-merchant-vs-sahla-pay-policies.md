Two different sets of rules can apply when you want your money back, and it helps to know which one you're dealing with.

## The merchant's policy

Each shop decides whether it accepts returns, for how long, and whether it refunds money, gives store credit, or exchanges items. This is between you and the merchant. Sahla Pay can't make a merchant accept a return that their policy doesn't allow.

## Sahla Pay's policies

Our rules cover:

- how quickly a refund reaches your balance once the merchant sends it ([Refund timelines](kb-0071))
- disputes, when a merchant didn't deliver, charged you wrongly, or broke their own policy ([Disputing a card transaction](kb-0076))
- payments you didn't make ([Transactions I didn't make](kb-0090))

## An example

You bought shoes, changed your mind, and the shop's policy says "no refunds on sale items". That's the merchant's decision, and a dispute won't change it. But if the shop promised a refund in writing and never sent it, that's something we can help with.
