Whether you're performing Hajj or Umrah from inside Saudi Arabia or travelling from the UAE or Egypt, here's how to use Sahla Pay for the journey, from paying your package to spending in Makkah and Madinah.

## Paying for your package

Licensed Hajj and Umrah companies accept payment in different ways. Ask your company which ones they accept before you book.

- **By card:** pay online or at the company's office with your Sahla Pay card. Card payments are limited to {{limits.card_daily_spend.SA}} a day in Saudi Arabia, {{limits.card_daily_spend.AE}} in the UAE and {{limits.card_daily_spend.EG}} in Egypt.
- **By bank transfer:** send money to the company's bank account from **Send** > **To a bank account**, in your own country. Keep the receipt from **Activity** as proof of payment.
- **By SADAD:** some packages booked in Saudi Arabia are paid by SADAD bill. See [Paying government bills with SADAD](kb-0058).

Only pay companies licensed by the authorities. Fake Hajj and Umrah offers on social media are a common scam, especially before the season. See [Investment scams and fake sellers](kb-0164).

## If your account is in Saudi Arabia

You're paying in riyals, so there are no exchange rate costs. Your normal limits apply: {{limits.daily_transfer_verified.SA}} a day for transfers. If you're paying a large amount for several family members at once, you can ask for a temporary increase of up to {{limits.temp_increase_daily.SA}}; see [Requesting a temporary limit increase](kb-0150).

## If your account is in the UAE

Payments in Saudi riyals are converted at our rate, which includes a markup of {{fees.fx_markup.AE}}. Cash withdrawals in Saudi Arabia cost {{fees.atm_abroad.AE}} each, plus VAT and the markup. Card payments in shops have no extra fee. Apple Pay and Google Pay both work.

## If your account is in Egypt

Payments in Saudi riyals are converted with a markup of {{fees.fx_markup.EG}}, and cash withdrawals cost {{fees.atm_abroad.EG}} each, plus VAT. Apple Pay is {{wallet.apple_pay.EG}} for accounts registered in Egypt, so add your card to Google Pay or carry the physical card. Since international transfers are {{transfer.intl_available.EG}} from Egypt, you can't pay a Saudi company by bank transfer from your Sahla Pay account; pay by card instead.

## Spending in Makkah and Madinah

Cards and contactless are accepted in hotels, restaurants, supermarkets and most shops around the Haramain. Some street sellers and small stalls take cash only, so carry a little. ATMs near the mosques are very busy during the season; withdraw what you need before the peak days.

## Keeping your money safe during the season

Crowds make phones and wallets easy targets. Keep only the card you need with you, use your phone's lock screen, and know how to freeze your card in the app. If something is stolen, call our fraud line, which is open {{support.fraud_line_hours.ALL}}.

During Hajj, the days of Arafah and Eid al-Adha are public holidays in all three countries. Bank transfers in the UAE and international transfers are processed on the next business day, so pay any remaining balance to your Hajj company well before the season starts. Transfers between Sahla Pay users and card payments are not affected.
