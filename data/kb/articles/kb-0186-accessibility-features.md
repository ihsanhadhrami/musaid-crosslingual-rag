We want everyone to be able to use Sahla Pay on their own.

## Screen readers

The app works with VoiceOver on iPhone and TalkBack on Android, in Arabic and English. Buttons, amounts and card details are labelled for screen readers.

## Text size and contrast

The app follows your phone's text size setting. You can also turn on **High contrast** in **Profile** > **Settings** > **Accessibility**.

## Verification

If you can't complete the ID scan or selfie because of a disability, contact us. We can verify you on an assisted video call instead.

## Cards

Our physical cards have a notch on the short edge so you can tell which way to insert them.

## Support

Chat is available for customers who are deaf or hard of hearing. If you'd like someone you trust to help you with a call, we can talk to them while you're present, but you'll still need to confirm your identity yourself.
