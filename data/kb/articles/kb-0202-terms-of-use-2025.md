These Terms of Use apply to personal Sahla Pay accounts from 1 January 2025. A plain-language summary is available in [Terms of use summary](kb-0191), but only this document is legally binding.

## 1. About these terms

### 1.1 Who we are

"Sahla Pay", "we", "us" and "our" mean the Sahla Pay company that provides your account in the country where your account is registered. Each country's company is licensed and supervised separately, under the laws of that country. When these terms say "your country", they mean the country in which your account is registered, not the country you happen to be in.

### 1.2 What these terms cover

These terms govern your Sahla Pay account, the Sahla Pay app, payments and transfers from your balance, and the services described in them. Your Sahla Pay card is also covered by the Card Terms and Conditions, and if the two documents conflict on a card matter, the Card Terms and Conditions apply. Our fees are set out in the Fees and Charges Schedule, which forms part of these terms.

### 1.3 Language

These terms are available in Arabic and English. If the two versions differ, the {{legal.governing_language.SA}} version prevails for accounts registered in Saudi Arabia, the {{legal.governing_language.AE}} version prevails for accounts registered in the UAE, and the {{legal.governing_language.EG}} version prevails for accounts registered in Egypt.

### 1.4 Contacting us

You can contact us through **Help** in the app, by phone on the number shown in the app and on your card, or in writing to the address published in the app under **Profile** > **Legal**. We will contact you through the app, by SMS, by email or by phone, using the details you have given us.
