Sahla Pay may close an account in certain circumstances, including when continuing to provide the account would breach our requirements or the law.

## Reasons an account may be closed

Examples include:

- The account was created using false information or another person's details.
- The account is connected to fraud, scams, or other unlawful activity.
- You have seriously or repeatedly breached our terms, such as operating a business through a personal account.
- You have not completed a required review, such as a source-of-funds check or re-verification, after repeated reminders.
- The account has remained dormant with no balance for an extended period. See [Dormant accounts](kb-0194).

## Notice before closure

In most cases, we provide at least {{legal.closure_notice.ALL}} notice by email and in the app. This gives you time to arrange access to or transfer your funds.

## Returning your balance

After closure, the remaining balance is sent to a bank account in your name within {{account.closure_payout.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{account.closure_payout.AE}} in the UAE, or {{account.closure_payout.EG}} in Egypt.

If the closure relates to suspected fraud or a legal requirement, we may act immediately without notice. We may also be unable to disclose the reason. In such cases, the balance may be held until the relevant authorities permit its release.
