In some situations, we may close an account ourselves.

## Why might you close my account?

- The account was opened with false or someone else's details.
- The account is used for fraud, scams or other illegal activity.
- You've seriously or repeatedly broken our terms, for example by using a personal account for a business.
- You haven't completed required checks, such as source of funds or re-verification, after several reminders.
- The account has been dormant with a zero balance for a long time. See [Dormant accounts](kb-0194).

## How much notice will I get?

Usually at least {{legal.closure_notice.ALL}}, by email and in the app, so you have time to move your money.

## What happens to my money?

Your balance is paid to a bank account in your name, within {{account.closure_payout.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{account.closure_payout.AE}} in the UAE or {{account.closure_payout.EG}} in Egypt after the account closes.

If we close an account because of fraud or a legal requirement, we may close it immediately, without notice, and we may not be able to tell you why. In these cases, the balance may be held until the authorities allow us to release it.
