This schedule covers personal accounts registered in Saudi Arabia from 1 March 2026. Where indicated, VAT at {{tax.vat.SA}} is added to the listed fee.

## Transfers

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| Transfer to another Sahla Pay user | free |
| Transfer to a local bank account | {{fees.local_transfer.SA}} |
| International transfer | {{fees.intl_transfer_pct.SA}}, with a minimum of {{fees.intl_transfer_min.SA}} + VAT |
| Exchange rate markup | {{fees.fx_markup.SA}}, included in the displayed rate |

## Top-ups and payments

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| Debit card top-up | {{fees.card_topup.SA}} + VAT |
| Bank transfer top-up | free |
| Bill payment | {{fees.bill_payment.SA}} |

## Cards

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| Virtual card | {{fees.virtual_card.SA}} |
| Physical card | {{fees.physical_card_issuance.SA}}, one-time charge with VAT included |
| Replacement for a lost, stolen, or damaged card | {{fees.card_replacement.SA}}, with VAT included |
| ATM withdrawal | {{fees.atm_withdrawal.SA}} + VAT; the first {{fees.atm_free_withdrawals.SA}} withdrawals each month are free |
| Foreign-currency card payment | {{fees.fx_markup.SA}}, included in the displayed rate |

## Account

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| Account opening and maintenance | free |
| Dormant account | {{fees.dormancy_monthly.SA}} |

We provide at least {{fees.change_notice.ALL}} notice before increasing any fee.
