All fees for personal accounts registered in Saudi Arabia, effective 1 March 2026. VAT at {{tax.vat.SA}} is added to fees where marked.

## Transfers

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| To another Sahla Pay user | free |
| To a local bank account | {{fees.local_transfer.SA}} |
| International transfer | {{fees.intl_transfer_pct.SA}}, minimum {{fees.intl_transfer_min.SA}} + VAT |
| Exchange rate markup | {{fees.fx_markup.SA}} (included in the rate) |

## Top-ups and payments

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| Top-up by debit card | {{fees.card_topup.SA}} + VAT |
| Top-up by bank transfer | free |
| Bill payments | {{fees.bill_payment.SA}} |

## Cards

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| Virtual card | {{fees.virtual_card.SA}} |
| Physical card | {{fees.physical_card_issuance.SA}} (one-time, VAT included) |
| Card replacement (lost, stolen or damaged) | {{fees.card_replacement.SA}} (VAT included) |
| ATM withdrawal | {{fees.atm_withdrawal.SA}} + VAT; the first {{fees.atm_free_withdrawals.SA}} each month are free |
| Card payments in a foreign currency | {{fees.fx_markup.SA}} (included in the rate) |

## Account

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| Opening and keeping an account | free |
| Dormant account | {{fees.dormancy_monthly.SA}} |

We give at least {{fees.change_notice.ALL}} notice before any fee goes up.
