Receiving money into your Sahla Pay account is free.

## From other Sahla Pay users

Free, and instant.

## From a bank account in your country

Free on our side. The sender's bank may charge them.

## From abroad

{{fees.intl_receive.ALL}} on our side. Intermediary banks and the sender's bank can take fees on the way, so the amount that arrives can be lower than what was sent. If it's in another currency, our exchange rate applies.

## Through a payment link

Payment links are the exception. When someone pays your payment link, a seller fee is taken from the payment: {{fees.payment_link.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{fees.payment_link.AE}} in the UAE and {{fees.payment_link.EG}} in Egypt. See [Payment links for small sellers](kb-0066).
