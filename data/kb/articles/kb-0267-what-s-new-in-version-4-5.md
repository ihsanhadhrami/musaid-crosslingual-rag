Just in time for the summer holidays: more control over where your card works.

## New

- **Card controls.** In **Cards** > **Card settings**, you can now switch on or off online payments, contactless payments, ATM withdrawals and international payments separately. See [Card spending controls](kb-0122).

International payments are on by default. If you switch them off at home for extra safety, remember to switch them back on before you travel.

## Fixes

- Some hotel holds stayed in **Activity** as **Pending** after the hotel had released them. They now disappear when the hold is released.
