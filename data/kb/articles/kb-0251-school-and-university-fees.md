School and university fees are often one of the largest payments of the year. Here's how to pay them with Sahla Pay.

## Paying a school or university in your country

Schools usually accept one or more of these:

- **Card payment** through the school's online portal. Card payments are limited to {{limits.card_daily_spend.SA}} a day in Saudi Arabia, {{limits.card_daily_spend.AE}} in the UAE and {{limits.card_daily_spend.EG}} in Egypt.
- **Bank transfer** to the school's account. Use the student's name and ID number as the reference, so the school can match the payment.
- **Bill payment**, where the school is listed under **Payments** > **Bills** > **Education**.

Ask the school which method it prefers and how long it takes to confirm a payment. Pay at least a few days before the deadline.

## Paying in instalments

Many schools let you pay in terms. Set up a scheduled transfer for each instalment date, so you don't miss one. See [Scheduled and recurring transfers](kb-0051).

## Paying a university abroad

From Saudi Arabia and the UAE, you can pay a university abroad by international transfer. Universities usually give an IBAN or account number and a SWIFT code, and ask you to include the student's ID in the reference. The fee is {{fees.intl_transfer_pct.SA}} from Saudi Arabia and {{fees.intl_transfer_pct.AE}} from the UAE, with a minimum per transfer. Intermediary banks may deduct fees, so check with the university whether they need the exact amount to arrive. International transfers aren't available from Egypt.

## Large payments

If the fees are more than your daily transfer limit, you can split the payment over several days, or, in Saudi Arabia and the UAE, ask for a temporary limit increase by uploading the school's invoice. See [Requesting a temporary limit increase](kb-0150).
