**Update, 14 January 2026:** Fixed in version 5.1.1. Update the app from Google Play.

After the last update, some customers using Android phones couldn't log in with their fingerprint. Logging in with your password still worked.

If you still can't log in after updating, see [Can't log in](kb-0173). Don't uninstall and reinstall the app as a first step: you'll need your SMS code and a selfie check on the next login, and new transfers may be paused for a while as if you'd moved to a new phone.
