An account is dormant when you haven't made any transaction yourself for a set period. Money you receive and fees we charge don't count as activity.

## When does an account become dormant?

| Country | Dormant after no activity for |
|---|---|
| Saudi Arabia | {{account.dormant_after.SA}} |
| UAE | {{account.dormant_after.AE}} |
| Egypt | {{account.dormant_after.EG}} |

We'll remind you by email and in the app before it happens.

## What changes when an account is dormant?

- Your card stops working and isn't renewed.
- You can still log in, see your balance and receive money.
- In the UAE and Egypt, a monthly dormancy fee applies. See [Dormant account fees](kb-0137).

## Reactivate your account

Log in and make any transaction yourself, such as a top-up or a payment. If your ID has expired in the meantime, you'll need to update it first.

## Closure of dormant accounts

If a dormant account has a zero balance for a further {{account.dormant_closure.ALL}}, we may close it, with notice by email. Accounts with money in them are never closed just for being dormant. Their balance stays yours, and we're required by law to keep it safe for you or pass it to the authority responsible for unclaimed funds.
