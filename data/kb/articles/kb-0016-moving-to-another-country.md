Each Sahla Pay account belongs to one country: Saudi Arabia, the UAE or Egypt. Your fees, limits and rules follow that country, and it can't be changed on an existing account.

## Moving between Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Egypt

If you move to another country where Sahla Pay operates, you'll need to close your current account and open a new one with a phone number and ID from your new country.

To move your money:

1. Open your new account and verify it.
2. In your old account, close the account and choose a bank account in your name for the remaining balance. See [Closing your account](kb-0193).
3. Top up your new account from that bank account.

We can't move your balance or history directly between countries, because each country's account is held under different regulations.

## Moving somewhere Sahla Pay doesn't operate

You can keep using your account while you're travelling. If you live abroad for a long time, your account may be restricted when your ID or residency expires, and you'll need to close it from abroad. Make sure your balance is paid out to a bank account you can still access.

## Visiting another country

Short trips are fine. Your card works abroad, and the normal foreign currency fees apply.
