Every fee appears in **Activity** as its own line or on the transaction it belongs to. Tap it to see what it's for.

## "Dormancy fee"

Your account had no activity from you for a long time. See [Dormant account fees](kb-0137).

## "Card top-up fee"

You added money with a debit card. Bank transfer top-ups are free. See [Top-up fees](kb-0055).

## "ATM fee"

You withdrew cash. In Saudi Arabia, the first few withdrawals each month are free. See [ATM withdrawal fees](kb-0117).

## "Transfer fee"

You sent money to a bank account or abroad. Transfers to other Sahla Pay users are free.

## "VAT"

Value added tax on one of our fees. See [VAT on fees](kb-0143).

## A fee I don't recognize

Some charges aren't ours: a fee taken by an ATM's owner, a merchant's convenience charge, or a difference caused by a currency conversion. If you still think a fee is wrong, contact us from the transaction with **Report a problem**.
