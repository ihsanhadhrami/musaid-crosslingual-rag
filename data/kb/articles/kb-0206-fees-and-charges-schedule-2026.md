This Fees and Charges Schedule forms part of the Terms of Use and the Card Terms and Conditions. It applies from 1 March 2026 and replaces the schedule dated 1 January 2025. Fees are charged in the currency of your account and deducted from your balance.

## 1. How to read this schedule

- "VAT included" means the amount shown is the total you pay.
- "+ VAT" means value added tax is added to the amount shown, at {{tax.vat.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{tax.vat.AE}} in the UAE and {{tax.vat.EG}} in Egypt.
- "free" means we don't charge a fee. Other providers, such as the recipient's bank or an ATM operator, may still charge their own fees.
- Percentages are calculated on the amount of the payment, in the currency of your account.

## 5. Fees that are waived or refunded

- A card that stops working within {{card.replacement_free_window.ALL}} of issue, without physical damage, is replaced free of charge.
- A card that never arrives is replaced free of charge, unless the courier's records show it was delivered and signed for.
- A card retained by an ATM is replaced without the lost card fee.
- Transfer fees are refunded if a transfer fails or is returned by the recipient's bank.
- The fee for recalling an international transfer is not refunded, even if the recall fails.
- Card top-up fees are not refunded if you later move the money out of your account.
- The dormancy fee is never charged if your balance is zero, and never reduces your balance below zero.

## 6. Changes to fees

We will give you at least {{fees.change_notice.ALL}} notice before increasing a fee or introducing a new fee. Reductions may apply immediately. Changes required by law, including changes to the rate of VAT, apply from the date set by law.
