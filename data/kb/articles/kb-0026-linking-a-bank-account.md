Linking your own bank account makes it quicker to top up and to move money back to your bank. You can link up to {{account.max_linked_banks.ALL}} bank accounts.

## Before you start

- Your Sahla Pay account must be verified.
- The bank account must be in your own name and in the same country as your Sahla Pay account.
- Have your IBAN ready. You'll find it in your banking app or on your bank statement.

## Link your bank account

To link an account:

1. Go to **Profile** > **Bank accounts** > **Add bank account**.
2. Enter your IBAN. The bank name is filled in automatically.
3. Confirm that the account is in your name.
4. Enter the code we send to your phone.

## What happens next

We check the name on the bank account with your bank. This usually takes a few minutes, but can take up to one business day. You'll see **Linked** next to the account when it's ready.

Moving money to a linked bank account in your own name doesn't count towards your monthly transfer limit. See [Daily and monthly transfer limits](kb-0034).

## Why was my bank account rejected?

The most common reason is that the name on the bank account doesn't match your Sahla Pay name, for example a joint account or a company account. Only personal accounts in your own name can be linked.
