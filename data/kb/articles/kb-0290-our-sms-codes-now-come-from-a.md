From 15 October 2025, SMS codes and security alerts from Sahla Pay come from the sender name **SAHLAPAY**. Earlier messages came from a number, which some phones put in a separate thread.

## Stay safe

The sender name doesn't prove a message is genuine; scammers can fake sender names. Our messages never ask you to tap a link to log in, and we never ask for a code by phone, chat or SMS. If someone asks for a code, it's a scam. See [We will never ask for your OTP](kb-0162).
