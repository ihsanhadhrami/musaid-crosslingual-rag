Adding money by bank transfer is always free. Debit card top-ups have a percentage fee.

| Country | Debit card top-up | Bank transfer top-up |
|---|---|---|
| Saudi Arabia | {{fees.card_topup.SA}} + VAT | free |
| UAE | {{fees.card_topup.AE}} + VAT | free |
| Egypt | {{fees.card_topup.EG}} + VAT | free |

VAT is {{tax.vat.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{tax.vat.AE}} in the UAE and {{tax.vat.EG}} in Egypt.

The fee is taken from the top-up, so the amount added to your balance is slightly less than the amount charged to your card. The app shows both before you confirm.

Your own bank may charge for the bank transfer. That's set by them, not by Sahla Pay.
