If you're unhappy with our service, tell us. Complaints are handled by a separate team from day-to-day support.

## Make a complaint

To raise a complaint:

1. Go to **Help** > **Make a complaint**.
2. Explain what happened and what you'd like us to do.
3. Attach any screenshots or documents that help.
4. Tap **Submit**.

You can also make a complaint by phone. Ask the agent to log it as a formal complaint.

## What happens next

We confirm we've received it within {{complaint.acknowledge.ALL}} and give you a reference number. You'll get our final response within:

| Country | Final response within |
|---|---|
| Saudi Arabia | {{complaint.response.SA}} |
| UAE | {{complaint.response.AE}} |
| Egypt | {{complaint.response.EG}} |

## If you're not satisfied

If you disagree with our final response, or haven't had one in time, you can take your complaint to the central bank's consumer protection department in your country. Our final response explains how.

A complaint doesn't replace a dispute. If you want money back for a transaction, open a dispute first, within the deadline; a complaint about the same transaction doesn't stop the dispute deadline from running.
