We update our terms of use and privacy notice from time to time, for example when we add new features or when the law changes.

## How will I know?

We'll tell you at least {{legal.terms_notice.ALL}} before a change takes effect, by email and in the app. The message summarizes what's changing, and the new version is available in **Profile** > **Legal**.

## Do I have to accept?

If you keep using your account after the change takes effect, you're treated as having accepted it. If you don't agree, you can close your account for free before that date. See [Closing your account](kb-0193).

## Fee changes

Changes to fees follow the same notice period; see [Fee changes and notice period](kb-0146).

## Where can I see older versions?

Previous versions of our terms are listed in **Profile** > **Legal** > **Previous versions**, with the dates they applied.

Changes that are only in your favour, such as a new free service, and changes required immediately by law or a regulator, can apply without notice.
