Sending money to someone with a Sahla Pay account in the same country is free and arrives instantly, any day of the week.

## Send money

To send:

1. Tap **Send** > **To a Sahla Pay user**.
2. Pick a contact, or enter their phone number.
3. Check the name that appears. It's the name on their verified account.
4. Enter the amount and, if you like, a note.
5. Tap **Send** and confirm with your fingerprint, face or passcode.

## What if they don't have Sahla Pay yet?

You can still send to their phone number. They'll get an SMS inviting them to join. If they don't open an account and claim it, the money comes back to you; see [Unclaimed transfers](kb-0067).

## Can I send to a Sahla Pay user in another country?

No. Transfers between users only work within the same country. To send to someone abroad, use an international transfer to their bank account; see [Sending money abroad](kb-0036).

## Limits

Transfers to other users count towards your daily and monthly limits. See [Daily and monthly transfer limits](kb-0034).
