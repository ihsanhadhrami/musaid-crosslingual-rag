A virtual card is a card that lives in the app. It has its own number, expiry date and CVV, and spends from your balance like a physical card.

## How do I get one?

In Saudi Arabia and the UAE, every verified account gets a virtual card automatically. You'll find it under **Cards**. In Egypt, tap **Cards** > **Get a virtual card**, because the card has a one-time fee and we don't create it until you ask.

## Can I have more than one?

Yes, up to {{card.max_virtual.ALL}} virtual cards. Some people keep one just for subscriptions, so they can freeze it without affecting their other payments. In Egypt, the one-time fee applies to each extra card.

## Where can I use it?

Online, in apps, and in shops through Apple Pay, Google Pay or Samsung Pay, where available in your country. You can't use it at ATMs or for chip-and-PIN payments, because there's no physical chip.

## Can I delete a virtual card?

Yes: **Card settings** > **Close card**. Subscriptions on that card will stop working.
