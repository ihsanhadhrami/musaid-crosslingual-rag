You can have your salary paid straight into your Sahla Pay balance, as long as your employer pays by bank transfer.

## What to give your employer

Your Sahla Pay IBAN and the name on your account. You'll find both in **Add money** > **Bank transfer**. Tap **Share** to send them as a PDF, which many HR departments ask for. You can also download an account confirmation letter from **Profile** > **Statements** > **Account letter**.

## When will it arrive?

Salary payments arrive like any bank transfer: {{topup.bank_transfer_time.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{topup.bank_transfer_time.AE}} in the UAE and {{topup.bank_transfer_time.EG}} in Egypt, after your employer's bank sends it.

## Will my salary count towards my limits?

Receiving money doesn't count towards your sending limits. It does count towards your balance limit, which is {{limits.balance_max_verified.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{limits.balance_max_verified.AE}} in the UAE and {{limits.balance_max_verified.EG}} in Egypt for verified accounts. If a salary payment would take you over it, it's returned to your employer, so move money out regularly if your balance is high.

Your account must be verified to receive a salary. Unverified accounts can only receive {{limits.receive_monthly_unverified.SA}} a month in Saudi Arabia, {{limits.receive_monthly_unverified.AE}} in the UAE and {{limits.receive_monthly_unverified.EG}} in Egypt, which a salary usually exceeds.

## Salaries paid through the UAE Wage Protection System

In the UAE, many employers pay salaries through the Wage Protection System (WPS), which only pays into accounts with banks and exchange houses registered as WPS agents. Sahla Pay isn't a WPS agent, so if your employer pays through WPS, your salary must go to a bank account first. You can then move it to Sahla Pay by bank transfer, which is free.

## Do you ask about salary payments?

Regular salary payments from an employer are normal and expected. If your salary is high, you may be asked once to confirm your employer and occupation, as part of our source of funds checks.
