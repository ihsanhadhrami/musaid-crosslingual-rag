If we couldn't accept your ID, the app shows the reason under **Profile** > **Verify identity**. These are the most common ones.

## "Image unclear"

Glare, blur or a cut-off corner. Scan again on a dark surface, away from direct light, with the whole card inside the frame. Don't use the flash.

## "Document not accepted"

You uploaded a document we can't use for your country, for example a driving licence or a passport for an account in Saudi Arabia. Check the list for your country and use one of those.

## "Document expired"

The ID must be valid on the day you submit it. Renew it first, then verify.

## "Details don't match"

The name or date of birth in your profile is different from your ID. You can correct small spelling differences yourself before submitting. A different name entirely means the account was opened with someone else's details, and that can't be fixed by editing.

## Rejected more than once

You can try again straight away after most rejections. If your ID was rejected because it appears to be damaged or altered, the retry button is disabled. You'll need to contact support and may be asked to show the original card on a video call before you can try again.
