No. Each person can have one Sahla Pay account, linked to one ID.

## What if I opened a second account by mistake?

If you open a new account with the same ID, verification of the second account will fail. Use your original account instead. If you can't log in to it, use **Forgot password** on the login screen rather than signing up again.

If both accounts have money in them, contact us. We'll close the newer one and move its balance to your original account.

## Can I have one account for personal use and one for savings?

Not as separate accounts. You can use **Pockets** to set money aside inside your account. Money in a pocket isn't spent by card payments and transfers until you move it back.

## Can I open an account for my child or my parents?

Not in your name. Each person must open their own account, with their own ID and phone number. In Egypt, a parent can approve an account opened by a child aged {{kyc.min_age.EG}} or over; see [Accounts for minors and guardian consent](kb-0019).
