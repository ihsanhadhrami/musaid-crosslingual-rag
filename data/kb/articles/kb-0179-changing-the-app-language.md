The app is available in Arabic and English.

## Change the language

To switch:

1. Go to **Profile** > **Settings** > **Language**.
2. Choose **العربية** or **English**.

The app restarts in the new language. In Arabic, the layout switches to right-to-left.

## What changes?

All app screens, notifications and in-app messages. Emails and SMS messages follow the same language from the next message on.

## What doesn't change?

- Merchant names in **Activity**, which appear as the merchant sends them
- Your name on your card, which is always in English letters
- Documents and statements you already downloaded

## Can I talk to support in Arabic?

Yes. Chat and phone support are available in Arabic and English, whatever your app language.
