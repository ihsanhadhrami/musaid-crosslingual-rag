Sometimes a merchant refunds only part of a payment, for example when you return one item from an order.

## How do partial refunds show up?

As a separate refund line in **Activity**, for the amount the merchant refunded. The original payment stays as it was.

## I got less back than I expected

Check with the merchant first. Common reasons:

- They kept a delivery or restocking fee.
- Only some of the items were returned.
- The payment was in a foreign currency and the exchange rate has changed since you paid.

## Can I get several partial refunds for one payment?

Yes. A merchant can send more than one refund for the same payment, up to the original amount. Each one arrives separately.

## The merchant refunded too little and won't fix it

You can dispute the difference if you can show the merchant agreed to a full refund, or that what they kept goes against their own refund policy.
