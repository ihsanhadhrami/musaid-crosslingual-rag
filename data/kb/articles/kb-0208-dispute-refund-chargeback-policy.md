This policy explains how Sahla Pay handles refunds, disputes about transfers and card payments, and chargebacks. It supplements clause 12 of the Terms of Use. Where this policy refers to days, it means calendar days unless it says business days.

## 1. Scope

This policy covers:

- refunds issued by merchants for card and QR payments;
- payments that failed but were deducted from your balance;
- transfers sent to the wrong recipient;
- card payments you dispute because of a problem with the merchant;
- ATM withdrawals where the cash was not dispensed.

Payments you did not authorize are handled under the Unauthorized Transactions and Liability Policy, which gives you stronger protection. If you are unsure which applies, report the problem from the transaction in the app and we will choose the right process.

## 2. Refunds from merchants

### 2.1 Who issues refunds

Only the merchant can issue a refund for a card or QR payment. You should first ask the merchant. We cannot oblige a merchant to accept a return that its own policy does not allow, but if a merchant breaks its own policy or a written promise, you may dispute the payment.

### 2.2 Refund times

| Country | Card refund credited within, after the merchant issues it |
|---|---|
| Saudi Arabia | {{refund.merchant_card.SA}} |
| UAE | {{refund.merchant_card.AE}} |
| Egypt | {{refund.merchant_card.EG}} |

QR refunds are credited immediately when the merchant sends them. Refunds are credited to your balance, including when the card used for the payment has expired, been replaced or been closed.

### 2.3 Currency

Refunds of payments made in a foreign currency are converted on the date the refund is processed, using our exchange rate including the markup. You may receive more or less than the amount originally debited, and we do not compensate exchange rate differences.

## 3. Failed payments

If a payment fails but the amount is deducted from your balance, it is reversed automatically within {{failed_payment.reversal.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{failed_payment.reversal.AE}} in the UAE and {{failed_payment.reversal.EG}} in Egypt. You do not need to report it unless that time has passed.

## 4. Transfers to the wrong recipient

### 4.1 Before opening a dispute

You may cancel a pending transfer within {{transfer.cancel_window.ALL}} of confirming it. After that, you should first ask the recipient to return the money if you know them.

### 4.2 Deadlines and process

You may open a wrong-transfer dispute within {{dispute.filing_window.SA}} of the transfer in Saudi Arabia, {{dispute.filing_window.AE}} in the UAE and {{dispute.filing_window.EG}} in Egypt. We will contact the recipient and, for bank transfers, the receiving bank. We aim to finish our investigation within {{dispute.investigation.SA}}, {{dispute.investigation.AE}} and {{dispute.investigation.EG}} respectively.

### 4.3 Outcome

If the dispute is approved, the recoverable amount is returned to your balance within {{refund.wrong_transfer.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{refund.wrong_transfer.AE}} in the UAE and {{refund.wrong_transfer.EG}} in Egypt after the decision. We can only recover money still held by the recipient. Where part of the money has already been spent or withdrawn, we return what we can recover and close the case. No temporary credit is given for wrong-transfer disputes.

### 4.4 International transfers

International transfers can only be recalled through the receiving bank, which decides whether to return the money. A recall fee applies, as set out in the Fees and Charges Schedule, and is not refunded if the recall fails.

## 5. Card payment disputes

### 5.1 Reasons for a dispute

You may dispute a card payment if you were charged more than once or charged the wrong amount, if goods or services were not provided, if goods were significantly not as described, or if a merchant did not process a refund it agreed to. You should contact the merchant before opening a dispute and keep a record of your contact.

### 5.2 Deadlines

The dispute must be opened within the period in clause 4.2, counted from the transaction date. For goods or services due to be delivered at a later date, the period is counted from the expected delivery date, but a dispute for non-delivery may only be opened {{dispute.goods_wait.ALL}} after that date.

### 5.3 Temporary credit

If a card dispute is still open {{dispute.temporary_credit_after.ALL}} after you submitted it, we usually credit the amount to your balance temporarily. If the dispute is decided in the merchant's favour or you cancel it, we will deduct the temporary credit.

### 5.4 Chargebacks

Where a card dispute qualifies under the card network's rules, we raise a chargeback with the merchant's bank. The merchant may accept it or provide evidence in response. The complete process can take up to {{chargeback.max_resolution.ALL}}. Chargebacks are not available for QR payments, for transfers, or for payments to gambling or cryptocurrency merchants; disputes involving those merchants are reviewed only as possible fraud.

### 5.5 Payments you approved

A payment that you approved yourself, including with a one-time password or in-app approval, is not reversed only because you later regret it. If the merchant provided what was agreed, the dispute will be rejected.

## 6. ATM withdrawals

If an ATM debited your balance but did not dispense cash, or dispensed less than requested, report it from the withdrawal in the app. We will check the ATM records with the bank that operates it and resolve the case within {{atm.dispense_resolution.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{atm.dispense_resolution.AE}} in the UAE and {{atm.dispense_resolution.EG}} in Egypt. Our ATM fee is refunded with the amount. Cases involving ATMs abroad are handled through the card network and may take up to {{chargeback.max_resolution.ALL}}.

## 7. Evidence and cooperation

We may ask you for evidence, such as receipts, messages with the merchant, tracking information or photographs. If you don't respond to our request within the time stated in it, we will decide on the information we have. Submitting false information in a dispute is a breach of the Terms of Use and may lead to the closure of your account.

## 8. Decisions and appeals

We will tell you the outcome of your dispute and the reasons for it. If you have new information, you may appeal once within {{dispute.appeal_window.ALL}} of the decision. An appeal is reviewed by a different member of our team. If you remain unhappy, you may make a complaint under our Complaints Handling Policy.

## 9. Disputes and account closure

You cannot close your account while a dispute is open. Refunds received after your account is closed are forwarded to the bank account you nominated, within {{refund.after_closure.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{refund.after_closure.AE}} in the UAE and {{refund.after_closure.EG}} in Egypt.

## 10. Fees

Refunds, disputes, chargebacks and appeals are free of charge, whatever the outcome. The only related fee is the recall fee for international transfers.
