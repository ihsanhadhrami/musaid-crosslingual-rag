This policy explains when an account becomes dormant, what happens to it, and how money in inactive or closed accounts is protected.

## 1. When an account becomes dormant

An account becomes dormant if the customer doesn't initiate any transaction for the following period:

| Country | Period without customer activity |
|---|---|
| Saudi Arabia | {{account.dormant_after.SA}} |
| UAE | {{account.dormant_after.AE}} |
| Egypt | {{account.dormant_after.EG}} |

Customer activity means a transaction you make yourself, such as a top-up, transfer, card payment or bill payment. Money you receive, refunds and fees we charge are not customer activity. Logging in to the app without making a transaction is not activity either.

## 2. Before an account becomes dormant

We will send you at least two reminders, by email and in the app, before your account becomes dormant, explaining how to keep it active.

## 3. Effects of dormancy

While an account is dormant:

- your card is suspended and is not renewed when it expires;
- you can still log in, view your balance and receive money;
- a monthly dormancy fee applies: {{fees.dormancy_monthly.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{fees.dormancy_monthly.AE}} in the UAE and {{fees.dormancy_monthly.EG}} in Egypt.

The dormancy fee is only taken from your balance. It is never charged when your balance is zero, and it never reduces your balance below zero. No debt is created.

## 4. Reactivating an account

You can reactivate a dormant account by making any transaction yourself. If your identity document has expired, you'll need to upload a valid one first. Dormancy fees stop from the following month. Fees already charged are not refunded.

## 5. Closure of dormant accounts

If a dormant account has a zero balance for a further {{account.dormant_closure.ALL}}, we may close it, after notifying you. A dormant account with money in it is not closed only because it is dormant.

## 6. Unclaimed funds

Money in a dormant account remains yours. Where the law of your country requires, after a long period without contact we may transfer the balance to the authority responsible for unclaimed funds, after trying to contact you through all the details we hold. You or your heirs can claim the money from us, or from that authority, at any time.

## 7. Refunds and payments after closure

If money is received for an account that has been closed, we forward it to the bank account nominated at closure. If that is not possible, for example because the bank account has also been closed, we hold the money as unclaimed funds under section 6.

## 8. Deceased customers

If we learn that a customer has died, the account is frozen and is not treated as dormant. The balance is paid to the heirs once they have provided the documents we require.
