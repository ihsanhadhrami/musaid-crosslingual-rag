Quick answers to the questions we get most about verifying your account.

## Why do I have to verify?

It's a legal requirement for payment accounts, and it protects you: it's much harder for someone to open an account in your name.

## Is verification free?

Yes, always.

## How long does it take?

Usually a few minutes. Manual reviews take up to {{kyc.verification_time.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{kyc.verification_time.AE}} in the UAE and {{kyc.verification_time.EG}} in Egypt.

## What changes after I verify?

You can send more, hold more and get a card. For example, in Saudi Arabia your daily sending limit goes from {{limits.daily_transfer_unverified.SA}} to {{limits.daily_transfer_verified.SA}}. In the UAE it goes from {{limits.daily_transfer_unverified.AE}} to {{limits.daily_transfer_verified.AE}}, and in Egypt from {{limits.daily_transfer_unverified.EG}} to {{limits.daily_transfer_verified.EG}}.

## Can I verify with someone else's ID?

No. The ID has to belong to you, and the selfie has to match it. Accounts verified with another person's ID are closed.

## Is my ID safe with you?

Your documents are encrypted and only used for verification and legal checks. We never share them with merchants or other users.

## I'm verified but the app still asks me to verify

You may have been asked to re-verify. See [Why we ask you to verify again](kb-0022).
