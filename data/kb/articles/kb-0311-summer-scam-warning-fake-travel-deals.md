Every summer, we see more scams involving holidays. Watch out for these.

## Fake holiday rentals

A beautiful apartment at a low price, advertised on social media. The "owner" asks for a deposit by bank transfer. The apartment doesn't exist, or belongs to someone else.

## Fake airline and Umrah packages

Websites and WhatsApp accounts offering cheap flights or Umrah packages, asking for payment to a personal account.

## Fake visa services

Messages offering fast visas for a fee, sometimes using real government logos.

## Protect yourself

- Pay by card, on the company's official website, where you can. Card payments can be disputed; bank transfers to a scammer usually can't be recovered.
- Never pay an individual for a package advertised as coming from a company.
- Check reviews outside the site itself.

If you've paid a scammer, report it straight away. See [Investment scams and fake sellers](kb-0164).
