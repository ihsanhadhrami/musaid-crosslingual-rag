We know many of our customers have family in Sudan and Yemen. We're sorry to say that international transfers to both countries are still unavailable, because of the situation there and the restrictions our partner banks apply.

| Yemen | {{corridor.available_YE.SA}} | {{corridor.available_YE.AE}} |

We review this regularly, and we'll announce any change here.

## What we can't help with

We can't recommend other money transfer services, and we can't send money to a bank in another country on the recipient's behalf.

## Avoid scams

Be careful with people offering to move money to Sudan or Yemen for you through your Sahla Pay account. Moving money for other people can break our terms and the law. See [Restricted countries and transactions](kb-0200).
