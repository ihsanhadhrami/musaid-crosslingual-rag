This is a plain-language summary of the main points in our terms of use. The full terms, which you accepted when you opened your account, are in **Profile** > **Legal** > **Terms of use**. If this summary and the full terms differ, the full terms apply.

## Your account

- Sahla Pay is a personal e-wallet, for individuals only. One account per person.
- Your account belongs to the country where you opened it, and that country's rules apply.
- Your balance isn't a bank deposit and doesn't earn interest.

## Your responsibilities

- Keep your password, PIN and codes secret, and tell us straight away if you think someone else knows them.
- Keep your details, ID and phone number up to date.
- Don't use your account for business, illegal activity or on behalf of someone else.

## Our responsibilities

- Carry out your payments correctly and on time.
- Protect your money and data.
- Refund unauthorized transactions according to [Transactions I didn't make](kb-0090).

## Ending the agreement

You can close your account at any time, for free. We can close it too, in the situations described in [When Sahla Pay closes an account](kb-0195).

## Changes

We can change the terms with {{legal.terms_notice.ALL}} notice. See [Changes to our terms](kb-0198).
