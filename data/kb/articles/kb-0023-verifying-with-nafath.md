If your account is registered in Saudi Arabia, you can verify your identity through Nafath instead of scanning your ID. It's usually the fastest option.

## Before you start

- Install the Nafath app and make sure you can log in to it.
- Your Sahla Pay profile must have the same national ID or Iqama number as your Nafath account.

## Verify with Nafath

To verify:

1. Go to **Profile** > **Verify identity** > **Verify with Nafath**.
2. Enter your national ID or Iqama number.
3. The Sahla Pay app shows a number. Open Nafath and tap the same number in the request.
4. Return to Sahla Pay. You'll see **Verified** within a minute or two.

## Didn't work?

If the Nafath request expires, start again from step 1. You'll get a new number.

Nafath confirms who you are, but we still need a selfie the first time you verify, to make sure it's you holding the phone. You'll be asked for it straight after the Nafath step. If you skip the selfie, your account stays unverified.
