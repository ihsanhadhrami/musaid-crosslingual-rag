## Are the limits different for unverified accounts?

No. The same QR limits apply, but your balance limit is lower, so in practice you can't spend more than you hold.

## The payment is above the limit

Split it into two QR payments if the shop agrees, or pay by card.

## Do QR payments count towards my transfer limits?

No. They only count towards the QR limits above.
