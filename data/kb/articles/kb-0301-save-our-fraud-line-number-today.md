If your card is stolen or you think someone is in your account, the fastest thing you can do is freeze your card in the app. The second is to call our fraud line, which is open {{support.fraud_line_hours.ALL}} in all three countries.

## Where to find the number

- In the app: **Help** > **Call us**.
- On the back of your physical card.

Save it in your contacts now, as "Sahla Pay fraud". If your phone is stolen too, you'll need to call from someone else's phone, so write the number down somewhere safe at home as well.

## When to call

- Your card is lost or stolen and you can't freeze it in the app.
- You see a payment you didn't make.
- You've told someone a code or your password.

See [Reporting fraud](kb-0156).
