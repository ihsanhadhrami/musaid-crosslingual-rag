The quick version of everything about refunds.

## Who issues refunds for card payments?

The merchant. Ask them first. We can only step in with a dispute if they won't help.

## How long until a refund shows up?

After the merchant sends it: up to {{refund.merchant_card.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{refund.merchant_card.AE}} in the UAE and {{refund.merchant_card.EG}} in Egypt.

## Do refunds go back to my card or my balance?

Your balance. Your card spends from your balance, so it's the same thing.

## I paid by QR. How do I get a refund?

Ask the shop. QR refunds arrive instantly once the shop sends them.

## A payment failed but I was charged

That's not a refund; it's reversed automatically within {{failed_payment.reversal.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{failed_payment.reversal.AE}} in the UAE and {{failed_payment.reversal.EG}} in Egypt.

## Do you charge for refunds?

No. See [Are there fees for refunds?](kb-0100).

## How long do I have to dispute a payment?

{{dispute.filing_window.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{dispute.filing_window.AE}} in the UAE and {{dispute.filing_window.EG}} in Egypt, counted from the transaction date.
