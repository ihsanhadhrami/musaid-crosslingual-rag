## Is there a limit on how much I can receive?

For verified accounts, there's no separate receiving limit. The only limit is your maximum balance: {{limits.balance_max_verified.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{limits.balance_max_verified.AE}} in the UAE and {{limits.balance_max_verified.EG}} in Egypt.

For unverified accounts, you can receive up to {{limits.receive_monthly_unverified.SA}} per calendar month in Saudi Arabia, {{limits.receive_monthly_unverified.AE}} in the UAE and {{limits.receive_monthly_unverified.EG}} in Egypt, as well as the lower balance limit.

## What happens if a payment would go over the limit?

It's declined and returned to the sender in full. We don't accept part of it.

## Will you ask about large amounts?

Possibly. If you receive more than {{kyc.source_of_funds_threshold.SA}} in a month in Saudi Arabia, {{kyc.source_of_funds_threshold.AE}} in the UAE or {{kyc.source_of_funds_threshold.EG}} in Egypt, we may ask where the money comes from. See [Source of funds questions](kb-0025).

Refunds aren't counted towards the monthly receiving limit for unverified accounts, and they're credited even if you've reached it.
