## Will Sahla Pay ever call me and ask for a code?

Never. Anyone asking for your OTP, PIN or password is a scammer.

## How long is a code valid?

{{security.otp_expiry.ALL}}.

## What happens if I enter the wrong password too often?

After {{security.max_login_attempts.ALL}} wrong attempts, login is locked for {{security.lockout_duration.ALL}}.

## How many devices can I use?

Up to {{security.trusted_devices_max.ALL}} trusted devices.

## Is the fraud line open at night?

Yes, {{support.fraud_line_hours.ALL}}, in Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Egypt.

## What if someone takes money from my account?

Report it straight away. If you report an unauthorized transaction within {{fraud.full_protection_window.ALL}} and didn't share your details, we refund it in full.

## Why can't I send money after getting a new phone?

For {{security.new_device_hold.ALL}} after adding a new device, sending to new recipients and abroad is paused. This protects you if someone else set up your account on their phone.
