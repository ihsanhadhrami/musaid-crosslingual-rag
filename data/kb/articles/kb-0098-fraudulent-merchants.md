Some online shops exist only to take payments and never deliver. If you think you've paid one, here's how we handle it.

## Signs of a fake shop

- Prices far below everywhere else, especially for phones, perfume and electronics.
- Only social media pages, no proper website or contact details.
- Asks you to pay by transfer to a personal account instead of by card.
- The shop disappears or blocks you after you pay.

## I paid a fake shop by card

Open a dispute from the payment as **Goods not received**. Card payments are protected by the card network rules, so you have a good chance of getting your money back. See [Goods or services not received](kb-0099).

## I paid by transfer

Transfers to a person, rather than card payments, aren't covered by chargeback rules. Report it as fraud as soon as possible; we'll try to freeze the money in the recipient's account. See [Reporting fraud](kb-0156).

## What happens to the merchant?

When we see several reports about the same merchant, we block card payments to them for all our customers and report them to the card network.
