Words and abbreviations you'll see in the app and in our help articles. Each entry links the idea to how it works in Sahla Pay; for the full rules, see the help article on that topic.

## A

**Account tier:** The level of your account, Basic or Verified, which sets your balance and transfer limits. Verifying your identity moves you to Verified.

**Activity:** The list of all your payments, transfers, top-ups, refunds and fees in the app.

**ARN (acquirer reference number):** A reference the merchant's bank gives a card refund. If a refund is late, the ARN lets us trace it through the card network.

**ATM withdrawal limit:** The most cash you can take out with your card in one day. It's separate from your card spending limit.

**Authorization:** The moment a merchant asks for approval of a card payment. The money is held at this point and paid to the merchant later.

## B

**Balance limit:** The most money your account can hold, including money in Pockets. A top-up or incoming transfer that would take you over it is declined.

**Beneficiary:** Another word for the recipient of a transfer. In the app we call them recipients.

**Bill payment:** Paying a utility, telecom, government or school bill from **Payments** > **Bills**.

**Business day:** A working day in your account's country. Business days are different in Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Egypt, and public holidays don't count.

## C

**Cashback:** Money we add to your balance as part of an offer. It's taken back if the payment that earned it is refunded.

**Chargeback:** A card payment reversed through the card network after a dispute. We raise it with the merchant's bank on your behalf.

**Contactless:** Paying by holding your card or phone near the card machine. Small card payments don't need a PIN.

**Cut-off time:** The time of day after which a bank transfer is treated as sent on the next business day.

**CVV:** The three-digit security code on your card, also shown in **Cards** > **Show details**. Never share it by phone or message.

## D

**Daily limit:** The most you can send in one day. It resets at midnight in your account's country.

**Dispute:** A request for us to investigate a payment, for example a wrong transfer or goods that never arrived.

**Dormant account:** An account with no transactions you've made yourself for a long period. Dormant accounts may pay a monthly fee in some countries.

**Dynamic currency conversion:** When a shop or ATM abroad offers to charge you in your own currency. The rate is usually worse than ours, so choose the local currency.

## E

**Eidiya:** Money given as a gift at Eid, often to children. You can send it to several people at once in the app.

**Exchange rate markup:** The amount we add to the reference exchange rate when converting currency. It's included in the rate the app shows.

**Expired ID:** An identity document past its expiry date. After a grace period, payments from your account are paused until you upload a valid one.

## F

**Fee schedule:** The list of all our fees for your country. The current one replaced the 2025 schedule on 1 March 2026.

**Freeze:** Temporarily stopping your card from working. You can unfreeze it yourself at any time.

**Fraud line:** Our phone line for lost cards and suspected fraud, open at all hours in all three countries.

## G

**Gross negligence:** Being very careless with your security details, for example telling someone your one-time password. It can mean we can't refund an unauthorized payment.

**Guardian:** In Egypt, a parent or legal guardian who approves a young person's account and can set limits on it.

## H

**Hold:** Money a merchant reserves on your card before charging the final amount, common at hotels, car rental companies and petrol stations.

## I

**IBAN:** Your international bank account number. Your Sahla Pay IBAN lets people send money to you by bank transfer.

**InstaPay:** Egypt's instant payment network. Customers in Egypt can send money to InstaPay addresses from the app.

**Iqama:** The residence permit for foreign residents of Saudi Arabia, accepted for verification.

**International payments:** A card setting that allows payments abroad and online payments in foreign currencies.

## K

**KYC (know your customer):** The identity checks we're required to do before you can use all our services.

## M

**Merchant:** A shop, website or service provider you pay by card or QR code.

**Monthly limit:** The most you can send in a calendar month. It resets on the first day of each month.

## N

**Nafath:** Saudi Arabia's national digital identity app, which you can use to verify your Sahla Pay account.

## O

**OTP (one-time password):** A short code we send to confirm it's you. It expires quickly and works only once. Nobody from Sahla Pay will ever ask for it.

## P

**Payment link:** A link you share so someone can pay you by debit card, even if they don't use Sahla Pay.

**Payment request:** Asking another Sahla Pay user for money in the app. They can pay it with one tap.

**Pending:** A payment or transfer that has started but isn't complete yet.

**Pockets:** Separate spaces in your account for setting money aside. Card payments and transfers use your main balance only.

## Q

**QR payment:** Paying in a shop by scanning the shop's QR code with the app. QR payments have their own limits.

## R

**Recall:** Asking the receiving bank to send back an international transfer. A fee applies, and the receiving bank decides.

**Recurring payment:** A card payment a merchant takes regularly, like a subscription.

**Reference rate:** The market exchange rate we start from before adding our markup.

## S

**SADAD:** Saudi Arabia's national bill payment system, for government and utility bills.

**Scheduled transfer:** A transfer you set up to be sent on a future date, once or on a repeating basis.

**SIM swap:** When your phone number is moved to a new SIM. We pause some transfers for a while after one, in case it wasn't you.

**Source of funds:** Where the money in your account comes from. We sometimes ask about it, as the law requires.

**Superseded:** Replaced by a newer version. Older fee schedules and terms stay available for reference, but no longer apply.

## T

**Temporary limit increase:** A higher daily transfer limit for a short time, for a large one-off payment. Available in Saudi Arabia and the UAE.

**3-D Secure:** An extra check for online card payments, where you approve the payment in the app.

**Trusted device:** A phone or tablet where you've logged in and confirmed it's you.

## U

**UAE Pass:** The UAE's national digital identity, which you can use to verify your Sahla Pay account.

**Unauthorized transaction:** A payment you didn't make or allow. It's different from a payment you made yourself after being tricked.

**Unverified account:** An account that hasn't completed identity verification. It has lower limits and can't use cards or bank transfers.

## V

**VAT:** Value added tax, charged on our fees at the rate of your account's country.

**Virtual card:** A card that exists only in the app, for online payments and mobile wallets.

## W

**Wage Protection System (WPS):** The UAE system for paying salaries through approved banks and exchange houses. WPS salaries can't be paid directly into Sahla Pay.
