This policy explains what happens when a payment is made from your account without your permission, who bears the loss, and what you must do. It supplements clause 11 of the Terms of Use.

## 1. What is an unauthorized transaction

A transaction is unauthorized if it was made from your account or with your card without your consent, for example by someone who stole your card or phone, obtained your card details, or took over your account.

A transaction is **authorized**, even if you were deceived, if you approved it yourself: by confirming it in the app, entering your PIN, approving it with biometrics, or entering or sharing a one-time password. Payments made because of a scam, such as a fake seller or a fraudster pretending to be from a bank, are usually authorized transactions. They are covered by our fraud reporting process, not by this policy.

## 2. What you must do

As soon as you notice a transaction you didn't authorize, you must:

1. freeze your card in the app;
2. change your password;
3. report the transaction from **Activity**, or call our fraud line, which is open {{support.fraud_line_hours.ALL}}.

## 3. Refunds of unauthorized transactions

If you report an unauthorized transaction within {{fraud.full_protection_window.ALL}} of becoming aware of it, we will refund it in full once we have confirmed it was unauthorized, together with any fees charged on it.

We may investigate before refunding. If we have reasonable grounds to suspect fraud on your part, we may delay the refund and report the matter to the authorities.

## 4. Your liability

### 4.1 Late reporting

If you report an unauthorized transaction later than the period in section 3, your liability for losses before you reported it is limited to:

You have no liability for transactions made after you have reported your card or account as compromised.

### 4.2 Fraud and gross negligence

You are liable for all losses if you acted fraudulently, or if you intentionally or with gross negligence failed to keep your security details safe. Examples of gross negligence include sharing a one-time password with anyone, writing your PIN on your card, keeping your password with your phone, or giving another person access to your app.

### 4.3 Very late reporting

We can't refund unauthorized transactions that you report more than {{fraud.max_report_age.ALL}} after the transaction date, except where the law of your country provides otherwise.

## 5. Our investigation

We will investigate every report. We may ask you for information, for example about how your card or details could have been obtained, and we may ask you to report the matter to the police. If our investigation shows that a transaction we refunded was in fact authorized by you, we may deduct the refund from your balance and will tell you why.

## 6. Account security after a report

After a report, we may block the affected card, log out all devices, and ask you to verify your identity again before you can use your account. We may also apply the security holds described in the Terms of Use, such as the block on new recipients after a change of device.
