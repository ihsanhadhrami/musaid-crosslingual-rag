If you selected the wrong contact or entered an incorrect phone number, the available recovery options depend on the transfer status.

## If the transfer is pending

Go to **Activity** and open the transfer. When the status is **Pending** and the transfer was sent less than {{transfer.cancel_window.ALL}} ago, select **Cancel transfer**. The full amount is returned to your balance immediately.

Cancellation is unavailable after the time window expires or after the transfer changes to **Completed**.

## If the transfer is completed

We cannot remove funds from the recipient's account without their consent or an investigation. Take these steps:

1. If you know the recipient, ask them to return the money. This is usually the quickest option.
2. If you do not know the recipient or they refuse, open **Activity**, select the transfer, and choose **Report a problem** > **I sent it to the wrong person**.
3. Include any information that may help, such as the phone number or contact you intended to use.

We will contact the recipient and investigate the transfer. For investigation times and the refund process, see [Disputing a wrong transfer](kb-0075).

## If the money went to a bank account

A mistaken bank transfer uses the same dispute process. However, we must request the return through the receiving bank, which makes the final decision. These cases generally take longer than transfers between Sahla Pay users.
