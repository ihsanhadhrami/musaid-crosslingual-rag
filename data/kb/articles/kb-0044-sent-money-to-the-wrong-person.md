It happens: a wrong digit in a phone number, or the wrong contact picked from a list. What you can do depends on whether the transfer has finished.

## If the transfer is still pending

Open the transfer in **Activity**. If it shows **Pending** and you sent it less than {{transfer.cancel_window.ALL}} ago, you'll see a **Cancel transfer** button. Cancelling returns the full amount to your balance straight away. After that window, or once the status is **Completed**, it can't be cancelled.

## If the transfer is completed

We can't take money back from someone's account without their agreement or an investigation. Here's what to do:

1. If you know the person, ask them to send it back. This is the fastest way.
2. If you don't know them, or they won't return it, open a dispute: tap the transfer in **Activity** > **Report a problem** > **I sent it to the wrong person**.
3. Add any details that help, such as the number you meant to send to.

We'll contact the recipient and look into it. How long it takes, and when you'll get your money if the dispute succeeds, is explained in [Disputing a wrong transfer](kb-0075).

## Sent to a bank account?

Transfers to a wrong bank account follow the same dispute process, but we have to ask the receiving bank to return the money, and that bank decides. It usually takes longer than a transfer between two Sahla Pay users.
