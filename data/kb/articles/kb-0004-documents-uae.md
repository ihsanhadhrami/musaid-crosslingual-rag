For an account registered in the UAE, you'll need {{kyc.id_document.AE}} (both sides) and a selfie taken in the app.

## I've applied for my Emirates ID but don't have the card yet

You'll have to wait for the card. An application receipt or a visa page can't be used instead.

## Is UAE Pass accepted?

Yes, and it's the quickest way: sign in with UAE Pass and we'll get your verified details directly. See [Verifying with UAE Pass](kb-0024).

## Do I need proof of address?

Usually not. Most customers are verified with their Emirates ID alone.

We do ask for a proof of address if the address on your Emirates ID record doesn't match the one you entered, or as part of a source of funds check (see [Source of funds questions](kb-0025)). A utility bill, a tenancy contract (Ejari) or a bank statement is accepted if it's dated within the last {{kyc.address_proof_max_age.AE}}. See [Proof of address: when it's needed](kb-0018).

## Does my Emirates ID need to be valid?

Yes. If it expires after you've verified, you have {{kyc.expired_id_grace.AE}} to upload the renewed one before your account is restricted. See [Updating an expired Emirates ID](kb-0012).
