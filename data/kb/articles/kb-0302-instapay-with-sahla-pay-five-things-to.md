Since we added InstaPay, it has become one of the most popular ways to send money from Sahla Pay in Egypt. Here's what customers ask us most.

## 1. It's for sending

You can send to any InstaPay address or to the mobile number linked to it. To receive money from InstaPay users, share your Sahla Pay IBAN instead.

## 2. Check the name

The app shows the recipient's name before you send. If it isn't who you expect, stop.

## 3. There's a limit per transfer

Each InstaPay transfer can be up to {{limits.instapay_per_transfer.EG}}, and it counts towards your daily limit.

## 4. It's usually instant

Most transfers arrive in seconds, including on Fridays and holidays.

## 5. A completed transfer can't be cancelled

If you sent money to the wrong person, see [I sent money to the wrong person](kb-0044).

Full details are in [Sending to InstaPay addresses](kb-0059).
