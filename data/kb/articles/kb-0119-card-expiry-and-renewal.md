Physical cards are valid for {{card.validity.ALL}}. The expiry date is printed on the card and shown under **Cards** > **Show details**.

## Will I get a new card automatically?

Yes. We send your new card about {{card.renewal_sent_before.ALL}} before your current one expires, to the address in your profile. Renewal cards are free.

## Do I need to activate it?

Yes, in the app, like any new card. Your old card keeps working until it expires or until you activate the new one, whichever comes first.

## Will my card number change?

The number stays the same, but the expiry date and CVV change. Update your card details with online shops and subscriptions after you activate the new card.

## Virtual cards

Virtual cards are renewed in the app automatically. The new expiry date and CVV appear on the card when the old one expires.

If your account is dormant, or your ID has expired, we don't send a renewal card. Make your account active or update your ID first, and then order a renewal from **Cards**. See [Dormant account fees](kb-0137).
