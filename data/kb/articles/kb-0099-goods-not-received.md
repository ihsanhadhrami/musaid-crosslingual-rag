You paid by card but your order never arrived, or the service never happened.

## Before you dispute

- Check the tracking link and your spam folder for delivery updates.
- Contact the merchant and ask them to deliver or refund. Keep their reply.
- Wait until the expected delivery date has passed.

## Open the dispute

To dispute it:

1. Tap the payment in **Activity** > **Report a problem** > **I didn't receive it**.
2. Add the expected delivery date and your messages with the merchant.
3. Tap **Submit**.

## Deadlines

The usual dispute deadline is {{dispute.filing_window.SA}} from the transaction in Saudi Arabia, {{dispute.filing_window.AE}} in the UAE and {{dispute.filing_window.EG}} in Egypt. See [Dispute deadlines](kb-0077).

For goods that are delivered late, for example a pre-order or furniture with a long delivery time, the deadline is counted from the expected delivery date instead of the payment date. But you need to wait at least {{dispute.goods_wait.ALL}} after that date before opening the dispute, to give the merchant a chance to deliver.
