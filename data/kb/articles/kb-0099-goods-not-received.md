Use this process when you paid by card but the goods were never delivered or the service you paid for was not provided.

## Check these things first

- Review the tracking link and your spam folder for delivery messages or updated dates.
- Contact the merchant and ask them either to complete the order or issue a refund. Keep their response and any other evidence.
- Wait until the promised delivery or service date has passed before opening a dispute.

## Submit the dispute

1. Open the payment in **Activity** and select **Report a problem** > **I didn't receive it**.
2. Enter the expected delivery or service date and attach your messages with the merchant.
3. Select **Submit**.

## Filing deadlines

The standard deadline is {{dispute.filing_window.SA}} from the transaction in Saudi Arabia, {{dispute.filing_window.AE}} in the UAE, and {{dispute.filing_window.EG}} in Egypt. See [Dispute deadlines](kb-0077).

There is a different rule for goods that were expected to arrive late, such as pre-orders or furniture with a long stated delivery period. In those cases, the deadline is measured from the expected delivery date rather than the payment date. You must still wait at least {{dispute.goods_wait.ALL}} after that expected date before submitting the dispute, giving the merchant an additional opportunity to deliver.
