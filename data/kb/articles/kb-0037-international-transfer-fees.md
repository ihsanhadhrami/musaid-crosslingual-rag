This is what you pay when you send money to a bank account abroad from an account registered in Saudi Arabia or the UAE.

| | Saudi Arabia | UAE |
|---|---|---|
| Transfer fee | {{fees.intl_transfer_pct.SA}} of the amount sent | {{fees.intl_transfer_pct.AE}} of the amount sent |
| Minimum fee per transfer | {{fees.intl_transfer_min.SA}} | {{fees.intl_transfer_min.AE}} |
| Exchange rate markup | {{fees.fx_markup.SA}} | {{fees.fx_markup.AE}} |

The fee is calculated on the amount you send, in your own currency, and is taken from your balance on top of that amount. The app shows the fee and the exact amount the recipient will get before you confirm.

The exchange rate markup is not a separate charge. It's included in the rate we show you, which is the reference rate plus the markup.

VAT is added to the transfer fee: {{tax.vat.SA}} in Saudi Arabia and {{tax.vat.AE}} in the UAE. It isn't added to the exchange rate markup.

The minimum fee applies to every transfer, so splitting one large transfer into several small ones usually costs more in total. The recipient's bank or an intermediary bank may also take its own fee. We don't control or receive those fees, and they can reduce the amount that arrives.
