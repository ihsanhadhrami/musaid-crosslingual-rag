The charges below apply when you send money from a Saudi Arabian or UAE-registered Sahla Pay account to a bank account in another country.

| | Saudi Arabia | UAE |
|---|---|---|
| Transfer fee | {{fees.intl_transfer_pct.SA}} of the amount sent | {{fees.intl_transfer_pct.AE}} of the amount sent |
| Minimum fee per transfer | {{fees.intl_transfer_min.SA}} | {{fees.intl_transfer_min.AE}} |
| Exchange rate markup | {{fees.fx_markup.SA}} | {{fees.fx_markup.AE}} |

Your transfer fee is calculated from the amount sent in your account currency and deducted from your balance separately from that amount. Before you confirm, the app displays the fee and the exact amount the recipient should receive.

The exchange rate markup is included in the rate displayed by the app. It is not charged as a separate fee; the displayed rate combines the reference rate and the applicable markup.

VAT applies to the transfer fee at {{tax.vat.SA}} in Saudi Arabia and {{tax.vat.AE}} in the UAE. The exchange rate markup is not subject to VAT.

The minimum fee is charged for each transfer. Splitting one large transfer into multiple smaller payments may therefore cost more overall. The recipient's bank or an intermediary bank may also deduct its own charge. These fees are outside our control and can reduce the amount delivered.
