If you haven't verified your identity yet, you can still send money to other Sahla Pay users, with a lower limit.

## How much can I send?

Per day: {{limits.daily_transfer_unverified.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{limits.daily_transfer_unverified.AE}} in the UAE and {{limits.daily_transfer_unverified.EG}} in Egypt.

## Can I send to a bank account?

No. Bank transfers and international transfers need a verified account.

## How much can I keep in my balance?

Up to {{limits.balance_max_unverified.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{limits.balance_max_unverified.AE}} in the UAE or {{limits.balance_max_unverified.EG}} in Egypt.

## How do I get higher limits?

Verify your identity. It usually takes a few minutes. See [How to verify your identity in the app](kb-0006).
