Version 5 brings a new look to the whole app.

## New

- **A refreshed design**, with clearer colours and bigger buttons.
- **Filters in Activity.** Show only card payments, transfers, top-ups or refunds.
- **Progress bars for Pockets** with a target amount.

## What hasn't changed

Your balance, cards, recipients and settings are all where they were. If you can't find something, search for it in **Help**.

## Fixes

- Statements requested by email sometimes arrived without the attachment.
