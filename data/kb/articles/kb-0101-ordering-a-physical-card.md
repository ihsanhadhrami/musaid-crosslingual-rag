Every verified account gets a virtual card for online payments. If you also want a plastic card for shops and ATMs, you can order one in the app.

## Before you start

- Your account must be verified.
- Your delivery address must be in the same country your account is registered in.
- You need enough balance to cover the card fee.

| Country | Physical card fee |
|---|---|
| Saudi Arabia | {{fees.physical_card_issuance.SA}} |
| UAE | {{fees.physical_card_issuance.AE}} |
| Egypt | {{fees.physical_card_issuance.EG}} |

The fee is charged once, when you order. VAT is included.

## Order your card

To order a card:

1. Go to **Cards** and tap **Get a physical card**.
2. Check your name as it will be printed. It comes from your ID and can't be edited here.
3. Confirm or change your delivery address.
4. Tap **Order card** and confirm the fee.

## What happens next

We'll print and send your card, and you can track it under **Cards** > **Track delivery**. Delivery times for each country are in [Card delivery times](kb-0102). When it arrives, activate it in the app before using it; see [Activating your card](kb-0103).
