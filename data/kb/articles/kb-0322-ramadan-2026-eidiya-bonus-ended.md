**This offer ended on 22 March 2026.**

## The offer

Send Eidiya to at least five different people between 15 February and 22 March 2026, and we added {{promo.eidiya2026_bonus.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{promo.eidiya2026_bonus.AE}} in the UAE or {{promo.eidiya2026_bonus.EG}} in Egypt to your balance.

## Terms

- Each transfer had to be at least {{promo.eidiya2026_min.SA}}, {{promo.eidiya2026_min.AE}} or {{promo.eidiya2026_min.EG}}, sent to another Sahla Pay user.
- The five people had to be different people. Several transfers to the same person counted once.
- Transfers to bank accounts and international transfers didn't count.
- One bonus per customer.

You could send to several people at once with **Add more people**. See [Ramadan and Eid: sending Eidiya and managing busy days](kb-0249).
