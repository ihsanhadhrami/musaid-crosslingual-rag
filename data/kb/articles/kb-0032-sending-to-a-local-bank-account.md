You can send money from your balance to any bank account in the country your Sahla Pay account is registered in.

## Before you start

- Your account must be verified.
- You'll need the recipient's IBAN and their full name as their bank has it.

## Send to a bank account

To send:

1. Tap **Send** > **To a bank account**.
2. Enter the IBAN, or choose a saved recipient.
3. Enter the recipient's name and the amount.
4. Check the fee and tap **Confirm**.
5. Enter the code we send to your phone if it's a new recipient.

## The name doesn't match

In Saudi Arabia and Egypt, the recipient's bank checks the name before accepting the transfer. If the name you entered is very different, the transfer is returned to your balance within a few minutes, with no fee charged. In the UAE the check happens later, so a returned transfer can take up to {{failed_payment.reversal.AE}} to come back.
