Ramadan Kareem. Eid is coming, so this update makes Eidiya easier.

## New

- **Send to several people at once.** In **Send** > **To a Sahla Pay user**, tap **Add more people** to send the same amount, or a different amount, to each person in one go. Each person gets their own transfer and their own notification. See [Ramadan and Eid: sending Eidiya and managing busy days](kb-0249).

Every transfer counts towards your daily limit separately, so check your limit before sending to the whole family.

## Fixes

- Notes in Arabic were sometimes shown in reverse order on receipts.
