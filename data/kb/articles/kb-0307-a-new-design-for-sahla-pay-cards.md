Physical cards issued from February 2026 have a new design: a darker green, with your name and the card number on the back for privacy.

## Do I need a new card?

No. Your current card keeps working until its expiry date. When it's renewed, you'll get the new design automatically, at no cost.

## Can I order one now?

Only if you need a replacement anyway, for example because your card is damaged. Replacement fees apply as usual. There's still a single design for physical cards; virtual cards come in several colours. See [Name and design on your card](kb-0129).

## Card number on the back

Online and on the phone, you'll find your card number and CVV in **Cards** > **Show details** as before.
