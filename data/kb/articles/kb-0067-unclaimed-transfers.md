When you send money to a phone number that isn't registered with Sahla Pay, the recipient gets an SMS with a link to join and claim it.

## How long do they have?

{{transfer.unclaimed_return.ALL}} from the day you sent it. If they open an account with that phone number within that time, the money appears in their balance as soon as they finish signing up.

## What if they don't claim it?

The transfer is cancelled, and the full amount comes back to your balance automatically. You'll get a notification.

## Can I cancel it myself?

Yes, at any time before they claim it. Open the transfer in **Activity** and tap **Cancel transfer**.

## Does it count towards my limits?

Yes, from the moment you send it. If it's cancelled or returned, the amount is added back to your limit.
