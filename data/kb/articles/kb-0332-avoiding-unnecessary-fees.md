Most of what you do with Sahla Pay is free. When fees do apply, there's often a free alternative.

## Adding money

Topping up by bank transfer to your Sahla Pay IBAN is free. Topping up with a debit card is quicker, but has a fee. If you top up regularly, a monthly bank transfer from your salary account saves money. See [Top-up fees](kb-0055).

## Cash

- In Saudi Arabia, your first few ATM withdrawals each month are free at Saudi ATMs. Plan larger withdrawals rather than many small ones.
- Abroad, every withdrawal has a fee, and some ATM operators add their own. Pay by card instead of carrying a lot of cash.

## Paying abroad

When a shop or ATM abroad offers to charge you in your own currency, say no and pay in the local currency. The shop's rate is almost always worse than ours.

## Cards

Virtual cards are free. You only need a physical card for shops that don't take phone payments and for ATMs. Look after it: replacements for lost or damaged cards have a fee.

## Keeping your account active

In some countries, accounts that haven't been used for a long time become dormant and pay a monthly fee. Making any payment yourself keeps your account active. See [Dormant accounts](kb-0194).

## Checking what you've paid

Every fee appears in **Activity**. If you don't understand a fee, see [Why was I charged a fee?](kb-0138), and check the fee schedule for your country.
