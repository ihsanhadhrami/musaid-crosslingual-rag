If your account is restricted, the banner in the app tells you what we need. Most restrictions are lifted once you've completed those steps.

## Lift a restriction

To get it removed:

1. Tap the banner on the home screen, or go to **Help** > **My cases**.
2. Complete what's asked. This could be a selfie, your ID, answers about recent transactions, or documents about the source of your money.
3. Tap **Submit**.

## How long does the review take?

Once we have everything, we review it within {{security.restriction_review.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{security.restriction_review.AE}} in the UAE and {{security.restriction_review.EG}} in Egypt.

## Can I speed it up?

Sending complete, clear documents the first time is the fastest way. Calling us doesn't speed up the review.

## Restricted after login lockouts?

If your account was restricted after being locked several times, support has to unlock it after checking your identity. See [Account locked after failed login attempts](kb-0154).

If we decide to close the account instead of lifting the restriction, we'll tell you, and your balance will be paid out to a bank account in your name. See [When Sahla Pay closes an account](kb-0195).
