Many shops and cafés accept Sahla Pay by QR code. You pay straight from your balance, without your card.

## Pay with a QR code

To pay:

1. Tap **Scan** on the home screen.
2. Point your camera at the shop's QR code.
3. Check the shop name and enter the amount, if it isn't filled in already.
4. Tap **Pay** and confirm with your fingerprint, face or passcode.

The shop sees the payment instantly. Show them the green confirmation screen if they ask.

QR payments don't count towards your transfer limits.

## Is there a fee?

No, QR payments are free for you.

## The shop's code won't scan

Make sure your camera has permission and the code isn't damaged. Some shops show a code on their card machine screen; tap **Scan** and hold steady. If a payment went wrong, see [Refunds for QR payments](kb-0084).
