If a payment screen keeps spinning, or a payment shows **Processing** in **Activity**, don't try again straight away. You could end up paying twice.

## The screen froze while paying

Close the app and open it again. Check **Activity**:

- **Completed**: the payment went through. Don't pay again.
- **Failed**: nothing was taken. You can try again.
- **Processing**: wait. Most payments finish within a few minutes.

## Still processing after an hour

The other side hasn't confirmed yet, often a bank or a biller. If it ends up failing, the money comes back within {{failed_payment.reversal.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{failed_payment.reversal.AE}} in the UAE and {{failed_payment.reversal.EG}} in Egypt. See [A failed payment was deducted from my balance](kb-0042).

## Bill payments

Bills can show **Processing** until they're posted to the company. See [Paying bills](kb-0048).
