Most transfers can't be cancelled once they've been sent, because they arrive within seconds or minutes.

## When can I cancel?

You can cancel a transfer while it shows **Pending**, for up to {{transfer.cancel_window.ALL}} after you sent it. Open it in **Activity** and tap **Cancel transfer**. The full amount, including any fee, goes back to your balance straight away.

## Transfers to someone who hasn't joined yet

If you sent money to a phone number that isn't on Sahla Pay, the transfer stays **Waiting for recipient** until they join. You can cancel it at any time before they claim it.

## International transfers

These can only be cancelled while the status is **Under review**. Once it shows **Sent to bank**, the money has left Sahla Pay.

## Scheduled and recurring transfers

These can be cancelled or edited until the day before they're due. On the day itself, they're sent at {{transfer.scheduled_run_time.ALL}} and can no longer be changed. See [Scheduled and recurring transfers](kb-0051).

If you sent money to the wrong person and it's too late to cancel, see [I sent money to the wrong person](kb-0044).
