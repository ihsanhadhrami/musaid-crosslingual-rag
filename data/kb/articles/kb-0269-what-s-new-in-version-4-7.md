## New

- **Monthly tax invoices.** Download a tax invoice for the fees you paid in any month from **Profile** > **Statements** > **Tax invoice**. See [VAT on fees](kb-0143).

## For customers in Egypt

Guardians can now set a lower daily sending limit for a young family member's account, and freeze or unfreeze their card from the guardian's own app. See [Helping a young family member in Egypt manage money](kb-0258).

## Fixes

- CSV statements opened with the wrong characters in some spreadsheet apps. They now use UTF-8.
