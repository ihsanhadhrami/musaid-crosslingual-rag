These Card Terms and Conditions apply to Sahla Pay physical and virtual cards from 1 January 2025. They form part of your agreement with us together with the Terms of Use. If these card terms and the Terms of Use conflict on a matter concerning your card, these card terms apply.

## 1. Your card

### 1.1 What your card is

Your Sahla Pay card is a prepaid payment card linked to your Sahla Pay balance. Every payment you make with the card is deducted from your balance. The card is not a credit card or a debit card linked to a bank account, and you cannot spend more than your available balance.

### 1.2 Who can have a card

Cards are issued only to holders of verified accounts. The card is issued in your name, as it appears on your verified identity document, and may only be used by you. You must not let anyone else use your card, your card details or a mobile wallet in which your card is stored.

### 1.3 Physical and virtual cards

You may hold one physical card and up to {{card.max_virtual.ALL}} virtual cards at the same time. In Saudi Arabia and the UAE, a virtual card is issued automatically when your account is verified. In Egypt, a virtual card is issued only when you request one, because a fee applies to each virtual card.

### 1.4 Validity

Physical cards are valid for {{card.validity.ALL}} from the date of issue, until the last day of the month shown on the card. We will send you a renewal card around {{card.renewal_sent_before.ALL}} before your card expires, unless your account is dormant, restricted, or your identity document has expired.

## 2. Receiving and activating your card

### 2.1 Delivery

We deliver physical cards by courier to an address in your country only. Delivery takes up to {{card.delivery.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{card.delivery.AE}} in the UAE and {{card.delivery.EG}} in Egypt, from the business day after you order it. The courier will hand the card only to you, on presentation of your identity document.

### 2.2 Cards that do not arrive

If your card has not arrived {{card.not_arrived_wait.ALL}} after the expected delivery date, you should report it in the app, and we will cancel it and send a new card free of charge. If the courier's records show that the card was delivered and signed for, we will treat the card as possibly stolen and the replacement fee in clause 7 applies, unless the courier confirms a delivery error.

### 2.3 Activation

You must activate a physical card in the app before using it. Cards that are not activated within {{card.activation_deadline.ALL}} of being sent are cancelled. The first payment with a new or replacement card must be made using the chip and your PIN; contactless payments are available after that.

## 3. Using your card

### 3.1 Where you can use it

You can use your card wherever the card network's logo is displayed, in shops, online, in apps and at ATMs, in your country and abroad, subject to the restrictions in clause 3.4. Virtual cards cannot be used at ATMs or for payments that need a chip.

### 3.2 Authorizing a payment

You authorize a card payment by inserting the card and entering your PIN, by tapping the card or a device for a contactless payment, by giving your card details for an online payment and approving it in the app where we ask you to, or by setting up a recurring payment with a merchant. Once authorized, a card payment cannot be stopped by us, but you may be able to dispute it as described in clause 9.

### 3.4 Restricted use

You must not use your card for anything illegal, for gambling where it is not permitted in your country, for buying cryptocurrency, or at merchants in countries subject to comprehensive international sanctions. We may decline card payments to categories of merchants that present a high risk of fraud, and we will tell you when a payment is declined for this reason.

### 3.5 Holds and pre-authorizations

Some merchants place a hold on part of your balance before charging you the final amount. Held amounts are not available to spend. A hold that is not used by the merchant is released within {{card.hold_release.ALL}}, except holds placed by hotels and car rental companies, which may last up to {{card.hold_release_extended.ALL}}. We cannot release a hold early without the merchant's agreement.

You may add your card to Apple Pay, Google Pay or Samsung Pay where available. Apple Pay is {{wallet.apple_pay.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{wallet.apple_pay.AE}} in the UAE, and {{wallet.apple_pay.EG}} in Egypt. Samsung Pay is {{wallet.samsung_pay.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{wallet.samsung_pay.AE}} in the UAE and {{wallet.samsung_pay.EG}} in Egypt. Google Pay is available in all three countries. The wallet provider's own terms also apply. Anyone whose fingerprint or face is registered on your device may be able to approve payments with your card, and you are responsible for those payments.

## 5. Your PIN and card security

### 5.1 Your PIN

You set your PIN in the app. We never send PINs by post, SMS or email. If you enter an incorrect PIN {{card.pin_attempts.ALL}} times in a row, your PIN is blocked; you can unblock it in the app. If an ATM retains your card, you must freeze it in the app and order a replacement; the lost card fee does not apply in that case.

### 5.2 Keeping your card safe

You must keep your card and its details safe, and you must not write your PIN on the card or keep it with the card. If your card is lost or stolen, you must freeze it in the app or call us immediately on our fraud line, which is available {{support.fraud_line_hours.ALL}}.

### 5.3 Online payments

For many online payments, we will ask you to approve the payment in the app. You have {{card.3ds_approval_window.ALL}} to approve it, after which the payment is cancelled. A payment you approve in this way is treated as authorized by you.

## 6. Foreign currency

Card payments and ATM withdrawals in a currency other than your account's currency are converted at our exchange rate, which is a reference rate plus a markup of {{fees.fx_markup.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{fees.fx_markup.AE}} in the UAE and {{fees.fx_markup.EG}} in Egypt. The rate is applied on the date the card network settles the payment, which may be later than the date you made it. If a merchant or ATM abroad offers to convert the payment into your own currency, the merchant's or ATM operator's rate applies instead of ours, and we are not responsible for it.

## 8. Replacing, freezing and closing a card

### 8.1 Freezing your card

You can freeze and unfreeze your card in the app at any time. While your card is frozen, new card payments and ATM withdrawals are declined, including recurring payments. Refunds to the card are still credited to your balance.

### 8.2 Lost or stolen cards

When you report your card lost or stolen, we cancel it permanently. It cannot be reactivated, even if you find it.

### 8.3 Closing a card

You can close a card in the app at any time. Closing a card does not close your Sahla Pay account. Closing your account closes all your cards.

### 8.4 Replacement cards

A replacement card has a new card number, expiry date and security code. A renewal card has the same card number, but a new expiry date and security code. Your existing card keeps working until you activate the replacement or renewal card, unless it has been reported lost or stolen.

## 9. Disputes and refunds for card payments

Refunds from merchants are credited to your balance even if the card used for the payment has expired, been replaced or been closed. You may dispute a card payment within {{dispute.filing_window.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{dispute.filing_window.AE}} in the UAE and {{dispute.filing_window.EG}} in Egypt of the transaction date. For goods not delivered by an agreed later date, the period runs from the expected delivery date, but you must wait {{dispute.goods_wait.ALL}} after that date before opening a dispute. Where we raise a chargeback, the card network's rules apply, and the process may take up to {{chargeback.max_resolution.ALL}}. Chargebacks are not available for payments to gambling or cryptocurrency merchants.

We may change these card terms, including card fees and limits, by giving you at least {{legal.terms_notice.ALL}} notice, in the same way as described in the Terms of Use. Changes in your favour may take effect immediately.
