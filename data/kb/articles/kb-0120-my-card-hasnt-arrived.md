Physical cards usually arrive within {{card.delivery.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{card.delivery.AE}} in the UAE, and {{card.delivery.EG}} in Egypt. If delivery is late, check the tracking information first.

## View delivery status

Open **Cards** > **Track delivery**. The tracking page shows whether the courier is still carrying the card, attempted delivery, or sent it back to us.

## If delivery was unsuccessful

After two unsuccessful delivery attempts, the courier returns the card. When the status changes to **Returned**, select **Arrange delivery** to choose another delivery date or address.

## If the card is overdue

Allow {{card.not_arrived_wait.ALL}} after the expected delivery date for possible courier delays. If the card still has not arrived, select **Card not received** on the tracking page. We will cancel the undelivered card and issue a replacement.

A replacement for a card that never arrived is free.

If tracking says the card was delivered and signed for but you did not receive it, we treat it as potentially stolen. The card is cancelled and the replacement fee is {{fees.card_replacement.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{fees.card_replacement.AE}} in the UAE, or {{fees.card_replacement.EG}} in Egypt, unless the courier confirms that the delivery was recorded incorrectly. See [Card replacement fees](kb-0109).
