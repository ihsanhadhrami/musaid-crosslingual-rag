Cards are delivered within {{card.delivery.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{card.delivery.AE}} in the UAE and {{card.delivery.EG}} in Egypt. If yours hasn't arrived yet, check these first.

## Check the tracking

Go to **Cards** > **Track delivery**. The status shows whether the courier has your card, tried to deliver it, or returned it to us.

## The courier couldn't deliver

After two failed attempts, the card is returned to us. You'll see **Returned** in the tracking. Tap **Arrange delivery** to choose a new date or a different address.

## It's been longer than the delivery time

Wait {{card.not_arrived_wait.ALL}} after the expected delivery date, in case of delays. If it still hasn't come, tap **Card not received** in the tracking screen. We'll cancel that card and send a new one.

## Is there a fee for the new card?

No. A card that never arrived is replaced free of charge.

If the tracking shows the card was delivered and signed for, but you didn't receive it, it's treated as possibly stolen. The card is cancelled, and the replacement fee of {{fees.card_replacement.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{fees.card_replacement.AE}} in the UAE or {{fees.card_replacement.EG}} in Egypt applies, unless the courier confirms a delivery mistake. See [Card replacement fees](kb-0109).
