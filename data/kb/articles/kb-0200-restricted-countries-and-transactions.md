Some payments aren't allowed with Sahla Pay, because of international sanctions, local laws or a high risk of fraud.

## Restricted countries

We can't process international transfers to, or card payments in, countries under comprehensive international sanctions. The list changes, so we don't publish it here. If you try to make a payment to a restricted country, the app tells you before you confirm.

## Restricted transactions

You can't use Sahla Pay for:

- gambling and betting, where it's illegal in your country
- buying or selling cryptocurrency
- adult content
- weapons, drugs or other illegal goods
- paying for or receiving money on behalf of someone else, as a business

Card payments to these merchants are declined, and transfers that look like these activities may be held for review.

## What if I didn't know?

If a payment is blocked, nothing is taken from your balance. If we find your account has been used for restricted activities, we may restrict or close it.

Connecting through a VPN that shows a location in a restricted country also blocks your transactions, even if you're not there. See [Using Sahla Pay with a VPN](kb-0187).
