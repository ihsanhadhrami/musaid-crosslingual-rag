Schedule a transfer for a later date, or set one up to repeat every week or month, for rent, family support or savings.

## Set up a scheduled transfer

To schedule a transfer:

1. Start a transfer as usual, to a Sahla Pay user or a bank account.
2. Before confirming, tap **Send later**.
3. Pick a date, up to {{transfer.schedule_max_ahead.ALL}} ahead, and choose **Once**, **Weekly** or **Monthly**.
4. Tap **Schedule**.

Scheduled transfers are sent at {{transfer.scheduled_run_time.ALL}} on the chosen day. You'll find them under **Payments** > **Scheduled**.

## What if I don't have enough money?

We try again at noon and at 6:00 PM the same day. If there still isn't enough, that payment is skipped and you're notified. For recurring transfers, the next one still goes ahead as planned.

## Monthly transfers at the end of the month

If you choose the 29th, 30th or 31st, the transfer is sent on the last day of shorter months.

International transfers can't be scheduled. Scheduled transfers also count towards your limits on the day they're sent, not the day you set them up.
