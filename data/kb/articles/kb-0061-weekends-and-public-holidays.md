Transfers between Sahla Pay users, QR payments and card payments work every day, around the clock. Transfers that involve banks can depend on the day.

## Local bank transfers

| Country | Weekends and holidays |
|---|---|
| Saudi Arabia | Arrive {{transfer.local_bank_delivery.SA}} |
| UAE | Arrive on the next business day; normally {{transfer.local_bank_delivery.AE}} |
| Egypt | Arrive {{transfer.local_bank_delivery.EG}} |

Business days are {{calendar.business_days.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{calendar.business_days.AE}} in the UAE, and {{calendar.business_days.EG}} in Egypt.

## International transfers

These are processed on our business days only, and the recipient's bank may also be closed on its own weekend. A transfer sent on a Thursday evening from Saudi Arabia may only be processed on Sunday.

## Bills

Bills paid on a weekend are sent to the company on the next business day, and the posting time starts from then.

The instant transfers in Saudi Arabia and Egypt can occasionally be delayed during bank system maintenance, which is usually scheduled overnight before a public holiday. We announce planned maintenance in the app.
