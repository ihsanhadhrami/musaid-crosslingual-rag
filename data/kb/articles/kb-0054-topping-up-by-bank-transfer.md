Every Sahla Pay account has its own IBAN. Send money to it from any bank account in your country, and it lands in your balance. Bank transfer top-ups are free.

## Find your IBAN

Tap **Add money** > **Bank transfer**. You'll see your IBAN and the account name. Tap **Copy** or **Share**.

## Send the money

To top up from your bank:

1. Open your banking app and start a local transfer.
2. Paste your Sahla Pay IBAN as the recipient.
3. Use your own name as the recipient name.
4. Send the amount you want to add.

## Can someone else top up my account?

Yes. Anyone can send a bank transfer to your Sahla Pay IBAN. It shows in **Activity** with the sender's name. Large or frequent payments from other people may trigger a source of funds check.
