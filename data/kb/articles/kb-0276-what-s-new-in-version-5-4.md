## New

- **Account details as a PDF.** In **Add money** > **Bank transfer**, tap **Share** to get your IBAN and account name as a PDF, ready to send to your employer or a family member. See [Receiving your salary in Sahla Pay](kb-0252).

## Improvements

- Card top-ups show the fee before you confirm, on the same screen as the amount.

## Fixes

- In some cases, the **Cancel transfer** button stayed visible after the cancellation window had ended. Tapping it did nothing. It now disappears on time.
