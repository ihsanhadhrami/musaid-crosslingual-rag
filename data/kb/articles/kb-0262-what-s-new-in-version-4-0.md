Version 4.0 is a bigger update than usual. Update the app from the App Store or Google Play to get it.

## New

- **A new home screen.** Your balance, your Pockets and your most recent payments are now on one screen. Tap **Pockets** on the home screen to see what you've set aside.
- **Search in Activity.** Search your payments by name, amount or the note you added. Tap the magnifying glass at the top of **Activity**.

## Improvements

- The selfie check during verification is quicker on older phones.
- Several screens in Arabic now show amounts and dates in the right order.

## Fixes

- The notification badge sometimes stayed on after you'd read every message in your **Inbox**.
- Some Arabic names were cut off in PDF statements.
