## Security improvements

- **Trusted devices** now shows the phone model and when each device last logged in. Remove any you don't recognize. See [Trusted devices](kb-0160).
- Suspicious login alerts now include the device model, so it's easier to tell whether it was you.

## Fixes

- After changing your password, some customers were logged out of their other trusted devices twice.
- The OTP field now fills in automatically on more Android phones.
