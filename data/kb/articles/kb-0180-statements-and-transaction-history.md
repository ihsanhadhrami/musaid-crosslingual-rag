## How far back can I see my transactions?

The app shows your full history for the last {{account.statement_history.ALL}}, in **Activity**.

## Can I search my history?

Yes. Use the search bar in **Activity** to find a merchant, a person or an amount, or filter by date and type.

## What's in a statement?

Your opening and closing balance, every transaction with its date, amount and fee, and the account details banks or embassies usually ask for. Statements are available in Arabic and English.

## Can I use it as proof for a visa or rental?

Yes. Statements are stamped digitally and include a reference number that can be checked with us.

## I need older transactions

For anything older than {{account.statement_history.ALL}}, request a statement from **Help** > **Chat with us**. It's sent to your email within {{account.statement_request_time.ALL}}.

Pending transactions aren't included in statements until they're completed. If a statement shows a different balance from the app, check **Activity** for pending items. To download a statement, see [Downloading a statement](kb-0181).
