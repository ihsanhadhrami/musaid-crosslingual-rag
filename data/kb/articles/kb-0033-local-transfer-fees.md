Fees for sending money from your balance to a bank account in your own country.

| Country | Fee per transfer |
|---|---|
| Saudi Arabia | {{fees.local_transfer.SA}} |
| UAE | {{fees.local_transfer.AE}} |
| Egypt | {{fees.local_transfer.EG}} of the amount, minimum {{fees.local_transfer_min.EG}}, maximum {{fees.local_transfer_max.EG}} |

Transfers to other Sahla Pay users in the same country are always free.

VAT is added to the fee in the UAE ({{tax.vat.AE}}) and Egypt ({{tax.vat.EG}}). The fee is shown before you confirm, and it's taken from your balance on top of the amount you send.

If a transfer is returned by the recipient's bank, for example because the IBAN doesn't exist, we refund the fee together with the amount. We don't refund the fee if you ask the recipient's bank to send the money back after it has arrived, because that counts as a new transfer.
