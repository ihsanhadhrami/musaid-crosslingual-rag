When a transfer fails, the app shows a reason on the transfer in **Activity**. Here's what each one means.

## "Limit reached"

You've reached your daily or monthly limit. Try again after it resets, or see [Daily and monthly transfer limits](kb-0034).

## "Insufficient balance"

Your balance doesn't cover the amount plus the fee. Top up and try again.

## "Recipient can't receive this amount"

The recipient's account would go over its balance limit, usually because it isn't verified. Ask them to verify, or send a smaller amount.

## "Rejected by the recipient's bank"

The IBAN or name is wrong, or the bank account is closed. Check the details with the recipient.

## "Blocked for your security"

Our systems stopped the transfer because it looked unusual. Nothing has been sent. Check your notifications, because we may ask you to confirm it was you.

## Was the money taken?

A failed transfer shouldn't take money from your balance. If it did, see [A failed payment was deducted from my balance](kb-0042).
