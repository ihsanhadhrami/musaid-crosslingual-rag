If we decided against your dispute, the email we sent explains why. Here's what you can do next.

## Common reasons

- The merchant showed the goods were delivered or the service was provided.
- The dispute was opened after the deadline.
- The payment was approved by you, for example with a one-time password.
- You didn't contact the merchant first.

## Can I appeal?

Yes, if you have new information. Reply from the case in **Help** > **My cases** within {{dispute.appeal_window.ALL}} of the decision and add the new evidence. A different person on our team will review it.

## What counts as new information?

Things we didn't have the first time, such as a tracking page showing the parcel was returned, or the merchant's written promise of a refund.

## Still not happy?

You can make a formal complaint; see [Making a complaint](kb-0196).

Each dispute can be appealed only once. A second appeal with the same evidence is closed without a new review.
