Android users can pay with their phone using Google Pay or, on Samsung phones, Samsung Pay.

## Availability

Google Pay is available in all three countries. Samsung Pay is {{wallet.samsung_pay.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{wallet.samsung_pay.AE}} in the UAE, and {{wallet.samsung_pay.EG}} in Egypt.

## Add to Google Pay

To add your card:

1. Go to **Cards**, tap your card, then **Add to Google Wallet**.
2. Accept the terms and confirm with the code we send you.

Your phone needs NFC turned on and a screen lock set.

## Add to Samsung Pay

Open the Samsung Wallet app, tap **Add card**, and enter your card details from **Cards** > **Show details**. Confirm with the code we send.

## My phone says the card can't be added

Check that your card isn't frozen, that your app is up to date, and that your phone isn't rooted. Rooted or modified phones can't use mobile wallets.
