Family abroad sending you money? Whichever country it comes from, receiving it into your Sahla Pay balance is {{fees.intl_receive.ALL}} on our side.

## What the sender needs

Your full name as shown in the app, and your Sahla Pay IBAN from **Add money** > **Bank transfer**.

## Fees you might still see

The sender's bank, or a bank in the middle, may charge its own fee, which reduces the amount that arrives. If money arrives in a different currency, we convert it at our exchange rate, which includes a markup. See [Receiving money from abroad](kb-0040).
