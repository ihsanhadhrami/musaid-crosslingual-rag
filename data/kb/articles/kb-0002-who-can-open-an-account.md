Sahla Pay is available to individuals who live in Saudi Arabia, the UAE, or Egypt. Your account is tied to the country associated with the phone number you register, and you must hold valid identification for that country. The country setting cannot be changed later; if you move, you need to open a new account. See [Moving to another country](kb-0016).

## Identity documents

Account verification requires {{kyc.id_document.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{kyc.id_document.AE}} in the UAE, or {{kyc.id_document.EG}} in Egypt. We do not accept photocopies, images of another screen, or expired documents.

## Who is not eligible

The following cannot open a personal Sahla Pay account:

- Companies, retailers, charities, and other organizations. See [Business accounts vs personal accounts](kb-0020).
- Anyone who already has an account, because each person may have only one.
- Visitors using a tourist or visit visa.

An account created for another person, or verified with someone else's identity document, is closed when discovered. Any remaining balance is held until the actual owner has been identified.

In Egypt, customers who are at least {{kyc.min_age.EG}} but younger than {{kyc.guardian_consent_under.EG}} may open an account with additional restrictions. A parent or legal guardian must approve the account in the app before funds can be sent. Until approval is recorded, the customer can add money and receive funds, but outgoing transfers and card payments remain disabled. See [Accounts for minors and guardian consent](kb-0019).
