Sahla Pay accounts are personal accounts for individuals who live in Saudi Arabia, the UAE or Egypt. You open your account in the country where you hold a valid ID, and the app sets your country from the phone number you register with. You can't change it later without opening a new account (see [Moving to another country](kb-0016)).

## Identity documents

To verify your account you'll need {{kyc.id_document.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{kyc.id_document.AE}} in the UAE, or {{kyc.id_document.EG}} in Egypt. Photocopies, photos of a screen and expired documents are not accepted.

## Who can't open an account

- Companies, shops, charities and other organizations. See [Business accounts vs personal accounts](kb-0020).
- Anyone who already has a Sahla Pay account. It's one account per person.
- Visitors on tourist or visit visas.

Accounts opened for someone else, or opened with another person's ID, are closed when we find them, and any balance is held until the real owner is identified.

Customers in Egypt aged {{kyc.min_age.EG}} or over but under {{kyc.guardian_consent_under.EG}} can open an account, but a parent or legal guardian has to approve it in the app before money can be sent out. Until they approve, you can top up and receive money, but outgoing transfers and card payments stay blocked. More in [Accounts for minors and guardian consent](kb-0019).
