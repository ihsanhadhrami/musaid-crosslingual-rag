Every transfer in **Activity** has a status. Here's what each one means.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Pending | We've received your transfer and are processing it. It can still be cancelled. |
| Under review | Held for a security check before it's sent. |
| Waiting for recipient | Sent to a phone number that isn't on Sahla Pay yet. |
| Processing | Sent to the recipient's bank, which hasn't confirmed yet. |
| Sent to bank | An international transfer has left Sahla Pay. |
| Completed | The money has arrived. |
| Failed | Nothing was sent. The reason is shown on the transfer. |
| Returned | The recipient's bank sent the money back. |
| Cancelled | You cancelled it, or it expired before it was claimed. |

## How long can a transfer stay "Pending"?

Usually a few seconds. If it's still pending after an hour, contact us.

## Does "Completed" mean the recipient can see it?

For transfers to Sahla Pay users, yes. For bank transfers, it means the receiving bank has accepted it. Most banks show it in the recipient's account straight away, but some take longer.
