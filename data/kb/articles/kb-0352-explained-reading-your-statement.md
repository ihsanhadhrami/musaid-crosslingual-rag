Your statement lists everything that happened in your account over a period. Here's how to read it.

## The summary

At the top: your opening balance at the start of the period, money in, money out, and your closing balance at the end. Money in Pockets is included in both balances.

## Each line

Each line has a date, a description, an amount and your balance after it. The description depends on the type:

- **Card payments** show the merchant's name as the merchant sends it, which isn't always the shop's name. Online payments often show the company that runs the website.
- **Transfers** show the recipient's or sender's name and the reference.
- **Fees** have their own line, separate from the payment they belong to, with the name of the fee.
- **Refunds** and **cashback** are separate lines with a positive amount.

## Pending payments aren't included

A statement shows completed transactions only. A card payment that is still **Pending** in **Activity** appears on the statement once it's settled, which may be in the next period.

## Payments in other currencies

For card payments abroad and international transfers, the line shows the amount in your currency, and the original amount and exchange rate. The markup is included in the rate shown, so there's no separate line for it.

## Statement dates

Statements use the date a transaction was completed, which can be a day or two after you made a card payment. If you're looking for a payment near the start or end of a month, check the next or previous statement too.

## Formats

PDF statements are for printing and sharing; CSV statements are for spreadsheets. Both have the same transactions. See [Downloading a statement](kb-0181).
