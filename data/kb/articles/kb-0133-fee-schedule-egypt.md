All fees for personal accounts registered in Egypt. VAT at {{tax.vat.EG}} is added where marked.

## Transfers

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| To another Sahla Pay user | free |
| To a local bank account or InstaPay | {{fees.local_transfer.EG}}, minimum {{fees.local_transfer_min.EG}}, maximum {{fees.local_transfer_max.EG}} + VAT |
| Receiving money from abroad | {{fees.intl_receive.ALL}} |

## Adding money and payments

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| Top-up by debit card | {{fees.card_topup.EG}} + VAT |
| Top-up by bank transfer | free |
| Bill payments and mobile recharge | {{fees.bill_payment.EG}} + VAT |
| Payment links (charged to the seller) | {{fees.payment_link.EG}} |

## Cards

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| Virtual card | {{fees.virtual_card.EG}} (one-time, per card) |
| Physical card | {{fees.physical_card_issuance.EG}} (VAT included) |
| Replacement card | {{fees.card_replacement.EG}} (VAT included) |
| ATM withdrawal in Egypt | {{fees.atm_withdrawal.EG}} + VAT |
| ATM withdrawal abroad | {{fees.atm_abroad.EG}} + VAT |
| Card payments in a foreign currency | {{fees.fx_markup.EG}} (included in the rate) |

## Account

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| Dormant account | {{fees.dormancy_monthly.EG}} |

International transfers are {{transfer.intl_available.EG}} for accounts in Egypt, so there is no international transfer fee on this schedule.
