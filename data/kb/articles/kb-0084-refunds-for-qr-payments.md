QR payments go straight from your balance to the shop, through the QR payment network we're part of, so refunds work a little differently from card payments.

## How do I get a refund?

Ask the shop. They can refund you from their payment terminal or app, and the money comes back to your balance instantly, as soon as they send it.

## I paid the wrong amount

Show the shop your payment confirmation in **Activity**. They can refund the difference, or the whole payment so you can pay again.

## The shop won't refund me

Open the payment in **Activity** and tap **Report a problem**. Because QR payments don't go through the card network, there's no chargeback. We contact the shop directly and review the case ourselves, within the normal dispute deadlines for your country; see [Dispute deadlines](kb-0077).

If you scanned a fake QR code stuck over the shop's real one, the payment went to someone else, not the shop. Report it as fraud straight away; see [Reporting fraud](kb-0156).
