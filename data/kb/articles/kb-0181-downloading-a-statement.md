## Download a statement

To get a statement:

1. Go to **Profile** > **Statements**.
2. Choose a month, or pick a custom date range.
3. Choose the format: **PDF** for printing and sharing, or **CSV** for spreadsheets.
4. Choose the language: Arabic or English.
5. Tap **Download** or **Send to email**.

## Statements for closed accounts

You can't download statements after closing your account. Download them before you close it, or ask support afterwards.

## Tax invoices

Tax invoices for fees are separate from statements: **Profile** > **Statements** > **Tax invoice**.

## The file won't open

PDFs open in any PDF reader. CSV files open in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets. If Arabic text looks broken in a CSV, open it with the UTF-8 option in your spreadsheet app.
