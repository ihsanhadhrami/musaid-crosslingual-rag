We protect your account with encryption, fraud monitoring and checks on every new device. A few habits on your side make it much safer still.

## The basics

- Use a password of at least {{security.password_min_length.ALL}} characters that you don't use anywhere else.
- Turn on fingerprint or face login, so you don't type your password in public.
- Keep your phone's own screen lock on.
- Install app updates when they come out. They often include security fixes.

## Never share

Your password, PIN, one-time passwords and card CVV are for you only. Sahla Pay staff will never ask for them, by phone, chat, SMS, email or social media. See [We will never ask for your OTP](kb-0162).

## Watch out for

- Calls or messages saying your account is blocked and asking you to "verify" it
- Links in SMS messages that open a login page
- Sellers who want you to pay by transfer instead of by card

## Built-in protection

The app locks itself after {{security.app_auto_lock.ALL}} without use. Logins on new devices need a code and a selfie. See [Trusted devices](kb-0160).

If anything seems wrong, freeze your card in the app and contact us. See [Reporting fraud](kb-0156).
