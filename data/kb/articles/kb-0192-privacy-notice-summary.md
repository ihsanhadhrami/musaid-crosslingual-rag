A short summary of how we handle your personal data. The full privacy notice is in **Profile** > **Legal** > **Privacy notice**.

## What we collect

- Identity details: name, date of birth, nationality, ID number and photo, and your selfie
- Contact details: phone number, email, address
- Financial details: your transactions, balance, linked bank accounts and cards
- Device details: phone model, app version, IP address and, if you allow it, location

## Why we use it

To verify your identity, run your account and payments, prevent fraud and money laundering, meet our legal obligations, and improve the app. We don't sell your data.

## Who we share it with

Only those who need it to provide our service or because the law requires it: banks and card networks that process your payments, identity verification partners, regulators and authorities, and our service providers under strict contracts.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, and object to marketing. Some data we must keep by law, even if you ask us to delete it. See [How long we keep your data](kb-0197).

## Marketing

You can turn off marketing messages in **Profile** > **Notifications**. Service and security messages can't be turned off.
