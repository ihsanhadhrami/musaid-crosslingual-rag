Buying a car, paying a rental deposit or paying for a wedding hall usually means moving more money than usual in one go. Planning ahead avoids a payment being declined on the day.

## Check your limits early

Your daily and monthly transfer limits apply to large payments too. A payment that's above your daily limit will be declined, and a large payment can use up most of your monthly limit. Check your limits in **Profile** > **Limits**. See [Daily and monthly transfer limits](kb-0034).

## Temporary limit increase

In Saudi Arabia and the UAE, verified customers can ask for a temporary increase to the daily limit for a one-off payment, with a document showing what the payment is for. Requests are reviewed within {{limits.temp_increase_review.ALL}}, so apply a few days before you need it. Temporary increases are {{limits.temp_increase_available.EG}} for accounts registered in Egypt. See [Requesting a temporary limit increase](kb-0150).

## Check your balance limit

Before receiving a large amount, for example the money from selling your old car, check that it won't take you over your balance limit. An incoming transfer that would take you over the limit is returned to the sender. See [Account tiers and balance limits](kb-0028).

## Source of funds

Large or unusual payments may lead us to ask where the money came from. Keep documents ready, like a sale contract or a salary letter. Answering quickly means your payments aren't held for long. See [Source of funds questions](kb-0025).

## Protect yourself

- Pay a company, not an individual, when you're buying from a business.
- For second-hand cars, check the ownership documents before paying anything.
- Be very careful with requests for a deposit before you've seen the car or the property.

## Keep the evidence

Save the receipt, the contract and the transfer confirmation from **Activity**.
