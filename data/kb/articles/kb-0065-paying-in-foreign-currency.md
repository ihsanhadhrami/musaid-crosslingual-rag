You can use your Sahla Pay card in almost any currency, online or abroad.

## How much does it cost?

There's no separate fee for paying in another currency. The cost is in the exchange rate: our rate includes a markup of {{fees.fx_markup.SA}} for accounts in Saudi Arabia, {{fees.fx_markup.AE}} in the UAE and {{fees.fx_markup.EG}} in Egypt.

## Why is the amount different from the receipt?

The shop's receipt shows the foreign currency. The amount taken from your balance is converted at our rate when the payment completes, usually within a day or two.

## Can I see the rate before I pay?

Yes. Tap **Cards** > **Exchange rates** to see today's rate for a currency.

## A shop abroad asked if I want to pay in riyals, dirhams or pounds

Say no and pay in the local currency. Paying in your own currency means the shop converts it, usually at a worse rate than ours.
