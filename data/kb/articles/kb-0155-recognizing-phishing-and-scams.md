Scammers often pretend to be Sahla Pay, a bank, a delivery company or a government office. Their goal is to get your login details or one-time passwords, or to make you send money.

## Common tricks

- **"Your account will be suspended."** A message with a link to "verify" your account. The link opens a fake login page.
- **"Your parcel is waiting."** A delivery fee request with a payment link.
- **A call from "the fraud team".** The caller knows your name and asks you to read out a code "to cancel a suspicious payment". The code actually approves a payment.
- **Prize or refund messages.** You've "won" something or are owed a refund, and need to enter your card details.

## How to tell it's fake

- We never send links that ask you to log in.
- We never ask for your OTP, PIN, password or CVV.
- Official messages from us appear in the app's **Inbox** as well.

## What to do

Don't click, don't reply, don't call back on numbers in the message. Report it through **Help** > **Report fraud**. If you've already shared a code or details, freeze your card and change your password immediately; see [Reporting fraud](kb-0156).
