Good news: you don't need your old card for a refund to reach you.

## My card was replaced after I paid

Refunds follow your account, not the card. If the merchant refunds the old card number, the refund still lands in your balance.

## My card has expired

The same applies. Expired and renewed cards are linked to the same account, so the refund arrives as normal.

## I closed my card

Refunds to a closed card still arrive in your balance, as long as your Sahla Pay account is open.

## What if the merchant says the refund failed?

Some merchants check the card before refunding and stop if it's expired. Ask them to try again anyway, or to refund you another way, such as store credit or a bank transfer.

If you've closed your whole Sahla Pay account, not just the card, it works differently: the refund is forwarded to your nominated bank account. See [Refunds after account closure](kb-0088).
