Sahla Pay is required by law to know who our customers are, to understand how their accounts are used, and to report suspicious activity to the authorities. This policy explains, for customers, what that means in practice.

## 1. Why we do this

Anti-money-laundering and counter-terrorist-financing laws in Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Egypt apply to all payment and e-money providers. They require us to identify customers, monitor transactions and keep records. These requirements protect the financial system, and they also protect you, by making it harder for criminals to open accounts in your name or use your account.

## 2. Identifying you

### 2.1 Documents

To verify an account we require:

|---|---|---|
| Saudi Arabia | {{kyc.id_document.SA}} | not required |
| UAE | {{kyc.id_document.AE}} | only in some cases, dated within {{kyc.address_proof_max_age.AE}} |
| Egypt | {{kyc.id_document.EG}} | required, dated within {{kyc.address_proof_max_age.EG}} |

We also require a live selfie to confirm that the document belongs to the person opening the account. Digital identity services, Nafath in Saudi Arabia and UAE Pass in the UAE, may be used instead of scanning a document, but the selfie is still required.

### 2.2 Review times

Most verifications are completed automatically. Where a manual review is needed, it takes up to {{kyc.verification_time.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{kyc.verification_time.AE}} in the UAE and {{kyc.verification_time.EG}} in Egypt.

### 2.3 Accounts we cannot open

We do not open accounts for people who appear on sanctions lists, for people who are not resident in the country where they apply, for anyone acting on behalf of another person or a business, or where we cannot verify the information provided.

## 3. Understanding how you use your account

### 3.1 Questions at account opening

When you open an account, we ask about your occupation and the main purpose of the account. Your answers help us recognize activity that is normal for you.

### 3.2 Source of funds

When your account receives large amounts, or the pattern of your activity changes, we may ask where the money comes from. We normally ask when the money you receive in a calendar month exceeds {{kyc.source_of_funds_threshold.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{kyc.source_of_funds_threshold.AE}} in the UAE or {{kyc.source_of_funds_threshold.EG}} in Egypt, but we may ask at any amount. Typical evidence includes a salary certificate, a contract of sale or a bank statement.

### 3.3 Periodic review

We review customer information regularly and ask customers to re-verify at least every {{kyc.reverification_interval.ALL}}, and whenever their identity document expires.

## 4. Monitoring transactions

We monitor transactions automatically and, where needed, manually. We may hold a payment for review before processing it, ask you questions about it, or decline it. Common reasons include payments to many new recipients in a short time, payments that don't match the purpose you gave us, and payments connected to countries subject to sanctions.

Transfers held for review are usually reviewed within a few hours. The execution times in the Terms of Use start only after the review is complete.

## 5. What happens if you don't provide information

If you don't provide information we request by the date in our message, we will restrict outgoing payments from your account until you do. If you still don't provide it, or if the information doesn't satisfy our legal obligations, we may close your account with notice, and pay your balance to a bank account in your name.

## 6. Reporting to the authorities

Where we suspect money laundering, terrorist financing or another crime, the law requires us to report it to the financial intelligence unit in your country. The law also prohibits us from telling you that we have made a report, or that an investigation is taking place. In these situations, we may restrict your account without explaining why, and we may not be able to tell you when the restriction will end.

## 7. Record keeping

We keep identification documents, transaction records and records of our checks for {{data.retention_after_closure.SA}} after the account closes in Saudi Arabia, and {{data.retention_after_closure.AE}} in the UAE and {{data.retention_after_closure.EG}} in Egypt, or longer if an investigation requires it.

## 8. Your responsibilities

You must give us true and complete information, tell us when your details change, use your account only for yourself, and not receive or send money on behalf of someone else. Receiving money and passing it on for another person, even as a favour or for a fee, can involve you in money laundering, and is a reason for us to close your account.
