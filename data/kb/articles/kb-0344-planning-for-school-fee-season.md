For families, the start of the school year often brings the biggest bills of the year: tuition, books, uniforms, transport and activities. Planning early spreads the cost.

## Know the dates

Ask the school for the fee schedule as early as possible, including the deadlines for each instalment and any discount for paying early. Put the dates in your phone's calendar.

## Save across the year

Create a pocket called "School fees" and divide the total by the months left until the first payment. Move that amount in every month, on salary day.

## Choose the right way to pay

- **Card payment** through the school's portal is instant, but check your daily card spending limit first.
- **Bank transfer** to the school's account needs the student's name and ID number as the reference.
- **Bill payment**, if the school is listed under **Payments** > **Bills** > **Education**.

See [Paying school and university fees](kb-0251).

## Large payments and your limits

A full year's fees may be above your daily limit. Pay in instalments, pay over several days, or, in Saudi Arabia and the UAE, ask for a temporary limit increase. See [Getting ready for a large purchase](kb-0341).

## Watch for extra costs

Uniforms, books, transport and activities can add a lot to the bill. Ask other parents what they spent last year.

## Keep the receipts

Keep the school's receipts and your payment confirmations. If the school says a payment is missing, the reference number lets them find it.
