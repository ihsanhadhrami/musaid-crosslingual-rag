We're updating our Terms of Use, Card Terms and Conditions, and Fees and Charges Schedule. The changes take effect on **1 March 2026**.

## What's changing

- Higher daily transfer limits for verified accounts in Saudi Arabia.
- Lower international transfer fees from Saudi Arabia and the UAE.
- A cheaper card replacement fee in Saudi Arabia.
- Faster merchant refunds for accounts in the UAE.
- A new option to request a temporary daily limit increase, in Saudi Arabia and the UAE.

The new values are in the [Fees and Charges Schedule (2026)](kb-0206) and the [Terms of Use (2026 edition)](kb-0201).

## Do I need to do anything?

No. If you keep using Sahla Pay after 1 March 2026, the new terms apply. If you don't agree, you can close your account for free before then. See [Closing your account](kb-0193).

We give at least {{legal.terms_notice.ALL}} notice of changes like these. See [Changes to our terms](kb-0198).
