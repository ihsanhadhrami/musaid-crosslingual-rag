## Improvements

- **Holds show an expected release date.** Hotel, car rental and petrol station holds now show when we expect them to drop off, in **Activity**. See [Merchant holds and pre-authorizations](kb-0123).
- The international transfer screen shows the expected delivery time for your recipient's country before you confirm.

## Fixes

- Some customers abroad were asked for a code again for each card payment on the same day.
