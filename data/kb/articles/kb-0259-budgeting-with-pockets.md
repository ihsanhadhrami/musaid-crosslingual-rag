Pockets let you set money aside inside your Sahla Pay account, for a goal or a regular bill, so you don't spend it by accident.

## How Pockets work

- You can have up to {{account.max_pockets.ALL}} pockets, each with its own name and, if you like, a target amount.
- Money in a pocket stays in your account, but card payments, QR payments and transfers only use your main balance.
- You can move money between your main balance and a pocket at any time, instantly and for free.

## Create a pocket

To create one:

1. On the home screen, tap **Pockets** > **New pocket**.
2. Give it a name, such as "Rent", "Emergency" or "Umrah".
3. Set a target amount, if you want one.
4. Move money in now, or set up an automatic weekly or monthly transfer from your main balance.

## Ideas

- **Bills pocket:** move your rent and bills into a pocket on salary day, so the money is there when they're due.
- **Emergency pocket:** build up a few months of expenses.
- **Goals:** travel, a wedding, a new phone, school fees.

## Pockets and your limits

Money in pockets counts towards your balance limit. Your home screen shows your main balance only; tap **Pockets** to see the total.

Pockets don't earn interest or profit, like the rest of your balance. If your account becomes dormant, the money in your pockets is treated like the rest of your balance, and dormancy fees can be taken from it when your main balance is empty.
