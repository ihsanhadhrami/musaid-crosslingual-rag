Your Sahla Pay card works in shops, online and at ATMs worldwide, wherever the card network logo is shown.

## Do I need to tell you I'm travelling?

No. But check that **International payments** is turned on in **Cards** > **Card settings**. It's on by default.

## What does it cost?

- Card payments: no fee, but the exchange rate includes our markup of {{fees.fx_markup.SA}} (Saudi Arabia), {{fees.fx_markup.AE}} (UAE) or {{fees.fx_markup.EG}} (Egypt).
- ATM withdrawals abroad: {{fees.atm_abroad.SA}}, {{fees.atm_abroad.AE}} or {{fees.atm_abroad.EG}} per withdrawal, plus the markup. The ATM's owner may charge as well.

See [Currency conversion and exchange rates](kb-0064).

## Tips

- Always choose to pay in the local currency.
- Keep the app installed and your phone number active, because we may send you a code to approve a payment.
- Know how to freeze your card in the app, in case it's lost.

Some countries are blocked for card payments because of sanctions or high fraud. Payments there are always declined. See [Restricted countries and transactions](kb-0200).
