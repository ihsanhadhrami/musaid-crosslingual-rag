Your statement for 2025 will be ready to download from **1 January 2026**, in **Profile** > **Statements**. Choose **Custom dates** and select the whole year, or download each month separately.

Tax invoices for the fees you paid are available by month in **Profile** > **Statements** > **Tax invoice**.

Statements in the app cover the last {{account.statement_history.ALL}}. For older records, contact support from **Help**.
