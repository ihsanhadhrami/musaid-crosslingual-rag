**This offer ended on 31 August 2026.**

## The offer

From 1 July to 31 August 2026, card payments abroad earned {{promo.travel2026_pct.ALL}} cashback, up to {{promo.travel2026_cap.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{promo.travel2026_cap.AE}} in the UAE or {{promo.travel2026_cap.EG}} in Egypt.

## What counted

Payments in shops, restaurants and hotels outside your country, with your card, Apple Pay or Google Pay.

## What didn't count

- ATM withdrawals abroad.
- Online payments, even in a foreign currency, because we can't tell where you were.
- Hotel and car rental holds, until the final amount was charged.
- Payments where you chose to pay in your own currency instead of the local currency.
