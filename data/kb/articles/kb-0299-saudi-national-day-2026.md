Happy Saudi National Day. Wednesday 23 September 2026 is a public holiday in Saudi Arabia.

- Transfers between Sahla Pay users, card payments, QR payments and local bank transfers work as usual.
- International transfers sent on the holiday leave on the next business day.
- SADAD payments are accepted and reach the biller after the holiday.
