Sell something now and then, like baked goods, a used phone or tutoring? Create a payment link and share it. Anyone can pay it with a debit card, even without Sahla Pay.

## Create a link

To create a payment link:

1. Tap **Request** > **Payment link**.
2. Enter the amount and a description.
3. Tap **Create** and share the link by WhatsApp, SMS or social media.

Links expire after {{payment_link.expiry.ALL}}.

## Limits and fees

| Country | Maximum per link | Fee, paid by you as the seller |
|---|---|---|
| Saudi Arabia | {{payment_link.max_amount.SA}} | {{fees.payment_link.SA}} |
| UAE | {{payment_link.max_amount.AE}} | {{fees.payment_link.AE}} |
| Egypt | {{payment_link.max_amount.EG}} | {{fees.payment_link.EG}} |

The fee is deducted from the payment when it arrives. The buyer pays only the amount on the link.

Payment links are for occasional personal sales. If someone pays through a link and then disputes the payment with their bank, the amount is taken back from your balance while the dispute is open, even if you've already delivered.
