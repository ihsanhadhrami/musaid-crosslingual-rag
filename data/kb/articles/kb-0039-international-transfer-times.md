Most international transfers arrive the same day. The maximum time depends on where you're sending from.

## Maximum delivery times

- From Saudi Arabia: {{transfer.intl_delivery.SA}}
- From the UAE: {{transfer.intl_delivery.AE}}

The time is counted from when your transfer shows **Sent to bank** in the app, in our business days. Business days are {{calendar.business_days.SA}} in Saudi Arabia and {{calendar.business_days.AE}} in the UAE.

## Why is my transfer taking longer?

- **It's a weekend or holiday in the recipient's country.** Their bank may only credit the money on its next working day.
- **The receiving bank is checking it.** Some banks hold large or first-time transfers for their own checks.
- **The details need fixing.** We'll message you if the bank asks for more information.

## Transfers held for review

Before a transfer leaves Sahla Pay, it may be held for a security review, especially the first time you send to a new recipient or when the amount is large. The review usually takes a few hours. The delivery time above only starts once it's finished, and the status shows **Under review** until then.
