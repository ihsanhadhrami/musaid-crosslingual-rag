You can close a card without closing your Sahla Pay account. Your balance stays, and you can keep sending money and paying with QR codes.

## Close a card

To close it:

1. Go to **Cards** and tap the card.
2. Tap **Card settings** > **Close card**.
3. Confirm.

Closing a card is permanent. If you only want to stop it for a while, freeze it instead; see [Freezing and unfreezing your card](kb-0106).

## What happens to payments on that card?

Subscriptions and recurring payments on the card will be declined. Refunds to the card still reach your balance.

## Can I get a new card later?

Yes. Order a new physical card from **Cards**. The normal card fee applies.

Closing your physical card doesn't refund the fee you paid for it, even if you closed it soon after ordering.
