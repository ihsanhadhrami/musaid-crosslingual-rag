Several of our offers pay cashback. These rules apply to all of them, unless an offer says otherwise.

## When it's paid

Within {{promo.cashback_credit_time.ALL}} after the offer ends, or after you qualify for an offer that has no end date. It appears in **Activity** as **Cashback**.

## Refunds and disputes

If a payment that earned cashback is refunded, partly refunded or disputed, we take back the cashback for that payment. If your balance is too low, we take it from the next money you add.

## Limits

Cashback counts towards your balance limit. If it would take you over the limit, we pay it once you have room.

## Closed accounts

Cashback isn't paid into closed accounts, and we don't pay it to a bank account instead.

## Tax

Cashback isn't a fee, so there's no VAT on it.

## Missing cashback

Wait until {{promo.cashback_credit_time.ALL}} have passed after the offer ends. Then contact us from **Help** > **Chat with us** with the dates and amounts of the payments.
