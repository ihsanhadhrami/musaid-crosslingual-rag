In Egypt, young people can have their own Sahla Pay account from age {{kyc.min_age.EG}}. Until they're {{kyc.guardian_consent_under.EG}}, a parent or legal guardian approves the account and can follow how it's used. Here's how it works from the guardian's side.

## Setting it up

1. Your son or daughter opens their own account and verifies it with their national ID.
2. In their app, they add you as guardian in **Profile** > **Guardian**.
3. You get a request in your own Sahla Pay app. Check the details and tap **Approve**.

You need a verified Sahla Pay account registered in Egypt to be a guardian.

## What you can do as guardian

- See their balance and transactions.
- Set a daily sending limit lower than the standard {{limits.daily_transfer_minor.EG}}.
- Freeze and unfreeze their card from your app.
- Send them pocket money, as a one-off or a scheduled weekly or monthly transfer.

## What you can't do

You can't send money out of their account, change their password, or read their chats with our support team.

## Pocket money ideas

Set up a scheduled monthly transfer on the 1st, and encourage them to create a pocket for something they're saving for. See [Budgeting with Pockets](kb-0259).

## When they turn {{kyc.guardian_consent_under.EG}}

Their account becomes a normal account automatically, and your guardian access ends. You don't need to do anything.

If you close your own Sahla Pay account, your child's account goes back to receive-only until another guardian approves it. Ask another parent or guardian to set it up before you close yours. See [Accounts for minors and guardian consent](kb-0019).
