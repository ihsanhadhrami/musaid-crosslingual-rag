## If I delete the app, is my account closed?

No. Deleting the app only removes it from your phone. Your account, balance and card stay active, and you can log in again by reinstalling it.

## What happens to an account I stop using?

It stays open. If you make no transactions for {{account.dormant_after.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{account.dormant_after.AE}} in the UAE or {{account.dormant_after.EG}} in Egypt, it becomes dormant, and a monthly dormancy fee may apply in the UAE and Egypt. See [Dormant account fees](kb-0137).

## How do I close my account properly?

From the app: **Profile** > **Account settings** > **Close account**. Your balance is sent to your bank. See [Closing your account](kb-0193).

## I already deleted the app

Reinstall it and log in to close your account, or to move your balance out first.
