## New

- **Notes and receipts on any payment.** Open a payment in **Activity** and tap **Add note** to write a note or attach a photo of the receipt. Only you can see them, and they're included in CSV statements.

## Improvements

- Search in **Activity** now finds payments by the note you added.

## Fixes

- Apple Watch payments sometimes appeared under the wrong card when you had both a physical and a virtual card.
