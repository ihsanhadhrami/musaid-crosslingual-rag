When you add money to Sahla Pay, where does it actually go? And what happens to it if something goes wrong? Here's a plain-language explanation.

## Your balance is electronic money

Your Sahla Pay balance is electronic money, not a bank deposit. That has three consequences:

- It doesn't earn interest or profit.
- It isn't covered by a deposit protection scheme.
- We don't lend it out. Every unit of your balance is backed by real money we hold for you.

The full legal wording is in the [Terms of Use (2026 edition)](kb-0201).

## Safeguarding

We keep the money that backs customers' balances separate from our own company money, at partner banks, in line with the laws of each country we operate in. This is called safeguarding. It means customer money isn't used to pay our own bills or staff, and it's protected for customers if the company has financial problems.

## Pockets

Money in Pockets is part of your balance. It's safeguarded in exactly the same way. Pockets are a way of organizing your money, not separate accounts.

## Your card

Your card is a prepaid card linked to your balance. You can't spend more than you have, and there's no credit or overdraft. If your card is stolen, the thief can't run up a debt in your name.

## What protects you from fraud

Safeguarding protects your money from our business risks. Different rules protect you from fraud:

- For payments you didn't make, you're protected under our unauthorized transactions rules, as long as you report them quickly and didn't share your security details. See [Transactions I didn't make](kb-0090).
- For payments you made yourself after being tricked, the protection is weaker, and we can only try to recover the money. See [Investment scams and fake sellers](kb-0164).

## If your account is closed

If you close your account, or we close it, your remaining balance is paid to a bank account in your name. It doesn't disappear. See [Closing your account](kb-0193).
