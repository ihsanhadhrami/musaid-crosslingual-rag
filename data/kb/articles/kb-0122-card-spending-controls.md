Decide where and how your card can be used. Changes apply straight away.

## Your controls

Go to **Cards** > **Card settings** to turn these on or off:

- **Online payments**
- **Contactless payments**
- **ATM withdrawals**
- **International payments**, meaning payments in shops or ATMs outside your country and online payments in a foreign currency

## Daily spending limit

The default daily card spending limit is {{limits.card_daily_spend.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{limits.card_daily_spend.AE}} in the UAE and {{limits.card_daily_spend.EG}} in Egypt. You can lower it under **Card settings** > **Daily limit**, but you can't raise it above the default.

ATM withdrawals have their own daily limit; see [ATM withdrawals](kb-0116).

## Block a merchant

To stop a merchant from charging your card again, go to **Cards** > **Recurring payments**, choose the merchant and tap **Block**.

## Do controls apply to my virtual card?

Each card has its own settings. Changing your physical card's controls doesn't change your virtual card.
