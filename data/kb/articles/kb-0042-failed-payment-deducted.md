Sometimes a payment or transfer fails, but the amount still disappears from your balance. The money isn't lost. It's held while the systems on both sides confirm the payment didn't go through.

## How long until it's back?

| Country | Reversed within |
|---|---|
| Saudi Arabia | {{failed_payment.reversal.SA}} |
| UAE | {{failed_payment.reversal.AE}} |
| Egypt | {{failed_payment.reversal.EG}} |

It comes back automatically, and you'll get a notification. You don't need to report it.

## It's been longer than that

Open the payment in **Activity**, tap **Report a problem** > **Money taken but payment failed**, and we'll trace it.

## Card payments in shops

If a card payment was declined at the till but still shows in your activity, it's usually a hold, not a charge. Holds drop off on their own within {{card.hold_release.ALL}}, which can be longer than the times above. Shop refunds for payments that did go through are different; see [Refund timelines](kb-0071).
