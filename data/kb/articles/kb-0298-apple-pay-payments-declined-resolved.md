**Update, 7 July 2026, 2:10 PM:** Resolved.

For about an hour this morning, some Apple Pay payments in Saudi Arabia and the UAE were declined. Payments with the physical card, the virtual card and Google Pay weren't affected.

You don't need to remove or re-add your card. If a payment shows as declined, it wasn't charged. See [Adding your card to Apple Pay](kb-0111).
