From 1 March 2026, several fees and limits change. This update shows the new values in the app.

## Saudi Arabia

- The daily transfer limit for verified accounts goes up to {{limits.daily_transfer_verified.SA}}.
- International transfers now cost {{fees.intl_transfer_pct.SA}} of the amount sent, with the same minimum fee.
- Replacing a lost, stolen or damaged card now costs {{fees.card_replacement.SA}}.
- You can ask for a temporary increase to your daily limit, up to {{limits.temp_increase_daily.SA}}, for a large one-off payment.

## UAE

- International transfers now cost {{fees.intl_transfer_pct.AE}} of the amount sent.
- Merchant refunds now reach your balance within {{refund.merchant_card.AE}}.
- Temporary daily limit increases are available, up to {{limits.temp_increase_daily.AE}}.

## Egypt

Fees and limits in Egypt don't change. Temporary limit increases are {{limits.temp_increase_available.EG}} for accounts registered in Egypt.

See [Requesting a temporary limit increase](kb-0150) and the fee schedule for your country.
