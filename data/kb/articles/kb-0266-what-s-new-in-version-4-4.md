Eid al-Adha Mubarak. This release focuses on accessibility.

## Improvements

- Better screen reader labels across **Send**, **Cards** and **Activity**, for VoiceOver and TalkBack.
- The app now follows your phone's text size on every screen, including statements and receipts.
- A new **High contrast** option in **Profile** > **Settings** > **Accessibility**. See [Accessibility features](kb-0186).

## Fixes

- Some buttons were too small to tap reliably with large text switched on.
