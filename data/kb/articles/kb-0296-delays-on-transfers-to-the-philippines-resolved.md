**Update, 15 April 2026:** Resolved. Delayed transfers have been credited.

On 14 April 2026, some transfers to banks in the Philippines were held at our partner bank for extra checks and arrived later than usual. Transfers to mobile wallets in the Philippines weren't affected.

Transfers to the Philippines are often returned because of a missing middle name or a different spelling of a surname, which is a different issue. See [Sending money to the Philippines](kb-0220).
