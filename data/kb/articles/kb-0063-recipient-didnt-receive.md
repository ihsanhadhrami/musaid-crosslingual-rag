If you sent money and the person says it hasn't arrived, start by checking the status in **Activity**.

## Status is "Completed"

The money has arrived. If they're a Sahla Pay user, ask them to pull down to refresh their balance. If it was a bank transfer, their bank has accepted it, but some banks take a few hours to show it. Share the receipt with them so their bank can trace it; see [Transfer receipts and reference numbers](kb-0060).

## Status is "Pending", "Processing" or "Under review"

It's on its way. Check the usual times for [local bank transfers](kb-0032) or [international transfers](kb-0039).

## Status is "Waiting for recipient"

You sent it to a phone number that isn't on Sahla Pay. The recipient needs to open an account with that number to claim it.

## Status is "Returned" or "Failed"

It didn't arrive, and the money is back in your balance, or on its way back.

## Sent to the wrong person?

If the details were wrong, see [I sent money to the wrong person](kb-0044).
