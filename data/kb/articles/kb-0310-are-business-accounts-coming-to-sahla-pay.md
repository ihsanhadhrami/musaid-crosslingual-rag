It's one of the questions we're asked most. The short answer: not yet, and we don't have a date to share.

## What you can do today

If you sell occasionally, for example home baking or private lessons, you can get paid with payment links. See [Payment links for small sellers](kb-0066) and [Selling on the side: what Sahla Pay can and can't do](kb-0260).

## What you can't do

A personal account can't be used as the main account of a shop, a company or a registered business. If your account looks like a business account, with many payments from many different people, we may ask about your activity, and in some cases restrict it. See [Business accounts vs personal accounts](kb-0020).
