**Update, 22 July 2025, 9:15 PM:** Resolved. Online payments are working normally.

Between about 6:00 PM and 8:30 PM Riyadh time, some online card payments were declined during the 3-D Secure step, because of a problem at our card security provider. Payments in shops and with Apple Pay or Google Pay weren't affected.

## Was I charged?

No. A declined payment isn't charged. Some merchants place a temporary hold before the payment is declined. If you see one in **Activity**, it drops off within {{card.hold_release.ALL}}. See [Merchant holds and pre-authorizations](kb-0123).

If you tried several times and more than one payment went through, see [Duplicate charges](kb-0080).
