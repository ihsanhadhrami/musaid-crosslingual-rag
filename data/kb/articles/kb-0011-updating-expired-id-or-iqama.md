When your national ID or Iqama is renewed, update it in the app so your account stays fully active.

## What happens when my ID expires?

We'll remind you in the app before the expiry date. After it expires, your account keeps working normally for {{kyc.expired_id_grace.SA}}. If you haven't updated it by then, outgoing transfers and card payments are paused until you do. You can still receive money and log in.

## Update your ID

To update it:

1. Go to **Profile** > **Personal details** > **ID document**.
2. Tap **Update ID**.
3. Scan the front and back of the renewed card, or confirm through Nafath.
4. Tap **Submit**.

Updates are usually approved within minutes, and the pause is lifted straight away.

## My Iqama number changed

If your Iqama number has changed, for example after a change of status, the app can't match it to your account. Don't open a new account. Contact us from **Help** > **Chat with us** and we'll update it manually after checking both documents.
