To verify an account registered in Saudi Arabia, you need {{kyc.id_document.SA}}, and a selfie taken in the app.

## Which ID should I use?

Saudi citizens use their national ID. Residents use their Iqama. The ID must be valid on the day you submit it, and the photo, name and ID number must be easy to read.

## Can I use my passport?

No. A passport alone can't be used to verify an account registered in Saudi Arabia, even if it's valid. If you're a resident, use your Iqama.

## Is there a faster way?

Yes. If you have a Nafath account, you can verify through Nafath instead of scanning your card, and it's usually approved within minutes. See [Verifying with Nafath](kb-0023).

## Do I need proof of address?

Not in Saudi Arabia. We use the address registered with your national ID or Iqama.

## My Iqama is about to expire

You can still verify with it, but you'll need to update it once it's renewed. Accounts with an expired Iqama are restricted after {{kyc.expired_id_grace.SA}}. See [Updating an expired ID or Iqama](kb-0011).
