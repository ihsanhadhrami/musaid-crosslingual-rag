## New security options

Two new options in **Profile** > **Security**, both switched off by default:

- **Code at every login.** We'll send you a code every time you log in, even on your trusted phone.
- **Passcode for transfers.** Every transfer needs your app passcode, not just your fingerprint or face.

These are useful if other people sometimes use your phone. See [Two-step verification](kb-0169).

## Fixes

- The login screen sometimes showed **Too many attempts** after a single wrong password, if you had tried on another device the same day.
