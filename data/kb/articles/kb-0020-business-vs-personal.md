Sahla Pay offers personal accounts only. We don't offer business, company or merchant accounts at the moment.

## Can I use my personal account for my small business?

You can receive occasional payments, for example from friends who buy something from you, and you can create payment links for small sales. See [Payment links for small sellers](kb-0066).

Personal accounts can't be used as the main account of a registered business. If your account receives regular, high-value business payments, we may ask you about the source of the funds and, in some cases, restrict the account. See [Source of funds questions](kb-0025).

## Can my company pay salaries into Sahla Pay?

Yes. Employers can send salaries to your account from their bank like any other bank transfer.

We can't share plans for future products. Any new products will be announced in the app.
