If you want your money back for something you bought, whether you returned it, cancelled it or it wasn't what you ordered, ask the merchant first. Only the merchant can issue a refund for a card or QR payment.

## Ask the merchant

To request a refund:

1. Contact the shop or website through their own customer service.
2. Give them the date, amount and the last four digits of your Sahla Pay card. You'll find these on the payment in **Activity**.
3. Ask them for a refund reference once they've processed it.

## After the merchant refunds you

The refund appears in your balance within {{refund.merchant_card.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{refund.merchant_card.AE}} in the UAE and {{refund.merchant_card.EG}} in Egypt, counted from when the merchant sends it. See [Refund timelines](kb-0071).

## The merchant won't refund me

If the merchant refuses, doesn't reply, or promised a refund that never came, you can open a dispute with us. See [Disputing a card transaction](kb-0076). We'll ask what you did to sort it out with the merchant first, so keep your emails or chat messages.
