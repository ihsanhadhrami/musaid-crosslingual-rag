You can change where your card is delivered until the courier has picked it up.

## Change the address

To change it:

1. Go to **Cards** > **Track delivery**.
2. Tap **Change address**.
3. Enter the new address, or choose your work address.
4. Tap **Save**.

## The courier already has it

Once the status shows **With courier**, the address can't be changed in the app. Use the link in the courier's SMS to reschedule the delivery, or wait for it to be returned to us and then arrange a new delivery.

## Can I collect it myself?

No. Cards are only delivered, not collected from an office.

The delivery address has to be in the country your account is registered in. If you're abroad, we can't send your card there; it waits for you until you're back. Cards that aren't activated within {{card.activation_deadline.ALL}} of being sent are cancelled, so arrange the delivery and activate it before then.
