| UAE | {{limits.atm_daily.AE}} |
| Egypt | {{limits.atm_daily.EG}} |

The limit applies to all withdrawals in a day combined, in your country and abroad. Withdrawals abroad are counted in your own currency, after conversion.

ATMs often have their own limit per withdrawal, which can be lower than ours. If you need more, make several withdrawals; each one is charged separately.

The daily ATM limit is separate from your card spending limit and your transfer limits. Using one doesn't reduce the others. Daily limits reset at midnight in your account's country.
