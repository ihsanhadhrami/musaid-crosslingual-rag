Short answers to common questions about sending and receiving money.

## Is it free to send money to friends?

Yes. Transfers between Sahla Pay users in the same country are free.

## How much can I send per day?

With a verified account, {{limits.daily_transfer_verified.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{limits.daily_transfer_verified.AE}} in the UAE and {{limits.daily_transfer_verified.EG}} in Egypt.

## Can I send money abroad?

From Saudi Arabia and the UAE, yes. International transfers are {{transfer.intl_available.EG}} for accounts in Egypt, but you can receive money from abroad.

## How do I get my money back if I sent it to the wrong person?

Ask them to return it. If they won't, open a dispute from the transfer in **Activity**.

## Can I cancel a transfer?

Only while it's pending, and within {{transfer.cancel_window.ALL}}.

## Why do I need a code for some transfers?

We ask for a one-time password when you send to a new recipient or send money abroad, to check it's really you.

## Do weekends affect transfers?

Not for transfers between Sahla Pay users. For bank transfers it depends on your country; see [Transfers on weekends and public holidays](kb-0061).
