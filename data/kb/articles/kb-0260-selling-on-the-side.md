If you sell things occasionally, like home baking, handmade items, second-hand goods or private lessons, Sahla Pay can help you get paid. But a personal account isn't a business account, and there are limits to what it's for.

## Getting paid

- **From other Sahla Pay users:** ask them to send money to your phone number. It's free and instant.
- **From anyone with a debit card:** create a payment link and share it. See [Payment links for small sellers](kb-0066).
- **By bank transfer:** share your Sahla Pay IBAN from **Add money** > **Bank transfer**.

## What a personal account isn't for

You can't use Sahla Pay as the main account of a registered business, a shop, or a company. We don't offer business accounts; see [Business accounts vs personal accounts](kb-0020). If your account starts to look like a business, with many payments from many different people, we may ask about your activity.

## Keeping records

Keep a simple list of what you sold, to whom and for how much. If we ask about the source of your funds, it's the quickest way to answer. Your statements in **Profile** > **Statements** show every payment.

Buyers who pay by payment link can dispute the payment with their bank. If they do, the amount is taken back from your balance while the dispute is open, even if you've already delivered. Keep proof of delivery, such as a photo or a signed note, for every sale.
