Top up a prepaid mobile line, yours or someone else's, from your Sahla Pay balance.

## Recharge a number

To recharge:

1. Tap **Payments** > **Mobile recharge**.
2. Enter the phone number or pick a contact. The operator is detected automatically.
3. Choose a recharge amount or a bundle.
4. Tap **Pay**.

The credit usually arrives within a minute. You'll get an SMS from the operator.

## Wrong operator or number

If the number was moved to another operator, the app may detect the old one. Choose the right operator manually before paying.

Recharges to a wrong number can't be reversed by us, because the credit is used by the operator immediately. Check the number before you confirm.

## Fees

Recharges follow the bill payment fee in your country. See [Bill payment fees](kb-0049).
