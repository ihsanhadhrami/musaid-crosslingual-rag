Eid al-Adha Mubarak. Eid al-Adha is expected on or around 27 May 2026.

## What works as usual

- Transfers between Sahla Pay users, QR payments and card payments.
- In Saudi Arabia and Egypt, local bank transfers.

## What waits for the next business day

- Local bank transfers in the UAE.
- International transfers, from both Saudi Arabia and the UAE. Banks in many destination countries are also closed.
- Bill payments are accepted, but the biller receives them after the holiday.

Scheduled transfers still run on their date. If one goes to a bank account in the UAE or abroad, it arrives after the holiday. See [Transfers on weekends and public holidays](kb-0061).
