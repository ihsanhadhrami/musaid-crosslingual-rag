Whether you're planning Umrah next year or saving for Hajj over several years, a clear plan makes the journey easier to afford.

## Work out the total

Make a list of everything the trip will cost:

- The package or flights and accommodation.
- Visa fees, if you need a visa.
- Transport between Makkah, Madinah and the holy sites.
- Food and daily spending.
- Gifts to bring home.
- Money for the family you leave behind, if you're the main earner.

Prices go up during Ramadan and the Hajj season, so check current prices for the dates you want.

## Make a pocket for it

Create a pocket called "Umrah" or "Hajj" and set the total as its target. The app shows how far you've come. Then set up an automatic weekly or monthly transfer into it.

To work out the monthly amount, divide the total by the number of months until you plan to go. If the number is too high, choose a later date rather than saving money you'll need for bills.

## Paying for the trip

- **Packages:** pay a licensed travel agency or the official platform, by card where possible. Card payments can be disputed if the agency doesn't deliver.
- **Never pay an individual** for a package advertised as coming from a company, and be careful with offers on social media that are much cheaper than others.

Watch out for fake Umrah and Hajj package scams, especially before Ramadan. See [Summer scam warning: fake travel deals](kb-0311).

## Using your card on the trip

If your account is in Saudi Arabia, your card works as usual. If you're travelling from the UAE or Egypt, check that **International payments** is switched on in **Cards** > **Card settings**, and read [Using your card in Saudi Arabia](kb-0246).
