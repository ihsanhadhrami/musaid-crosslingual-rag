## New

- **Favourite recipients.** Star the people you pay most often in **Send** > **Recipients**, and they'll appear first. See [Saved recipients](kb-0057).
- **IBAN check before you send abroad.** When you add a bank account in another country, the app now checks the IBAN format before you confirm, which catches most typing mistakes.

## For customers in Saudi Arabia

SADAD bills you've paid before now appear in a **Due soon** list when the biller issues a new bill. See [Paying government bills with SADAD](kb-0058).

## Fixes

- Exchange rates on the confirmation screen didn't refresh if you left the app open for a long time before confirming.
