Pay electricity, water, telecom, internet and other bills from your Sahla Pay balance.

## Pay a bill

To pay a bill:

1. Tap **Payments** > **Bills**.
2. Choose the category and the company.
3. Enter your account or subscriber number from the bill.
4. Check the amount due, which we fetch from the company where possible.
5. Tap **Pay**.

Save the bill to pay it faster next time, or set a reminder.
