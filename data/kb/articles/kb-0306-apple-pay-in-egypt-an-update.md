Many of you have asked when Apple Pay will come to Sahla Pay in Egypt. We're working on it, but we can't give a date yet.

For now, Apple Pay is {{wallet.apple_pay.EG}} for accounts registered in Egypt. For accounts in Saudi Arabia it's {{wallet.apple_pay.SA}}, and for accounts in the UAE it's {{wallet.apple_pay.AE}}.

## What you can use instead

- **Google Pay** works on Android phones in all three countries. See [Adding your card to Google Pay or Samsung Pay](kb-0112).
- Your **virtual card** works for online payments straight away.
- Your **physical card** works contactless in shops.

We'll announce it here and in the app when Apple Pay is available in Egypt.
