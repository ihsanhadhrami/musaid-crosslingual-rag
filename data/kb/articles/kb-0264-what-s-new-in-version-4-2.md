## New

- **Edit each person's share when you split a bill.** Previously every split was equal. Now you can change any person's amount before you send the requests. See [Splitting a bill](kb-0046).
- **Reminders for payment requests.** If someone hasn't paid your request, tap it in **Activity** and choose **Send reminder**. You can send a reminder once a day.

## Fixes

- The **Share receipt** button didn't work for some transfers to bank accounts.
- On some Android phones, the keyboard covered the amount field.
