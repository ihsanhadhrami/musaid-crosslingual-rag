You can send money from your Sahla Pay balance to a bank account in the Philippines, if your account is registered in Saudi Arabia or the UAE and is verified. The recipient receives Philippine pesos (PHP).

## What your recipient needs to give you

- the recipient's full name, including their middle name, exactly as their bank or wallet has it
- their bank account number and bank name
- or, instead of a bank account, a verified mobile wallet number

Ask the recipient to send you these details in writing, for example by copying them from their banking app, rather than reading them out. Most returned transfers are caused by a wrong digit or a name that doesn't match.

## How to send

To send money to the Philippines:

1. Tap **Send** > **International** and choose the Philippines.
2. Enter the amount, in your currency or in Philippine pesos (PHP).
3. Enter the recipient's details, or choose a saved recipient.
4. Check the exchange rate, the fee and the amount the recipient will get.
5. Tap **Confirm** and enter the one-time password we send you.

The exchange rate is fixed when you confirm.

## Things to know about this destination

Filipino banks are strict about names. A missing middle name or a different spelling of a surname is the most common reason transfers to the Philippines are returned.

Transfers to mobile wallets usually arrive within minutes. Transfers to banks may take until the next banking day if sent late at night.

Typhoons and public holidays can close banks in parts of the country. Mobile wallets usually keep working.

## If something goes wrong

If the receiving bank rejects the transfer, the full amount, including our fee, comes back to your balance in your own currency, at the exchange rate on the day it's returned. If you sent it to the wrong person, you can ask us to recall it, but a recall fee applies and the receiving bank decides whether to return the money. See [Disputes for international transfers](kb-0095).
