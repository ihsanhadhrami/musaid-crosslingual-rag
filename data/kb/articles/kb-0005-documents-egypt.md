To verify an account registered in Egypt, you need {{kyc.id_document.EG}}, a selfie, and a recent proof of address.

## Which ID is accepted?

Your Egyptian national ID card, front and back. It must be valid and not damaged. Foreign residents in Egypt can't open a Sahla Pay account at the moment.

## What counts as proof of address?

Any of these, in your name and dated within the last {{kyc.address_proof_max_age.EG}}:

- an electricity, water or gas bill
- a landline or internet bill
- a bank statement

If the bill is in a family member's name, upload it together with a document showing you live at the same address, such as the address on the back of your national ID. More in [Proof of address: when it's needed](kb-0018).

## I'm under {{kyc.guardian_consent_under.EG}}

You can still verify with your own national ID, but a parent or guardian will need to approve your account. See [Accounts for minors and guardian consent](kb-0019).

## How long will it take?

Manual reviews in Egypt take up to {{kyc.verification_time.EG}}. See [How long does verification take?](kb-0007).
