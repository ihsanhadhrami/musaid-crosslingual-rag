**Update, 11 April 2025:** All delayed transfers have now been credited.

From 9 to 11 April 2025, some transfers from Saudi Arabia and the UAE to banks in Pakistan took longer than usual, because of a system upgrade at one of our partner banks. Transfers to mobile wallets in Pakistan weren't affected.

No money was lost, and you don't need to do anything. If your recipient still hasn't received a transfer, check the status in **Activity**, then see [Recipient didn't receive the money](kb-0063).
