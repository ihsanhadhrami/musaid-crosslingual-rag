New to Sahla Pay? Make your first card payment and get money back.

## The offer

Spend at least {{promo.welcome_min_spend.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{promo.welcome_min_spend.AE}} in the UAE or {{promo.welcome_min_spend.EG}} in Egypt in a single card payment, and we'll give you {{promo.welcome_cashback.SA}}, {{promo.welcome_cashback.AE}} or {{promo.welcome_cashback.EG}} back.

## How to take part

1. Verify your identity. See [How to verify your identity in the app](kb-0006).
2. Within {{promo.welcome_window.ALL}} of verification, pay with your physical card, your virtual card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, in a shop or online.

You don't need to sign up or enter a code.

## Terms

- One welcome cashback per person, even if you closed an earlier account and opened a new one.
- Only card payments count. ATM withdrawals, top-ups, transfers, bill payments, QR payments and payments through payment links don't.
- The payment must be a single payment of at least the minimum amount. Several smaller payments don't add up.
- If the payment is refunded or disputed, we take the cashback back.
- In Egypt, accounts of customers under {{kyc.guardian_consent_under.EG}} can't get the welcome offer, even with guardian approval.

Cashback is paid into your balance within {{promo.cashback_credit_time.ALL}} after the offer ends, and appears in **Activity** as **Cashback**. See [How cashback works](kb-0327).
