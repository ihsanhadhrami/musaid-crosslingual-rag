If your card was declined, the app usually tells you why. Tap the declined payment in **Activity**.

## "Insufficient balance"

Your balance didn't cover the payment. For payments in another currency, the amount after conversion counts, so a payment can be declined even when it looks like you have enough.

## "Card frozen"

Unfreeze it in **Cards**.

## "Daily spending limit reached"

Card payments are limited to {{limits.card_daily_spend.SA}} a day in Saudi Arabia, {{limits.card_daily_spend.AE}} in the UAE and {{limits.card_daily_spend.EG}} in Egypt, or less if you lowered it yourself. See [Card spending controls](kb-0122).

## "Blocked by your settings"

You've turned off online, contactless, ATM or international payments. Turn the setting back on in **Card settings**.

## "Incorrect PIN" or "PIN blocked"

See [Card blocked after wrong PIN attempts](kb-0105).

## "Declined for security"

Our systems stopped the payment because it looked unusual. Check your notifications to confirm it was you, then try again.

## No reason shown

The merchant's bank may have declined it, often for online payments abroad. Try again later, or use your virtual card or a mobile wallet.
