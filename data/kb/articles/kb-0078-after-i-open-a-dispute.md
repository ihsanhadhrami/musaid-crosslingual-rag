Once you've submitted a dispute, you'll get a case number by email and in the app under **Help** > **My cases**.

## How long does it take?

Our investigation takes up to {{dispute.investigation.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{dispute.investigation.AE}} in the UAE and {{dispute.investigation.EG}} in Egypt.

If the dispute becomes a chargeback with the merchant's bank, it can take longer, up to {{chargeback.max_resolution.ALL}} in total. See [Chargebacks explained](kb-0079).

## Will you contact me?

Only if we need more information. We'll message you in the app, and you'll usually have a few days to reply. If you don't reply, we decide on what we have.

## Do I get my money back while I wait?

For card disputes, often yes. If the case is still open after {{dispute.temporary_credit_after.ALL}}, we usually add a temporary credit to your balance. For wrong transfers between users, no; the money is returned only if the dispute is approved.

## How will I know the outcome?

We'll send you a notification and an email explaining the decision. If you disagree, see [My dispute was rejected](kb-0086).
