This policy sets out what you may not use your Sahla Pay account, card or payment links for. It forms part of the Terms of Use. Breaking it may lead us to decline payments, restrict your account, or close it.

## 1. Illegal activity

You must not use Sahla Pay for anything that is illegal in your country or in the country where the payment is received, including fraud, money laundering, terrorist financing, tax evasion, bribery or the purchase of stolen goods.

## 2. Prohibited goods and services

You must not send or receive payments for:

- weapons, ammunition or explosives;
- narcotics, controlled substances or drug paraphernalia;
- counterfeit goods or goods that infringe intellectual property;
- adult content or services;
- gambling or betting, where it is not lawful in your country;
- the purchase or sale of cryptocurrencies or other virtual assets;
- unlicensed financial services, such as money transfer or currency exchange for others;
- unlicensed pharmaceuticals, tobacco or alcohol, where restricted by law.

## 3. Using your account for others

Your account is for your own personal use. You must not:

- allow another person to use your account, card or device;
- receive money for another person and pass it on to them or to someone else;
- use your personal account as the account of a business;
- open an account for someone else or in a false name.

Receiving salary payments from your employer, family support from relatives, and occasional payments for things you sell personally are not business use.

## 4. Misusing our services

You must not:

- attempt to get around our limits, for example by splitting payments between accounts or making many small payments to avoid a limit;
- use our services to harass, threaten or defraud anyone, including through payment notes and requests;
- try to access another person's account, or interfere with the security of our app or systems;
- use automated tools, emulators or modified versions of the app.

## 5. Sanctions

You must not use Sahla Pay to make payments to, or receive payments from, people or countries subject to comprehensive international sanctions, or to get around sanctions in any way.

## 6. Payment notes

Notes you add to transfers and requests are visible to the recipient. They must not contain offensive, threatening or misleading content. We may remove notes that break this policy.

## 7. What we may do

If we reasonably believe you have broken this policy, we may decline or reverse payments where the law allows, hold funds while we investigate, restrict or close your account, and report the matter to the authorities. Where the law prohibits us from telling you about an investigation, we will not explain the reason.

We may also act on information from other customers, banks or the authorities, for example where several people report payments to your account as fraud, even if we have not yet finished our own investigation.
