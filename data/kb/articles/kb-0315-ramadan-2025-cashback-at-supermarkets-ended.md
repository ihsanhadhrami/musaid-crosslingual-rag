**This offer ended on 30 March 2025.**

## The offer

From 1 to 30 March 2025, we gave {{promo.ramadan2025_pct.ALL}} cashback on card payments at supermarkets, up to {{promo.ramadan2025_cap.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{promo.ramadan2025_cap.AE}} in the UAE or {{promo.ramadan2025_cap.EG}} in Egypt for the whole month.

## Terms

- Only payments at merchants registered as supermarkets or grocery stores counted. We go by the merchant category that the shop's bank gives the card network, not by what the shop sells. Some hypermarkets and delivery apps are registered under other categories and didn't count.
- Payments with Apple Pay and Google Pay counted.
- Refunded payments didn't count.

Cashback was paid by 30 April 2025. If you think yours is missing, contact us from **Help** with the dates of the payments.
