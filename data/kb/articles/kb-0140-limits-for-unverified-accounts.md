These are the restrictions applied to accounts that have not completed identity verification.

| Limit | Saudi Arabia | UAE | Egypt |
|---|---|---|---|
| Maximum balance | {{limits.balance_max_unverified.SA}} | {{limits.balance_max_unverified.AE}} | {{limits.balance_max_unverified.EG}} |
| Daily sending to Sahla Pay users | {{limits.daily_transfer_unverified.SA}} | {{limits.daily_transfer_unverified.AE}} | {{limits.daily_transfer_unverified.EG}} |
| Monthly receiving limit | {{limits.receive_monthly_unverified.SA}} | {{limits.receive_monthly_unverified.AE}} | {{limits.receive_monthly_unverified.EG}} |
| Bank and international transfers | not available | not available | not available |
| Cards | not available | not available | not available |

You can still use an unverified account to make QR payments and pay bills. Those services have separate limits and do not reduce the daily sending allowance shown above.

To remove these restrictions, open **Profile** > **Verify identity**. See [Unverified vs verified accounts](kb-0010).
