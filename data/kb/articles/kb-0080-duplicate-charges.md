Seeing the same payment twice? Before disputing it, check whether one of them is just a hold.

## One says "Pending", the other "Completed"

This is usually a hold plus the final charge. Hotels, petrol stations and car rental companies often place a hold first and then charge the final amount separately. The hold drops off on its own within {{card.hold_release.ALL}}. See [Merchant holds and pre-authorizations](kb-0123).

## Both say "Completed"

You may really have been charged twice. Contact the merchant first and ask them to refund one of the payments. If they don't, open a dispute from either payment: **Report a problem** > **I was charged twice**.

## The amounts are slightly different

This often happens with payments in a foreign currency, or tips added after the payment. It's usually two different charges, not a duplicate.

If you paid twice yourself, for example because the first payment seemed to fail and you tried again, it's not a duplicate charge for dispute purposes. Ask the merchant for a refund of the extra payment.
