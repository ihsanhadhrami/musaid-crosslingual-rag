Opening a Sahla Pay account takes a few minutes, but we ask a lot: your ID, a selfie, your address, what you'll use the account for. Here's why.

## The law requires it

Like every regulated payment provider in Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Egypt, we have to know who our customers are. This is called KYC, short for "know your customer". The rules are there to stop criminals using payment accounts for fraud, money laundering or financing terrorism.

## Your ID

Your ID shows who you are and that you're allowed to open an account in that country. We check that it's genuine and valid. We don't accept photocopies, photos of a screen, or expired documents. See [Who can open a Sahla Pay account](kb-0002).

## The selfie

The selfie shows that the person opening the account is the person on the ID, and that it's a live person, not a photo. It protects you from someone opening an account in your name with a stolen ID.

## Your address and occupation

These help us understand what's normal for your account. Someone who receives a salary and pays rent has a different pattern from a student who receives money from family. When a payment is very different from your usual pattern, we may ask about it.

## Questions later on

Sometimes we ask again, for example when your ID expires, every few years as the law requires, or when you start receiving much larger amounts. See [Why we ask you to verify again](kb-0022) and [Source of funds questions](kb-0025).

## What we do with it

We use your information to meet these legal duties and to protect your account, and we keep it for the periods the law requires, even after your account is closed. See [Privacy notice summary](kb-0192).
