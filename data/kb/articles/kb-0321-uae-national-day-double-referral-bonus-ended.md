**This offer ended on 7 December 2025.**

## The offer

From 25 November to 7 December 2025, referral bonuses in the UAE were doubled to {{promo.union_day2025_referral.AE}} each, for you and your friend.

## Terms

The normal referral terms applied. Your friend had to open their account during the offer, using your link, and reach the minimum amount within the usual time. If they reached it after 7 December, you both still got the double bonus. See [Refer a friend and you both get a bonus](kb-0314).
