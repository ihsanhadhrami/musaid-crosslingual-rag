**This offer ended on 1 December 2025.**

## The offer

From 27 November to 1 December 2025, online card payments earned {{promo.white_friday2025_pct.ALL}} cashback, up to {{promo.white_friday2025_cap.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{promo.white_friday2025_cap.AE}} in the UAE or {{promo.white_friday2025_cap.EG}} in Egypt.

## Terms

- Online payments only, with your physical or virtual card. Payments in shops didn't count, even with Apple Pay or Google Pay.
- Payments in a foreign currency counted, but our exchange rate markup applied as usual.
- If you returned an item and the merchant refunded you, we took the cashback for that payment back, even if the refund came after the cashback was paid.
- Payments to gambling sites, money transfer services and digital wallets didn't count.
