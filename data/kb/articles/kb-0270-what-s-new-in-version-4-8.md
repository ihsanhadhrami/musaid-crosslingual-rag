## Improvements

- **Payment links** now show when each link expires, and you can resend an expired link with one tap. See [Payment links for small sellers](kb-0066).
- **Transfer statuses** now explain what happens next. For example, a transfer marked **Sent to bank** tells you that the receiving bank is processing it. See [Transfer statuses explained](kb-0052).

## Fixes

- Some customers saw the same payment twice in **Activity** after a top-up by bank transfer. The duplicate was only on screen; you were charged once.
