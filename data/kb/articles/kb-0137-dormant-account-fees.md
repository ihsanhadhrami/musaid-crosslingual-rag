An account becomes dormant when you haven't made any transaction yourself for a while. Money you receive, interest and fees don't count; it has to be something you did, like a payment, a transfer or a top-up.

We'll email you and send an app notification before your account becomes dormant, so you have time to use it.

To make your account active again, log in and make any transaction, even a small top-up. The fee stops from the next month.

The dormancy fee is only taken from money in your balance. It never takes your balance below zero, and if your balance is zero, nothing is charged and nothing is owed. Accounts that stay dormant with a zero balance may eventually be closed; see [Dormant accounts](kb-0194).
