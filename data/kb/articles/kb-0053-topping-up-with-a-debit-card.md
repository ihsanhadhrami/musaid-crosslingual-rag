A debit card top-up is the fastest way to add money. It arrives in your balance immediately.

## Top up

To top up with a card:

1. Tap **Add money** > **Debit card**.
2. Enter the amount.
3. Choose a saved card, or add a new one.
4. Approve the payment in your bank's app or with the code your bank sends.

## Fees

Card top-ups have a small fee; see [Top-up fees](kb-0055). Bank transfer top-ups are free.

## Which cards work?

Debit cards issued in your country, in your own name. The name on the card is checked against your Sahla Pay name, so a family member's card will be declined even if they agree to it.

Credit cards and prepaid cards are not accepted.
