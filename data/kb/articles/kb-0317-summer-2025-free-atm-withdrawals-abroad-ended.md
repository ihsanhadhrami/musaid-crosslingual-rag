**This offer ended on 31 August 2025.**

## The offer

From 1 July to 31 August 2025, customers in Saudi Arabia and the UAE got their first {{promo.summer2025_free_atm.ALL}} ATM withdrawals abroad free of our ATM fee.

## Terms

- The offer covered our fee only. Some ATM operators abroad charge their own fee, shown on the ATM screen, and we didn't refund it.
- Our exchange rate markup still applied.
- Withdrawals in your own country didn't count, and didn't use up any of the free withdrawals abroad.
- Accounts registered in Egypt weren't included.

Normal fees: [ATM withdrawal fees](kb-0117).
