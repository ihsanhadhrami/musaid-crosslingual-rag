Sending money to InstaPay addresses will be unavailable on **Friday 16 May 2025, from 2:00 AM to 6:00 AM Cairo time**, while the InstaPay network carries out maintenance.

During this time:

- **Send** > **InstaPay** shows a message that the service is temporarily unavailable.
- Transfers to other Sahla Pay users, card payments and bill payments work as usual.
- Money sent to your Sahla Pay IBAN through InstaPay may arrive after the maintenance ends.

If you start an InstaPay transfer just before the maintenance and it shows **Processing**, don't send it again. It will complete or come back to your balance. See [Sending to InstaPay addresses](kb-0059).
