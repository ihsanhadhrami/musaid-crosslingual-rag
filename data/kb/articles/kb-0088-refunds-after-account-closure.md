If a refund arrives after you've closed your Sahla Pay account, you still get it. We forward it to the bank account you chose when you closed the account.

## How long does it take?

You'll get an email when we send it.

## My bank account has changed

Contact us with the details of your new bank account in your own name. We'll ask you to confirm your identity first, because you can no longer log in to the app.

## Can I still open a dispute?

No. Disputes need an open account, which is why you can't close your account while a dispute is in progress.

If the bank account you nominated is closed and the forwarded refund is returned to us, we hold it for you. Contact us to claim it; we keep it until it's claimed or until we're legally required to pass it on as unclaimed money.
