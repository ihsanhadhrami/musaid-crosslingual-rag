Sometimes we ask customers who are already verified to confirm their details again. This is a legal requirement for payment companies, not a sign that something is wrong with your account.

## When does this happen?

- Every {{kyc.reverification_interval.ALL}}, as a routine check.
- When your ID expires.
- If your activity changes a lot, for example you start receiving much larger amounts.
- If we detect a login from a new device together with other unusual activity.

## What do I need to do?

Open the notification or go to **Profile** > **Verify identity**. Usually it's just a new selfie. Sometimes we also ask for your ID again, or a few questions about how you use the account.

## What if I don't do it?

We'll remind you several times. If you still haven't completed it by the date in the notification, sending money and card payments are paused until you do. Receiving money keeps working.

If we've asked about the source of your money, see [Source of funds questions](kb-0025).
