Older people are a common target for phone and message scams. If your parents use Sahla Pay, a few simple steps can protect their money.

## Talk about the golden rule

Nobody from Sahla Pay, a bank, the police or any government office will ever ask for a one-time password, PIN or password. If anyone asks, it's a scam, however official they sound. See [We will never ask for your OTP](kb-0162).

## Set up the app together

- Turn on fingerprint or face login, so they don't need to type their password.
- Turn on **Code at every login** and **Passcode for transfers** in **Profile** > **Security**, for extra checks before money can move. See [Two-step verification](kb-0169).
- Lower their daily card spending limit in **Card settings**, if they don't need the full amount.
- Turn on notifications, so they see every payment straight away.

## Scams to warn them about

- **"Your account is blocked"** calls and messages asking them to verify their details.
- **Family emergency** messages from a new number claiming to be a son or daughter who needs money urgently. Always call the family member on their usual number first.
- **Prize and refund** messages with a link.
- **Investment offers** with guaranteed returns.

## If something happens

Freeze the card in the app and call our fraud line, which is open {{support.fraud_line_hours.ALL}}. You can call together with your parent; we'll talk to you with their permission, but we'll need to confirm their identity with them directly. See [Reporting fraud](kb-0156).

## Can I manage their account for them?

No. Each account belongs to one person, and only they can use it. You can't log in as them or approve payments on their behalf, even with their permission. If a parent can no longer manage their own money, ask us about the documents needed for a legal representative.
