The fastest way to reach us is in the app: tap **Help** > **Chat with us**. You'll talk to a person, in Arabic or English, and your account details are already attached, so you don't need to repeat them.

Outside these hours, you can still leave a chat message and we'll reply when we open.

## Can I call you?

Yes. The number for your country is on the back of your card and under **Help** > **Call us**. If you call, we'll confirm your identity with a code sent to your phone.

## Urgent: lost card or fraud

You don't need to wait for us. Freeze your card instantly in **Cards** > **Freeze**. It works at any time, including outside support hours, and you can unfreeze it yourself if you find the card.

## Email

We don't handle account questions by email, because we can't confirm who is writing. Emails asking for personal details that appear to come from us are not from us.
