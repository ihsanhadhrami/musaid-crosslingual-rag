For the quickest help, open the app and select **Help** > **Chat with us**. A support agent can assist you in Arabic or English, and your account information is attached to the conversation so you do not need to provide it again.

You can send a chat message outside your country's listed hours. We will respond when support reopens.

## Calling support

Find your country's phone number on the back of your card or in **Help** > **Call us**. During the call, we verify your identity using a code sent to your phone.

## Lost card or suspected fraud

Do not wait for support to respond. Freeze the card immediately through **Cards** > **Freeze**. Card freezing is available at all times, including outside support hours. If you find the card later, you can unfreeze it yourself.

## Email requests

We do not resolve account questions by email because we cannot reliably verify the sender's identity. Treat emails requesting personal information that claim to be from Sahla Pay as fraudulent.
