Sometimes a transfer doesn't go straight through. Instead, it shows **Under review** in **Activity**. Here's why that happens, and what you can do.

## Why we review payments

Like every regulated payment provider, we're required by law to check payments for signs of fraud, money laundering and sanctions breaches. Most of these checks are automatic and invisible. A small share of payments are held so that a person can look at them.

## Common reasons

- **A payment to many new recipients in a short time.** This is a pattern we see in some scams, where a victim is persuaded to send money to several accounts.
- **A payment that's unusual for you.** For example, a much larger amount than you normally send, or a first transfer to a new country.
- **A recipient we need to check.** For example, a name similar to one on a sanctions list. Most of these turn out to be a different person with a similar name.
- **A payment that doesn't match what you told us.** When you opened your account, we asked how you'd use it. A very different pattern can lead to questions.
- **A new phone or a new SIM.** After you set up a new phone, or after a SIM swap, some transfers are paused for a while. This is a separate protection from reviews. See [SIM swap protection](kb-0161).

## How long it takes

Most reviews are finished within a few hours. Execution times for the transfer start only after the review is complete, so a transfer that's normally instant may arrive later than usual.

## What you can do

- Check your **Inbox** and email. If we need information, we'll ask there, and answering quickly is the fastest way to release the payment.
- Don't send the same payment again. If both are approved, you'll have paid twice.

## What we can't tell you

In some cases, the law prohibits us from explaining why a payment was held, or from telling you about a report we've made to the authorities. This isn't a judgement about you; it's a legal requirement. See [Account restrictions due to suspicious activity](kb-0167).

## If a payment is declined

If we decide not to process a payment, the money stays in your balance or comes back to it. If you think the decision is wrong, contact us from **Help** > **Chat with us**.
