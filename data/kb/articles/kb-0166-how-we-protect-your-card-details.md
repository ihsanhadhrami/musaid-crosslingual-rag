Your card details are stored and processed according to the card industry's security standard (PCI DSS).

## In the app

Your full card number and CVV are hidden until you tap **Show details** and confirm with your fingerprint, face or passcode. They're never shown in notifications, emails or screenshots of your receipts.

## When you pay online

Most online payments need your approval in the app (3-D Secure), so card details alone aren't enough to pay.

## When you use a mobile wallet

Apple Pay, Google Pay and Samsung Pay don't share your real card number with the shop. They use a separate device number that only works on your phone.

## Our staff

Support staff can see the last four digits of your card, never the full number, CVV or PIN.

## What you can do

Don't save your card details in browsers or apps you don't trust. Use a separate virtual card for online shopping and subscriptions, so you can close it without affecting your physical card.
