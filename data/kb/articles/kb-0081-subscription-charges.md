An unexpected monthly charge is often a subscription you signed up for, perhaps a free trial that turned into a paid plan.

## Find out what it is

Tap the payment in **Activity**. We show the merchant's name and, where available, their website or logo. The name on your statement can look different from the brand, for example the name of the parent company.

## Cancel the subscription

Cancel directly with the merchant, in their app or website, or through the App Store or Google Play if you subscribed there. Cancelling with the merchant is the only way to stop future charges for good.

## Block a merchant

You can also stop a merchant from charging your card again: **Cards** > **Recurring payments** > choose the merchant > **Block**. This doesn't cancel your subscription, and the merchant may still say you owe them.

## I didn't sign up for this

If you're sure you never subscribed, freeze your card and see [Transactions I didn't make](kb-0090).
