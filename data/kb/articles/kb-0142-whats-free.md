Many everyday things cost nothing.

## Free everywhere

- Opening and keeping an account
- Verifying your identity
- Sending money to other Sahla Pay users
- Receiving money, including from abroad
- Topping up by bank transfer
- QR payments in shops
- Card payments in your own currency
- Renewing an expired card
- Freezing, unfreezing and changing your PIN
- Disputes and refunds

## Free in some countries

- Local bank transfers: free in Saudi Arabia ({{fees.local_transfer.SA}}), but {{fees.local_transfer.AE}} in the UAE and {{fees.local_transfer.EG}} in Egypt.
- Bill payments: {{fees.bill_payment.SA}} in Saudi Arabia and {{fees.bill_payment.AE}} in the UAE; {{fees.bill_payment.EG}} in Egypt.
- Virtual cards: {{fees.virtual_card.SA}} in Saudi Arabia and the UAE; {{fees.virtual_card.EG}} in Egypt.
- ATM withdrawals: the first {{fees.atm_free_withdrawals.SA}} each month are free in Saudi Arabia.
- Dormant accounts: {{fees.dormancy_monthly.SA}} in Saudi Arabia.
