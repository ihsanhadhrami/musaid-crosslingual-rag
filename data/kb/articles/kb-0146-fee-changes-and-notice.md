We sometimes change our fees and limits. When we do, we tell you in advance.

## How much notice do you give?

At least {{fees.change_notice.ALL}} before a fee goes up or a new fee is introduced. We tell you by email and in the app, and the updated fee schedule is published on the date the change is announced.

## What if I don't agree?

You can close your account for free before the change takes effect. See [Closing your account](kb-0193).

## Do you give notice when fees go down?

Not always. Fee reductions and new free services can apply immediately.

## Where can I find the current fees?

In the fee schedule for your country: [Saudi Arabia](kb-0131), [UAE](kb-0132) or [Egypt](kb-0133).

The notice period doesn't apply to changes required by law or a regulator, such as a change in the VAT rate. These apply from the date the law sets, which can be sooner.
