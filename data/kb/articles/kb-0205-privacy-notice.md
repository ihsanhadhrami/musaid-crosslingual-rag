This Privacy Notice explains how Sahla Pay collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data when you use our app and services. It applies to customers, people who apply for an account, and people who send money to or receive money from our customers. A shorter summary is available in [Privacy notice summary](kb-0192).

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

The Sahla Pay company that provides your account in your country is responsible for your personal data, as the data controller under the personal data protection law of that country. If you have an account in one country and send money to someone in another, each company is responsible for the data it holds.

## 2. The data we collect

### 2.1 Data you give us

- **Identity data:** full name, date of birth, nationality, gender, national ID, Iqama or Emirates ID number, and images of your identity document.
- **Biometric data:** the selfie and liveness video you take during verification, and the facial template we create from it to compare with your ID photo.
- **Contact data:** mobile number, email address and home address.
- **Financial and background data:** your occupation, the purpose of your account, the source of your funds, and documents you provide to support these, such as payslips or contracts.
- **Communications:** messages, chats, call recordings, complaints and feedback.

### 2.2 Data we collect when you use our services

- **Transaction data:** payments, transfers, top-ups, card payments, refunds and disputes, including the amount, date, merchant or recipient, and location of the merchant.
- **Device and usage data:** device model, operating system, app version, device identifiers, IP address, language settings, and how you use the app.
- **Location data:** your approximate location when you open the app or make a payment, if you allow it in your phone's settings.

### 2.3 Data we receive from others

- Identity verification services, including Nafath in Saudi Arabia and UAE Pass in the UAE, when you choose to use them.
- Banks, card networks and payment systems that process your payments.
- Fraud prevention databases, sanctions lists and public registers.
- People who send you money, who give us your phone number, name or IBAN.

## 3. Why we use your data

| Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Opening and running your account, making payments | Performing our contract with you |
| Verifying your identity, checking sanctions lists, preventing money laundering | Legal obligation |
| Detecting and preventing fraud, securing your account | Legal obligation and our legitimate interest |
| Handling complaints and disputes | Contract and legal obligation |
| Improving the app and our services | Our legitimate interest |
| Sending you marketing messages | Your consent, which you can withdraw |

We use automated systems to check your identity, to score the risk of a payment and to decide whether to hold a payment for review. If an automated decision has a significant effect on you, such as refusing to open an account, you can ask for it to be reviewed by a person.

## 4. Biometric data

We use your selfie and facial template only to verify your identity, to prevent fraud, and to confirm it's you when you log in on a new device or reset your password. We do not use it for marketing, and we do not share it with merchants. Facial templates are stored encrypted and separately from your other data.

Biometric data stored on your phone for fingerprint or face login, such as Face ID or Android biometrics, never leaves your phone and is never received by us. The app only receives a yes-or-no answer from your phone.

## 5. Who we share your data with

We share your data only where necessary:

- **Payment partners:** banks, card networks, payment systems and the recipients' providers, to make your payments. Recipients see your name and, for bank transfers, a reference.
- **Service providers:** identity verification, cloud hosting, customer support tools, SMS and email delivery, under contracts that protect your data.
- **Authorities:** central banks, financial intelligence units, tax authorities, courts and police, where the law requires it.
- **Fraud prevention:** other financial institutions and fraud databases, to prevent and detect fraud.
- **Your representatives:** for example, a guardian approving a minor's account in Egypt, or heirs after a customer's death, once they have proved their entitlement.

We never sell your personal data.

## 6. International transfers of data

Some of our service providers process data outside your country. Where the law of your country restricts transfers abroad, we transfer data only where it is permitted, for example with the approval of the competent authority or with appropriate contractual safeguards. Data about accounts in Saudi Arabia that the law requires to remain in the Kingdom is stored there.

## 7. How long we keep your data

We keep your data for as long as your account is open, and after it closes for {{data.retention_after_closure.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{data.retention_after_closure.AE}} in the UAE and {{data.retention_after_closure.EG}} in Egypt, as required by financial regulations. If you applied for an account but it was never opened, we keep your application data for the same period, because anti-money-laundering rules apply to applications as well.

Call recordings and chat transcripts are kept for the same period. Device and usage data that isn't linked to a transaction is deleted after a shorter period. Data connected to an ongoing dispute, investigation or legal claim is kept until the matter is closed, even if that is longer than the periods above.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on the law of your country, you have the right to:

- be informed about how we use your data, as in this notice;
- access a copy of your data;
- correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
- ask us to delete your data, where we are not required to keep it;
- withdraw your consent, for example to marketing, at any time;
- object to some uses of your data, and ask for a person to review an automated decision.

To use your rights, contact us through **Help** > **Chat with us** or **Profile** > **Privacy**. We will confirm your identity first. We will answer within the time allowed by the law of your country.

Some requests can't be met in full. We can't delete identity documents, transaction records or verification data before the end of the retention period, because the law requires us to keep them, and we can't give you data about another person, such as the full details of someone who sent you money.

## 9. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit and at rest, strict access controls, monitoring and regular security testing. Our staff can access your data only when their role requires it, and every access is logged. Support staff can see the last four digits of your card, never the full number, PIN or security code.

## 10. Marketing

We send you marketing messages only if you have agreed to them. You can turn them off at any time in **Profile** > **Notifications**. Service messages, security alerts and notices we are legally required to send are not marketing, and can't be turned off.

## 11. Children

Accounts are not available to children under the minimum age in each country. In Egypt, where customers aged {{kyc.min_age.EG}} to under {{kyc.guardian_consent_under.EG}} may hold an account with guardian approval, the guardian can see the account's transactions and balance, but not the young person's chats with our support team.

## 12. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice. If the changes are significant, we will tell you in the app at least {{legal.terms_notice.ALL}} before they apply.

## 13. Contact and complaints

You can contact our data protection officer through **Profile** > **Privacy** > **Contact us**. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to us, and you may also complain to the data protection authority in your country.
