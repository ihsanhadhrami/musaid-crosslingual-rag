## When do daily limits reset?

At midnight in the country your account is registered in: Riyadh time for Saudi Arabia, UAE time for the UAE, and Cairo time for Egypt. It doesn't matter where you are when you make the payment.

## When do monthly limits reset?

At midnight at the start of the 1st day of each calendar month. It's a calendar month, not a rolling month counted from your first payment.

## Which limits reset this way?

All of them: transfer limits, card spending, ATM withdrawals, QR payments and, for unverified accounts, the monthly receiving limit. Balance limits don't reset, because they're about how much you hold, not how much you move.

## Does a cancelled transfer give me my limit back?

Yes, as soon as it's cancelled or fails.

A refund doesn't. If you pay for something and get a refund later, the original payment still counts towards that day's and month's limit.
