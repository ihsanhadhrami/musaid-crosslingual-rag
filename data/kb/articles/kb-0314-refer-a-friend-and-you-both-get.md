Invite friends to Sahla Pay. When they join and start using it, you both get a bonus.

## The reward

{{promo.referral_bonus.SA}} each in Saudi Arabia, {{promo.referral_bonus.AE}} each in the UAE, or {{promo.referral_bonus.EG}} each in Egypt.

## How it works

1. Go to **Profile** > **Invite friends** and share your link.
2. Your friend opens an account using your link, and verifies their identity.
3. Within {{promo.referral_window.ALL}} of opening the account, your friend makes card payments or transfers adding up to at least {{promo.referral_min_activity.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{promo.referral_min_activity.AE}} in the UAE or {{promo.referral_min_activity.EG}} in Egypt.

You both get the bonus within a few days of your friend reaching the amount.

## Terms

- You can get a bonus for up to {{promo.referral_max.ALL}} friends each calendar year.
- Your friend must open their account in the same country as yours. A friend in another country can still join, but neither of you gets the bonus.
- Your friend must be new to Sahla Pay. People who already had an account, even a closed one, don't count.
- Transfers between you and the friend you invited don't count towards their amount.
- If we find that accounts were opened only to collect bonuses, we take the bonuses back and may close the accounts.
