You can send money from your Sahla Pay balance to a bank account in Egypt, if your account is registered in Saudi Arabia or the UAE and is verified. The recipient receives Egyptian pounds (EGP).

## What your recipient needs to give you

- the recipient's full name in English, as on their bank account
- their IBAN, which starts with EG and has 29 characters

Ask the recipient to send you these details in writing, for example by copying them from their banking app, rather than reading them out. Most returned transfers are caused by a wrong digit or a name that doesn't match.

## How to send

To send money to Egypt:

1. Tap **Send** > **International** and choose Egypt.
2. Enter the amount, in your currency or in Egyptian pounds (EGP).
3. Enter the recipient's details, or choose a saved recipient.
4. Check the exchange rate, the fee and the amount the recipient will get.
5. Tap **Confirm** and enter the one-time password we send you.

The exchange rate is fixed when you confirm.

## Things to know about this destination

Transfers to Egypt are paid out in Egyptian pounds only.

If the recipient has a Sahla Pay account registered in Egypt, you can send to the IBAN shown in their app under **Add money** > **Bank transfer** > **From abroad**. It's treated as an international transfer, with the same fees, because the two accounts are in different countries.

Banks in Egypt don't process incoming transfers on Fridays and Saturdays, but transfers to Sahla Pay accounts in Egypt are credited every day.

## If something goes wrong

If the receiving bank rejects the transfer, the full amount, including our fee, comes back to your balance in your own currency, at the exchange rate on the day it's returned. If you sent it to the wrong person, you can ask us to recall it, but a recall fee applies and the receiving bank decides whether to return the money. See [Disputes for international transfers](kb-0095).
