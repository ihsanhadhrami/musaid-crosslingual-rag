If you sent money to the wrong Sahla Pay user or bank account and can't get it back directly, you can ask us to investigate.

## Deadlines

You must open the dispute within {{dispute.filing_window.SA}} of the transfer in Saudi Arabia, {{dispute.filing_window.AE}} in the UAE, or {{dispute.filing_window.EG}} in Egypt. We can't accept wrong-transfer disputes after that.

## Open a dispute

To open a dispute:

1. Go to **Activity** and tap the transfer.
2. Tap **Report a problem** > **I sent it to the wrong person**.
3. Tell us who you meant to send it to, and anything else that helps.
4. Tap **Submit**. You'll get a case number by email and in the app.

## What happens next

We contact the recipient and, for bank transfers, the receiving bank. Investigations take up to {{dispute.investigation.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{dispute.investigation.AE}} in the UAE and {{dispute.investigation.EG}} in Egypt. You can follow progress under **Help** > **My cases**.

## If the dispute is approved

| Country | Money back in your balance within |
|---|---|
| Saudi Arabia | {{refund.wrong_transfer.SA}} |
| UAE | {{refund.wrong_transfer.AE}} |
| Egypt | {{refund.wrong_transfer.EG}} |

We can only return money that's still in the recipient's account. If they've already spent or withdrawn some of it, we return what's there and close the case. For the rest, you'll need to go to the police or the courts, and we'll provide the transaction records they ask for.

Disputes are only for mistakes. If someone tricked you into sending money, report it as fraud instead. See [Reporting fraud](kb-0156).
