If you transferred money to the wrong Sahla Pay user or bank account and cannot recover it yourself, you can submit a dispute for investigation.

## Filing deadline

The dispute must be opened within {{dispute.filing_window.SA}} of the transfer in Saudi Arabia, {{dispute.filing_window.AE}} in the UAE, or {{dispute.filing_window.EG}} in Egypt. Requests submitted after the applicable deadline cannot be accepted.

## Submit a dispute

1. Open **Activity** and select the transfer.
2. Choose **Report a problem** > **I sent it to the wrong person**.
3. Explain who should have received the money and provide any useful details.
4. Select **Submit**. Your case number is sent by email and displayed in the app.

## Investigation process

We contact the recipient and, where relevant, the receiving bank. Investigations can take up to {{dispute.investigation.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{dispute.investigation.AE}} in the UAE, and {{dispute.investigation.EG}} in Egypt. Track the case in **Help** > **My cases**.

## If your dispute is approved

| Country | Amount returned within |
|---|---|
| Saudi Arabia | {{refund.wrong_transfer.SA}} |
| UAE | {{refund.wrong_transfer.AE}} |
| Egypt | {{refund.wrong_transfer.EG}} |

We can recover only the funds that remain in the recipient's account. If the recipient has already spent or withdrawn part of the transfer, we return whatever remains and close the case. To pursue any unrecovered amount, you must contact the police or courts; we can provide transaction records when requested.

Use this process only for genuine transfer mistakes. If you were deceived into sending the money, report the incident as fraud instead. See [Reporting fraud](kb-0156).
