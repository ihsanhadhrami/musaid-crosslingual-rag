Your Emirates ID needs to be valid for your account to stay fully active. When you renew it, upload the new card.

## Before you start

Have the renewed Emirates ID card with you. A renewal receipt isn't enough.

## Upload your renewed Emirates ID

To update your Emirates ID:

1. Open **Profile** > **Personal details** > **ID document**.
2. Tap **Update ID**.
3. Scan both sides of the new card, or sign in with UAE Pass to update automatically.
4. Tap **Submit**.

## If you don't update it

You have {{kyc.expired_id_grace.AE}} from the expiry date printed on the card. After that, sending money and paying with your card are paused until the new ID is approved. Your balance stays safe and you can still receive money.

Waiting for your new card to be printed? The grace period is the same either way, so if your renewal is taking a long time, contact us before it runs out and we'll note it on your account.
