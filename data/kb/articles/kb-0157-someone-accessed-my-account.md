Signs include a login alert you don't recognize, a changed email or phone number, or transactions you didn't make.

## Secure your account now

To lock someone out:

1. Change your password: **Profile** > **Security** > **Change password**.
2. Check **Profile** > **Security** > **Trusted devices** and remove any device you don't recognize.
3. Freeze your card.
4. Check your email address and phone number under **Personal details**.

## If you can't log in

The other person may have changed your password or phone number. Call our fraud line, open {{support.fraud_line_hours.ALL}}. We'll lock the account and help you get back in after checking your identity.

## Check your transactions

Go through **Activity** and report anything you didn't make. See [Transactions I didn't make](kb-0090).

## How did it happen?

Usually through a shared code, a fake login page, or someone who knows your password and had access to your phone. See [Recognizing phishing and scam messages](kb-0155).
