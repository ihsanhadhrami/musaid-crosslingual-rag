We're upgrading our systems on **Friday 31 January 2025, from 1:00 AM to 3:00 AM Riyadh time** (2:00 AM to 4:00 AM UAE time, 12:00 AM to 2:00 AM Cairo time).

## During the maintenance

- You won't be able to log in to the app or send transfers.
- Your card keeps working for payments in shops, online and at ATMs.
- Scheduled transfers due during the maintenance are sent when it ends.

The fraud line stays open. If your card is lost during the maintenance, call us to block it.
