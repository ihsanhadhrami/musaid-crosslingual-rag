If you paid in a foreign currency, the merchant refunds you in that same currency, and we convert it back.

## Why didn't I get back exactly what I paid?

Because the refund is converted on the day it arrives, not the day you paid. If the exchange rate moved in between, you may get a little more or a little less.

The markup in our rate also applies again when the refund is converted: {{fees.fx_markup.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{fees.fx_markup.AE}} in the UAE and {{fees.fx_markup.EG}} in Egypt. So even if the rate didn't move at all, a foreign-currency refund comes back slightly lower than the original payment.

## Can you refund me the difference?

No. Exchange rate differences on refunds aren't something we can refund, because we don't control the rate or when the merchant sends the refund.

## The merchant refunded in my own currency

Then no conversion is needed, and you get exactly the amount they sent.
