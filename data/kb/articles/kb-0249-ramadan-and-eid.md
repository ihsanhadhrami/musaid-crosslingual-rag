Ramadan and Eid are the busiest weeks of the year for transfers. Here's how to send Eidiya, plan around the holidays, and avoid the most common problems.

## Sending Eidiya

The quickest way to send Eidiya to family and friends who use Sahla Pay is a normal transfer to their phone number. It's free and arrives instantly, even on the day of Eid.

To send to several people at once:

1. Tap **Send** > **To a Sahla Pay user**.
2. Tap **Add more people** and choose the contacts you want to send to.
3. Enter the amount for each person. You can send everyone the same amount, or different amounts.
4. Add a note, for example "Eid Mubarak", and tap **Send**.

If someone doesn't have Sahla Pay yet, they'll get an SMS to join. If they don't claim the money within {{transfer.unclaimed_return.ALL}}, it comes back to you.

## Sending Eidiya abroad

From Saudi Arabia and the UAE, you can send money to family abroad by international transfer. Banks in many countries are closed for several days over Eid, so send it a few days early if you want it to arrive in time. See the guide for your destination under **Transfers** > **International** > **Destinations**, for example [Sending money to Pakistan](kb-0218).

## Holidays and transfer times

Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha are public holidays in Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Egypt, and the exact dates depend on the moon. During the holidays:

- transfers between Sahla Pay users, QR payments and card payments work as normal;
- in Saudi Arabia and Egypt, local bank transfers arrive as quickly as on any other day;
- local bank transfers in the UAE, and international transfers, wait for the next business day;
- bill payments are posted to the company after the holiday.

## Support during Ramadan

In Saudi Arabia and the UAE, chat and phone support hours don't change during Ramadan. In Egypt, support hours may change during Ramadan; we announce any changes in the app. The fraud line is open {{support.fraud_line_hours.ALL}} in all three countries, whatever the date.

## Watch out for Eid scams

Fraudsters send fake "Eid gift" and "Ramadan prize" messages with links. We never send prizes that ask you to log in or enter a code. See [Recognizing phishing and scam messages](kb-0155).

Scheduled transfers that fall on Eid are sent as usual at {{transfer.scheduled_run_time.ALL}}, but if they go to a bank account in the UAE, the receiving bank only credits them after the holiday. Monthly rent or family transfers scheduled for the first of the month may arrive several days late if the first falls on Eid.
