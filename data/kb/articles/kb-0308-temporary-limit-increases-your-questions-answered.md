Since 1 March 2026, verified customers in Saudi Arabia and the UAE can ask for a temporary increase to their daily transfer limit. Here are the questions we've had most.

## How much can I get?

Up to {{limits.temp_increase_daily.SA}} a day in Saudi Arabia and {{limits.temp_increase_daily.AE}} a day in the UAE, for {{limits.temp_increase_duration.ALL}}.

## How quickly is it approved?

We review requests within {{limits.temp_increase_review.ALL}}. Send it a few days before your payment is due.

## Does my monthly limit go up too?

No. Only the daily limit changes. A large payment can use up most of your monthly limit.

## Can I use it in Egypt?

No. It's {{limits.temp_increase_available.EG}} for accounts registered in Egypt.

To apply, see [Requesting a temporary limit increase](kb-0150).
