**Update, 20 August 2025:** Resolved. You can download statements again.

For part of 19 August 2025, downloading statements and tax invoices from **Profile** > **Statements** showed an error. Your transaction history in **Activity** wasn't affected.

If you requested a statement by email during this time and it didn't arrive, request it again. See [Downloading a statement](kb-0181).
