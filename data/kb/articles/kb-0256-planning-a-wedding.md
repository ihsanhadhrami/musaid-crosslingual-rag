Weddings involve many payments to many people: halls, caterers, photographers, dresses and gifts. Sahla Pay can help you keep track of them.

## Set money aside in Pockets

Create a **Wedding** pocket and move money into it each month. Money in a pocket isn't spent by card payments or transfers until you move it back, so it's protected from everyday spending. You can create up to {{account.max_pockets.ALL}} pockets, for example one for the hall and one for the honeymoon. See [Budgeting with Pockets](kb-0259).

## Paying suppliers

- **Deposits:** pay by card where you can. If a supplier closes or doesn't deliver, card payments can be disputed; see [Goods or services not received](kb-0099).
- **Bank transfers:** ask each supplier for an invoice with their company name and IBAN.
- **Sharing costs with family:** use payment requests, so everyone can pay their share with one tap; see [Requesting money from friends](kb-0045).

## Large payments and limits

A hall or catering bill can be more than your daily limit. In Saudi Arabia and the UAE, you can request a temporary increase with the supplier's invoice; see [Requesting a temporary limit increase](kb-0150). In Egypt, plan to pay large suppliers over several days.

## Wedding gifts

Gifts sent to you through Sahla Pay arrive instantly. If you receive a lot of money at once, it counts towards your balance limit, and very large amounts may lead to a source of funds question, which you can answer with a short explanation.

## Honeymoon abroad

Check the travel guide for your destination, and take your card details and the fraud line number with you. See [Summer travel checklist](kb-0257).
