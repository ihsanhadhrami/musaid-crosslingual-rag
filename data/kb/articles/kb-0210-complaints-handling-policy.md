We want to know when we get things wrong. This policy explains how to make a complaint, how we handle it, and what you can do if you're not satisfied with our answer.

## 1. What counts as a complaint

A complaint is any expression of dissatisfaction about our products, services, staff or decisions, where you expect a response. You don't need to use the word "complaint". If you raise a problem with our support team and say you're unhappy with how it was handled, we will treat it as a complaint.

A complaint is different from a request to dispute a transaction. If you want money back for a specific payment, you should open a dispute first, within the dispute deadline. Making a complaint does not stop the dispute deadline from running.

## 2. How to complain

You can complain:

- in the app, through **Help** > **Make a complaint**;
- by phone, by asking the agent to register a formal complaint;
- in writing, to the address shown in the app under **Profile** > **Legal**.

Complaints can be made in Arabic or English. Someone else may complain on your behalf, such as a family member or lawyer, if you give us your consent in the app or in writing.

## 3. What we need from you

Please tell us what happened, when, which transactions are involved, and what you would like us to do. Include any screenshots, receipts or messages that support your complaint.

## 4. How we handle complaints

### 4.1 Acknowledgement

We will acknowledge your complaint within {{complaint.acknowledge.ALL}} and give you a reference number.

### 4.2 Investigation

Your complaint is handled by our complaints team, which is separate from the team that made the original decision. We may contact you for more information. If we need more time than expected, we will tell you why and when to expect our answer.

### 4.3 Final response

Our final response will explain our decision, any remedy we offer, and how you can take the matter further.

## 5. Remedies

Where we find that we got something wrong, we will put it right. Depending on the case, this may include correcting your account, refunding fees or charges, paying interest or compensation for losses you suffered as a result of our error, or apologizing and explaining what we will change.

## 6. If you're not satisfied

If you disagree with our final response, or if we haven't responded within the time above, you may refer your complaint to the consumer protection department of the central bank in your country. You can do this free of charge. Our final response includes the contact details. The central bank may expect you to have complained to us first.

## 7. How we use complaints

We record and analyse complaints to find and fix the causes of recurring problems. Complaint records are kept for the same period as other account records.

## 8. Fair treatment

Making a complaint will never affect how we treat you or your account. Complaints that are abusive or threatening towards our staff may be handled in writing only.
