You can follow every dispute you've opened in the app.

## Check your case

To see where your dispute is:

1. Go to **Help** > **My cases**.
2. Tap the case. You'll see its status, the timeline so far, and any messages from our team.

## What the statuses mean

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Submitted | We've received it and it's waiting for review. |
| Need info | We've asked you something. Reply from the case. |
| Investigating | We're looking into it, or waiting for the other side. |
| Chargeback raised | We've sent it to the merchant's bank. |
| Resolved | A decision has been made. The outcome is in the case. |
| Closed | Finished. You can no longer add to it. |

## Adding information later

While a case is open, tap **Add information** to send more details or files. Adding information doesn't restart the investigation time.
