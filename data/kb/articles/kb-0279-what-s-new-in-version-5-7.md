## New

- **Recurring payments in one place.** Go to **Cards** > **Recurring payments** to see every merchant that charges your card on a repeating basis, and block the ones you no longer want. See [Recurring card payments](kb-0127).

Blocking a merchant stops future charges on your card, but it doesn't cancel your subscription. Cancel with the merchant too, or they may ask you to pay another way.

## Fixes

- The home screen sometimes showed your main balance including Pockets. It now shows your main balance only, as intended.
