Build a savings habit this autumn. Keep money aside in a pocket and get a reward.

## The challenge

Between 1 September and 30 November 2026, keep at least {{promo.pocket_challenge_min.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{promo.pocket_challenge_min.AE}} in the UAE or {{promo.pocket_challenge_min.EG}} in Egypt in one pocket for {{promo.pocket_challenge_period.ALL}} in a row. We'll add {{promo.pocket_challenge_reward.SA}}, {{promo.pocket_challenge_reward.AE}} or {{promo.pocket_challenge_reward.EG}} to that pocket.

## How to join

Tap **Pockets** > **Savings challenge** > **Join**, and choose the pocket. You can join until 7 September 2026.

## Terms

- If the pocket's balance drops below the minimum, even for a moment, the count starts again from that day.
- The reward is a one-off promotional payment. Pockets don't earn interest or profit.
- Money in pockets counts towards your balance limit, including the reward. If the reward would take you over your limit, we pay it once you have room.
- One reward per customer.

See [Budgeting with Pockets](kb-0259).
