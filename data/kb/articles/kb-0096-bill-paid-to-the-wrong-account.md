Typed the wrong subscriber number, or picked the wrong company? Here's what you can do.

## The payment hasn't been posted yet

Bills are posted to the company within {{bill.posting_time.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{bill.posting_time.AE}} in the UAE and {{bill.posting_time.EG}} in Egypt. Before that, the payment shows **Processing** in **Activity**. Contact us straight away through **Help** > **Chat with us**, and we'll try to stop it. We can't promise this, because many billers accept payments instantly.

## The payment has been posted

Only the company you paid can move or refund the money. Contact them with the payment receipt from **Activity** and ask them to:

- move the payment to your correct account, or
- refund it to you.

## The account number belongs to someone else

The company usually needs that customer's permission to refund, or will move the credit if you can show the payment was a mistake. We can give you an official confirmation of the payment to help.

Mobile recharges to a wrong number can't be reversed; see [Mobile recharge](kb-0050).
