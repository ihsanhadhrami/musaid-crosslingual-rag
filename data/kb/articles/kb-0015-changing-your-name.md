The name on your Sahla Pay account has to match your ID. You can't edit it freely in the app, but you can update it if your legal name has changed or if there's a spelling mistake.

## Fix a spelling mistake

If the app shows your name differently from your ID, for example a missing middle name or a different English spelling, go to **Profile** > **Personal details** > **Name** > **Report a problem** and scan your ID. We'll correct it to match.

## Your legal name has changed

Update your ID first, then follow the same steps and upload the new ID. If you have a physical card, you can order a new one with the updated name; the replacement fee applies.

## How long does it take?

Name changes are always checked by a person. They take up to {{kyc.verification_time.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{kyc.verification_time.AE}} in the UAE and {{kyc.verification_time.EG}} in Egypt.

Transfers you receive while the change is being reviewed still arrive normally.
