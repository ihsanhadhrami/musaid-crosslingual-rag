If there's a problem with a card payment that you and the merchant can't sort out, you can dispute it.

## What can I dispute?

- You were charged twice, or the wrong amount.
- You paid but never got the goods or service.
- You cancelled and the merchant didn't refund you.
- You don't recognize the payment. In that case, freeze your card first and see [Transactions I didn't make](kb-0090).

## Deadline

Open the dispute within {{dispute.filing_window.SA}} of the transaction in Saudi Arabia, {{dispute.filing_window.AE}} in the UAE or {{dispute.filing_window.EG}} in Egypt.

## Open a dispute

To dispute a payment:

1. Tap the payment in **Activity**.
2. Tap **Report a problem** and choose the reason.
3. Answer the questions and add evidence. See [Evidence to include in a dispute](kb-0087).
4. Tap **Submit**.

## What happens next

We review it and, if needed, raise a chargeback with the merchant's bank. See [What happens after I open a dispute](kb-0078).

If your dispute is still open {{dispute.temporary_credit_after.ALL}} after you submit it, we usually put the amount back in your balance as a temporary credit while the case continues. If the dispute is decided in the merchant's favour, the temporary credit is taken back, even if that leaves your balance lower than you expected.
