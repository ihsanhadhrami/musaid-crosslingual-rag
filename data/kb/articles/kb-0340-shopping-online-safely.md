Online shopping is convenient, and most of the time it's safe. These habits protect you on the rare occasions it isn't.

## Use your virtual card

Your virtual card works anywhere online. If a website you've used is ever hacked, you can freeze or replace the virtual card without affecting your physical card. Virtual cards are free to replace. See [Virtual cards](kb-0110).

## Check the website

- Type the address yourself, or use the shop's official app, rather than following links in messages.
- Be suspicious of prices far below those of other shops, especially for phones, electronics and designer goods.
- Look for a real address and contact details, and read reviews on other websites.

## Approve only what you're buying

For most online payments, we ask you to approve the payment in the app. Check the merchant name and the amount before you approve. If the amount is different from what you expected, decline it. See [Online payments and 3-D Secure](kb-0118).

## Pay by card, not by transfer

Card payments can be disputed if the goods never arrive or aren't as described. A bank transfer to a seller usually can't be recovered if the seller turns out to be a fraud. Be very careful when a seller insists on a transfer.

## Social media sellers

Many genuine small sellers advertise on Instagram and WhatsApp. If you buy from one, ask for a payment link rather than sending a transfer, and check that the seller has a history of real customers.

## If something goes wrong

If your order never arrives, contact the seller first. If they don't help, see [Goods or services not received](kb-0099). If you think a website stole your card details, freeze the card and see [Transactions I didn't make](kb-0090).
