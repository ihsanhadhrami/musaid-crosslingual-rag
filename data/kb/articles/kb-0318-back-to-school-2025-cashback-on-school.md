**This offer ended on 30 September 2025.**

## The offer

From 25 August to 30 September 2025, we gave {{promo.school2025_pct.ALL}} cashback on school and university fees, up to {{promo.school2025_cap.SA}} in Saudi Arabia, {{promo.school2025_cap.AE}} in the UAE or {{promo.school2025_cap.EG}} in Egypt.

## What counted

- Card payments to schools, universities and nurseries.
- Bill payments under **Payments** > **Bills** > **Education**.

## What didn't count

- Bank transfers to a school's account, even with the student's name as the reference. We can't tell that these are school fees.
- Payments for uniforms, books and transport, unless the school charged them on the same invoice as fees.
- Instalments paid after 30 September 2025, even if the first instalment was paid during the offer.

See [Paying school and university fees](kb-0251).
