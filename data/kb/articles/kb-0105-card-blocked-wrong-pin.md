If you enter the wrong PIN {{card.pin_attempts.ALL}} times in a row, your card's PIN is blocked. This protects you if someone else has your card.

## What still works

Online payments and payments through Apple Pay or Google Pay keep working, because they don't use your PIN. Contactless payments below the no-PIN limit may also work.

## Unblock your PIN

To unblock it:

1. Go to **Cards** > **Card settings** > **Unblock PIN**.
2. Confirm it's you.
3. Check your PIN with **Show PIN**, or set a new one.

That's all. You don't need a new card.

## I unblocked it, but the ATM still says the PIN is blocked

Some ATMs remember the blocked status until you make a chip-and-PIN purchase. Make one small chip-and-PIN payment in a shop with your correct PIN, then try the ATM again.

If the ATM kept your card after the wrong PIN attempts, the card can't be returned to you. Freeze it in the app and order a replacement; the lost-card fee doesn't apply when an ATM keeps your card.
