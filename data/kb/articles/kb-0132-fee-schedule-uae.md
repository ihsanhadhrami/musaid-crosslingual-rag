All fees for personal accounts registered in the UAE, effective 1 March 2026. VAT at {{tax.vat.AE}} is added where marked.

## Transfers

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| To another Sahla Pay user | free |
| To a local bank account | {{fees.local_transfer.AE}} + VAT |
| International transfer | {{fees.intl_transfer_pct.AE}}, minimum {{fees.intl_transfer_min.AE}} + VAT |
| Recalling an international transfer | {{fees.intl_recall.AE}} |
| Exchange rate markup | {{fees.fx_markup.AE}} (included in the rate) |

## Adding money and payments

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| Top-up by debit card | {{fees.card_topup.AE}} + VAT |
| Top-up by bank transfer | free |
| Bill payments and mobile recharge | {{fees.bill_payment.AE}} |
| Payment links (charged to the seller) | {{fees.payment_link.AE}} |
