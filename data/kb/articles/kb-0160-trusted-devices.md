A trusted device is a phone or tablet where you've logged in and confirmed it's you. You can have up to {{security.trusted_devices_max.ALL}} trusted devices at a time.

## How does a device become trusted?

The first time you log in on a device, you enter your password, a code we send by SMS, and take a selfie. After that, the device is trusted and you just log in normally.

## Where can I see my devices?

**Profile** > **Security** > **Trusted devices**. You'll see each device's name, when it was added and when it was last used.

## Remove a device

Tap the device and choose **Remove**. It's logged out straight away. Do this if you sell or lose a phone.

## I've reached the maximum

Remove an old device before adding a new one. The app will ask you to choose one when you log in on a new device.

For your protection, for {{security.new_device_hold.ALL}} after you add a new trusted device, you can't send money to new recipients or send money abroad from it. Payments to people you've paid before work normally. See [Using Sahla Pay on a new phone](kb-0178).
