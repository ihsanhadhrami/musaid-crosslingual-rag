Getting a refund or opening a dispute is free. There are a few situations where fees you've already paid aren't returned.

## Refunds from merchants

Free. You get back whatever the merchant refunds. For refunds in a foreign currency, the exchange rate on the refund day applies; see [Refunds in a different currency](kb-0091).

## Disputes and chargebacks

Free, whatever the outcome.

## Failed or returned transfers

If a transfer fails or is returned by the recipient's bank, the transfer fee is refunded too.

## Fees that aren't refunded

- Card top-up fees, if you later move the money out again.
- ATM fees abroad charged by the ATM's owner.

Recalling an international transfer has its own fee: {{fees.intl_recall.SA}} in Saudi Arabia and {{fees.intl_recall.AE}} in the UAE. It's charged when you ask for the recall, and isn't refunded even if the recall fails. See [Disputes for international transfers](kb-0095).
