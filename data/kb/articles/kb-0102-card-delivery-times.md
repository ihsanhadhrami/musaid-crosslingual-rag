After you order a physical card, we print it and send it by courier to the address you chose.

## How long does delivery take?

| Country | Delivery within |
|---|---|
| Saudi Arabia | {{card.delivery.SA}} |
| UAE | {{card.delivery.AE}} |
| Egypt | {{card.delivery.EG}} |

The time starts the day after you order. Replacement cards take the same time.

## Can I track my card?

Yes. Go to **Cards** > **Track delivery**. Once the courier has it, you'll also get an SMS from them with a tracking link, and they'll call before delivering.

## Do I need to be home?

Someone has to sign for the card, and it can only be handed to you in person, with your ID. The courier tries twice. After two failed attempts, the card goes back to us and you'll need to arrange a new delivery in the app.

## Can I use my card before it arrives?

Yes. Your virtual card works straight away for online payments and in Apple Pay or Google Pay, where available.
